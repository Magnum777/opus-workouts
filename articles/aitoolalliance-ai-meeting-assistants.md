## What Is an AI Meeting Assistant?

An **AI meeting assistant** is software that joins your calls—Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack huddles, or in-person conversations—and uses speech recognition and large language models to:

- **Transcribe** conversations in real time with speaker labels
- **Summarize** key points, decisions, and outcomes automatically
- **Extract action items** and assign owners
- **Integrate** with calendars, CRMs, project management tools, and notetaking apps
- **Search** past meetings to surface context instantly

The best tools do not just dump a wall of text on you. They deliver structured, searchable outputs you can act on without re-reading an hour-long transcript.

## The 7 Best AI Meeting Assistants Compared

### 1. Otter.ai

Otter remains the ** accessibility and collaboration** leader. It offers real-time transcription with speaker identification, shared highlight snippets, and tight integration with Google and Microsoft calendars.

**Best for:** Teams that need live collaboration during meetings—multiple people can highlight, comment, and add notes simultaneously.

- Live transcription with speaker ID
- AI-generated summaries and takeaways
- Slack, Salesforce, and HubSpot integrations
- Generous free tier (300 minutes/month)

### 2. Fireflies.ai

Fireflies is the **automation powerhouse**. It joins meetings automatically, transcribes everything, and pushes summaries into your CRM or task manager without you lifting a finger.

**Best for:** Sales and customer-success teams who need meeting intelligence piped directly into HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive.

- Automatic meeting capture across all major platforms
- Conversation intelligence and topic tracking
- Custom topic alerts and coaching insights
- Deep workflow automation via Zapier and native integrations

### 3. Notion AI Meeting Notes

Notion's AI Meeting Notes (currently in beta) turns raw transcripts into **structured wiki pages** inside your workspace. Because it lives in Notion, the output is immediately connected to your existing docs, projects, and databases.

**Best for:** Companies already running their operations in Notion who want meeting outputs to live where work happens.

- Transcribes and structures notes inside Notion
- Links meeting notes to relevant project pages automatically
- Action items become Notion tasks instantly
- No context-switching between apps

### 4. tl;dv

tl;dv specializes in **async meeting culture**. It timestamps key moments, creates highlight reels, and makes it easy to share specific clips with teammates who could not attend.

**Best for:** Remote and hybrid teams across time zones who need searchable video libraries, not just text transcripts.

- Timestamped highlights and clip sharing
- Multi-language transcription (30+ languages)
- CRM integration for sales workflows
- Strong free plan for small teams

### 5. Fathom

Fathom focuses on **simplicity and speed**. It records, transcribes, and delivers a clean summary within seconds of a call ending. No bloat, no steep learning curve.

**Best for:** Individual contributors and small teams who want a reliable notetaker without configuration overhead.

- One-click summary generation
- Automatic CRM sync
- Highlight key moments during the call
- Completely free for individuals

### 6. Fellow

Fellow treats meetings as **structured workflows**, not just conversations. It combines agendas, notes, action items, and feedback into one collaborative space.

**Best for:** Managers and teams who want to improve meeting quality, not just document them.

- Collaborative agendas before the meeting
- Real-time collaborative notes during the meeting
- Action items with owner assignments
- Meeting feedback and analytics

### 7. Zoom AI Companion / Microsoft 365 Copilot

If your organization already lives inside **Zoom Workplace** or **Microsoft 365**, the built-in AI assistants are increasingly competitive. Zoom's AI Companion now offers real-time note-taking, summary generation, and even draft email follow-ups. Microsoft 365 Copilot does the same inside Teams, with deep hooks into Outlook, Word, and OneNote.

**Best for:** Enterprises that prioritize security, compliance, and keeping data within their existing vendor ecosystem.

- Native integration—no third-party apps required
- Enterprise-grade security and data governance
- Real-time and post-meeting summaries
- Draft follow-up emails and documents automatically

## How to Choose the Right AI Meeting Assistant

Not every tool fits every team. Use this checklist:

- **Where do your meetings happen?** Zoom-native tools struggle with Google Meet unless they explicitly support it.
- **What do you do with the output?** If it needs to land in Salesforce, prioritize Fireflies or Fathom. If it needs to become a wiki page, choose Notion AI.
- **How big is your team?** Some tools charge per seat; others charge per meeting minute. Match pricing to your usage pattern.
- **Is async viewing important?** If teammates replay meetings, tl;dv's video highlights win.
- **Do you need pre-meeting agendas?** Fellow is the only tool that treats the full meeting lifecycle, not just the conversation.
