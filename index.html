⚠️ Knee-Safe Protocol: No squats, no lunges, no box jumps. All leg work is machine-based or lying down. Start with weights you can control for 12 reps. Focus on slow controlled movement, not heavy weight. If your knees bark, drop weight or skip the exercise.
Monday — Leg Day Knee-Safe
45 min | Machines + Glutes | No squats, no lunges
ExerciseSets x RepsNote
Leg Press (Machine)
Feet HIGH on plate, shoulder width. Drives through glutes/hams, less knee stress.
 3 x 12 Start light. Control the negative.
Hip Thrust / Glute Bridge
Back on bench, barbell or dumbbell on hips. Squeeze glutes hard at top.
 3 x 12 Knee-friendly leg builder.
Leg Curl (Machine)
Lying or seated. Isolates hamstrings, zero knee load.
 3 x 12 Don't swing the weight.
Leg Extension (Machine)
Light weight only. Builds quad strength to support knee. Stop if pain.
 2 x 15 Very light. Stop if knee hurts.
Calf Raises (Machine or Seated)
Slow tempo. Full stretch at bottom, squeeze at top.
 3 x 15 Seated takes pressure off knee.
Seated Abductor / Adductor
Inner/outer thigh machine. Good for hip stability.
 2 x 15 each Light, controlled.
Tuesday — Push Day Chest/Shoulders/Triceps
45 min | All machine or dumbbell based
ExerciseSets x RepsNote
Chest Press (Machine)
Seated machine press. Safer than bench at your weight. Full range of motion.
 3 x 10-12 Keep shoulder blades pinned.
Incline Chest Press (Machine or Smith)
Hits upper chest. Low incline angle (15-30 degrees).
 3 x 10-12 Slow and controlled.
Shoulder Press (Machine)
Seated overhead press machine. Better stability than dumbbells.
 3 x 10-12 Don't let elbows flare.
Lateral Raise (Machine or Cable)
Builds shoulder width. Light weight, focus on squeeze at top.
 3 x 12-15 Lead with elbows, not hands.
Tricep Pushdown (Cable)
Rope or straight bar. Keep elbows at sides.
 3 x 12-15 Full extension at bottom.
Tricep Extension (Machine or Overhead)
Overhead or cable extension for long head.
 2 x 12 Keep elbows in.
Wednesday — Active Recovery / Core Light Day
~45 min | Core + Mobility + Light Cardio | Easy on knees
ExerciseSets x Reps/TimeNote
Plank
On forearms. Keep body straight. Start with what you can hold.
 3 x 20-45 sec Build up time each week.
Dead Bug
Lying on back. Opposite arm/leg extend. Keep lower back flat.
 3 x 10 each Slow and controlled.
Bird Dog
On hands and knees. Opposite arm/leg extend. Balance + core.
 3 x 10 each Hold each rep 2 seconds.
Side Plank
On forearm. Stack hips. Modified version: knees on ground.
 2 x 20-30 sec each Knee modification is fine.
Seated Torso Twist
Seated on floor or bench. Twist with light medicine ball or bodyweight.
 3 x 15 each Control the twist.
Glute Bridge (Bodyweight)
Lying on back. Squeeze glutes at top. No weight today.
 3 x 20 Hold 2 seconds at top.
Foam Roll / Stretch
Quads, hamstrings, hips, lower back. 5-10 min total.
 5-10 min Recovery is the point today.
Thursday — Pull Day Back/Biceps
45 min | Build that back, protect posture
ExerciseSets x RepsNote
Lat Pulldown (Machine)
Wide grip. Pull to upper chest. Squeeze shoulder blades together.
 3 x 10-12 Don't use momentum.
Seated Row (Machine)
Chest supported if available. Pull to lower ribs. Squeeze.
 3 x 10-12 Keep chest against pad.
Straight Arm Pulldown (Cable)
Arms straight, pull bar down to thighs. Isolates lats.
 3 x 12-15 Light weight, feel the lat stretch.
Face Pulls (Cable)
Rope attachment. Pull to face height, external rotation at end.
 3 x 15 Great for shoulder health.
Bicep Curl (Machine or Cable)
Preacher curl machine or cable curl. Full range of motion.
 3 x 12 No swinging.
Hammer Curl (Dumbbell or Cable)
Neutral grip. Hits brachialis and forearms.
 2 x 12 Control the negative.
Friday — Full Body + Cardio Compound
45 min | Hit everything, finish with light cardio
ExerciseSets x RepsNote
Leg Press (Machine)
High foot placement again. Moderate weight. Control every rep.
 2 x 12 Not max effort today.
Chest Press (Machine)
One compound push movement. Moderate weight.
 2 x 10 Full range.
Lat Pulldown (Machine)
One compound pull. Moderate weight.
 2 x 10 Squeeze at bottom.
Shoulder Press (Machine)
Overhead press. Moderate weight.
 2 x 10 Core tight.
Glute Bridge / Hip Thrust
Bodyweight or light load. Burn out the glutes.
 2 x 20 Hold at top.
Bicep Curl + Tricep Pushdown (Superset)
One set curls, immediately to tricep pushdown. No rest between.
 2 x 12 each Superset = arms finished.
Plank
Core finisher. Hold as long as good form allows.
 2 x max time Form > time.
