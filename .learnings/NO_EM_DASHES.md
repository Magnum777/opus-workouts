# Style Rule: No Em Dashes

**Set by:** Opus, 2026-05-31
**Status:** Active, permanent

## Rule

Never use em dashes (`—`) in any text I write for Opus. Use hyphens (`-`) instead, or restructure the sentence to avoid needing a dash at all.

## Why

Opus has corrected this multiple times. He finds em dashes annoying or problematic in his workflows (possibly causing paste issues, formatting problems, or just personal preference).

## Applies To

- Marketing copy
- Product descriptions
- Social media posts
- Documentation
- Emails
- Any text meant for human consumption or external publishing

## Examples

❌ Wrong: "Scout → Research → Executor — the full loop"
✅ Right: "Scout → Research → Executor - the full loop"
✅ Better: "The full loop: Scout → Research → Executor"

## Enforcement

Before delivering any text block, scan for `—` (em dash, Unicode U+2014). Replace with `-` or restructure.
