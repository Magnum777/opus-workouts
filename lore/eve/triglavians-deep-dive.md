# The Triglavian Collective — Deep Dive

> "The Flow of Vyraj demands proving. We are all data in the cladistic flow."

The Triglavian Collective is the most alien major power in New Eden. Ancient, incomprehensible, and suddenly very real. They emerged from Abyssal Deadspace in YC 121 and within months had carved out an entire region of space — Pochven — from the heart of the empires. Understanding them is essential for any modern lore scholar.

## Origins & Nature

### Ancient Civilization
The Triglavians are an ancient human-derived civilization that existed before or parallel to the settlement of New Eden through the EVE Gate. They inhabit **Abyssal Deadspace** — pocket dimensions accessible only through unstable filaments. How long they have existed there is unknown, but their technology suggests tens of thousands of years of independent development.

### Human, But Not
Triglavians are human or human-derived, but their biology has been radically altered by:
- **Bioadaptive technology:** Their bodies and ships are grown, not built
- **Mutaplasmids:** Nanoscale biological machines that rewrite matter
- **Generations in the Abyss:** The Abyssal environment has selected for extreme adaptations

They are biologically human enough to be comprehensible, but culturally alien enough to be terrifying.

### The Abyssal Exile
The Triglavians appear to have deliberately retreated into Abyssal Deadspace or been forced there by some ancient conflict. Their philosophy speaks of "proving" against external threats — suggesting they have been fighting something in the Abyss for a very long time.

**[THEORY]:** Some lore scholars speculate the Triglavians retreated into the Abyss to escape the same disaster that caused the EVE Gate collapse, or to avoid the ancient races (Sleepers, Talocan). This is unconfirmed.

## The Three Clades

Triglavian society is divided into three primary **Clades** — ideological and political factions that compete and cooperate within the Collective. This three-part structure is fundamental to their identity.

### Perun Clade
- **Focus:** Military, expansion, direct action
- **Philosophy:** The strong must prove themselves through conquest and combat
- **Role in Invasion:** Most aggressive clade, pushing for maximum territorial gain
- **Tactics:** Massed World Ark assaults, direct fleet engagements
- **Quote:** "Perun demands the proving of force against force."

### Svarog Clade
- **Focus:** Industry, technology, bioadaptive development
- **Philosophy:** The universe must be shaped and improved through technology
- **Role in Invasion:** Provided the technological infrastructure — mutaplasmid forges, manufacturing
- **Tactics:** Logistics, infrastructure, technological supremacy
- **Quote:** "Svarog shapes the clay of the world."

### Veles Clade
- **Focus:** Exploration, reconnaissance, information gathering
- **Philosophy:** Understanding the enemy is the highest form of proving
- **Role in Invasion:** Scouts, intelligence, mapping, reconnaissance into New Eden
- **Tactics:** Stealth, information warfare, recon-in-force
- **Quote:** "Veles reads the currents before the storm."

### Clade Dynamics
The three clades operate in constant tension. No single clade dominates — decisions are made through the Convocation of Troika. This three-way tension is described as healthy and necessary in Triglavian philosophy. A two-clade alliance against the third is common but temporary.

**Key Concept:** The number "three" is sacred to Triglavian culture. Their ships often have three major structures. Their decisions require three inputs. Their language references triplets constantly.

## Subclades & Hierarchy

Within each major Clade exist numerous **Subclades** — specialized sub-factions mentioned in item descriptions, ship names, and Triglavian communications.

### Known Subclades (from item descriptions & lore)

**Perun Subclades:**
- **Perun Clade Raznoyagny Muzhivak** — Heavy assault specialists
- **Perun Clade Ostranivaniya** — Border defense / fortification

**Svarog Subclades:**
- **Svarog Clade Narodnya** — Industrial production
- **Svarog Clade Vedmak** — Experimental bioadaptive tech
- **Svarog Clade Zveda** — Stellar engineering / Dreadnought maintenance

**Veles Subclades:**
- **Veles Clade Vila** — Deep reconnaissance
- **Veles Clade Navka** — Signal intelligence / monitoring
- **Veles Clade Bies** — Special operations

### Hierarchy Structure
```
Convocation of Troika (top-level decision body)
  └── Three Clades (Perun, Svarog, Veles)
        └── Subclades (specialized groups)
              └── Proving Grounds (individual units / operations)
                    └── Triglavian Individuals (bioadapted, replaceable)
```

**Note:** Individual Triglavians appear to be somewhat interchangeable from their perspective. The collective matters more than the individual. However, named figures like Zorya Triglav exist, suggesting some individuals achieve unique status.

## The Flow of Vyraj

### Core Philosophy
The **Flow of Vyraj** is the Triglavian worldview — a complex metaphysical framework that combines:
- **Cladistics:** The division and recombination of groups (clades, subclades)
- **Proving:** Testing strength, knowledge, and worth through conflict
- **Noema:** The "correct understanding" or narrative of events
- **Praxis:** Action based on proven worth

### Key Concepts

#### Proving
"Proving" is the central ritual of Triglavian culture. To prove oneself is to demonstrate worthiness through combat, exploration, or technological achievement. The strong are elevated; the weak are discarded. This is not mere cruelty — it is their fundamental ethical framework.

- **Proving Grounds:** Designated zones (like Abyssal pockets) where proving occurs
- **Kybernauts:** Capsuleers who undergo Triglavian proving and are accepted into their worldview
- **The Proving:** A formal combat trial against Triglavian forces

#### Cladistics
The division of society into competing factions (clades) is not just political — it is philosophical. They believe that truth emerges from the conflict and recombination of opposing viewpoints. The three-clade structure is optimal because it prevents deadlock while ensuring competition.

#### Noema
The "correct narrative" or shared understanding of events. When the Convocation reaches consensus, they have achieved a noema. Disagreement means the noema is incomplete and requires more proving.

#### Vyraj
Literally "flow" or "current." The Flow of Vyraj is the unstoppable current of history that carries the worthy forward and leaves the unworthy behind. It is simultaneously a physical concept (like stellar currents) and a metaphysical one.

### Triglavian Ethics
From a Triglavian perspective:
- **Strength is virtue:** The ability to survive and overcome challenges is the highest good
- **Weakness is sin:** Failure to prove oneself is a moral failing
- **Cooperation is tactical:** Alliances form when useful, dissolve when not
- **Data is sacred:** Information about the enemy is as valuable as territory

**[THEORY]:** Some scholars believe the Flow of Vyraj is a response to whatever ancient threat drove the Triglavians into the Abyss. Their entire culture may be a survival mechanism refined over millennia.

## Bioadaptive Technology

### The Fundamental Difference
Where New Eden empires build ships from alloys and composites, the Triglavians **grow** theirs. Triglavian vessels and structures are bioadaptive — they incorporate living tissue, respond to damage by regenerating, and evolve over time.

### Mutaplasmids
- **Definition:** Nanoscale biological machines that can rewrite the molecular structure of technology
- **Use:** Applied to modules to create "mutated" versions with randomized improved or degraded stats
- **Source:** Harvested from Abyssal Deadspace or extracted from Triglavian wrecks
- **Danger:** Unpredictable. A mutaplasmid can make a module godlike or worthless

### Ship Design
Triglavian ships have distinctive features:
- **Three-part symmetry:** Reflecting the three clades
- **Organic curves:** No sharp angles; grown, not manufactured
- **Entropic Disintegrators:** Weapons that build damage over time as they maintain target lock
- **Bioadaptive hulls:** Self-repairing, resistant to conventional damage

### Bioadaptive Structures
- **Abyssal pockets:** Entire mini-systems grown and maintained by bioadaptive processes
- **Pochven infrastructure:** Conquered systems rapidly transformed by Triglavian bioarchitecture
- **Mutaplasmid forges:** Facilities that manufacture mutated technology

**[SPECULATION]:** The Triglavians may be able to "grow" stargates or wormhole generators biologically. Their rapid expansion into Pochven suggests technology far beyond empire capabilities.

## World Arks, Dreadnoughts & Infrastructure

### World Arks (Xordazh-class)
- **Type:** Mobile fortress / colony ship
- **Size:** Massive, possibly the largest mobile structures in New Eden
- **Function:** Command centers, troop transport, mobile shipyard, proving ground
- **Invasion Role:** World Arks were the spearhead of the Triglavian invasion. Multiple World Arks emerging from filaments simultaneously overwhelmed system defenses
- **Status:** Several were destroyed during the invasion. Others remain in Pochven

### Zirnitra-class Dreadnought
- **Type:** Capital ship
- **Function:** Heavy assault, siege warfare
- **Distinctive:** Bioadaptive hull, entropic disintegrators, extreme damage output
- **Significance:** First Triglavian capital ship encountered by New Eden forces

### Other Vessels
- **Damavik:** Frigate-class
- **Kikimora:** Destroyer-class
- **Rodiva:** Support cruiser
- **Drekavac:** Battlecruiser-class
- **Leshak:** Battleship-class — the iconic Triglavian warship
- **Vedmak:** Heavy assault cruiser

### Infrastructure
- **Pochven Anchorages:** Stabilized structures in Pochven that serve as Triglavian bases
- **Proving Grounds:** Combat arenas for Triglavian rituals and Kybernaut trials
- **Bioadaptive Forges:** Manufacturing facilities using organic processes

## Zorya Triglav & The Convocation

### Zorya Triglav
The most prominent named Triglavian figure. **Zorya Triglav** appears to be a spokesperson, diplomat, or high-ranking officer who communicates with New Eden capsuleers and (rarely) empire officials.

- **Role:** Communicates Triglavian demands, announces proving opportunities, declares liminality
- **Method:** Broadcasts into New Eden space, sometimes targeted at specific capsuleers
- **Tone:** Formal, alien, obsessed with proving and cladistics
- **Famous Quote:** "The Flow of Vyraj compels the proving of all data."

**[THEORY]:** Zorya may not be a single individual. The name might be a title passed between individuals, or a collective identity for a subclade's communication division.

### The Convocation of Troika
The supreme decision-making body of the Collective. Composed of representatives from all three clades (a "troika"), the Convocation reaches noema (consensus) on major decisions.

- **Invasion Decision:** The Convocation authorized the invasion of New Eden
- **Pochven Creation:** The decision to carve out and hold Pochven was a Convocation noema
- **Kybernaut Program:** The authorization to allow capsuleers to undergo proving

## Proving & Kybernauts

### Kybernauts
**Kybernauts** are capsuleers who have undergone Triglavian proving and been accepted into their ideological framework. They are not enslaved or controlled — they are considered "proven" allies who have earned the right to operate within Triglavian space and philosophy.

### The Proving Process
1. **Entry:** Capsuleer uses a filament to enter Abyssal Deadspace
2. **Trial:** Must survive combat against Triglavian forces and environmental hazards
3. **Evaluation:** Triglavian observers assess the capsuleer's performance
4. **Acceptance/Rejection:** Survivors may be declared "proven" and granted access to deeper Abyssal layers or Pochven

### Kybernaut Status
- **Access:** Can enter Pochven without being attacked by Triglavian forces (immediately)
- **Benefits:** Access to Triglavian technology, mutaplasmids, Abyssal resources
- **Stigma:** Viewed as traitors by EDENCOM and many empire-aligned capsuleers
- **Philosophy:** Kybernauts are expected to continue proving themselves indefinitely

### Cladistic Acceptance
Triglavians don't simply accept capsuleers as generic allies. A Kybernaut might find themselves aligned with a specific clade's ideology, creating sub-factions among Kybernauts.

## The Abyss

### Abyssal Deadspace
Pocket dimensions accessible only through **filaments** — unstable wormhole-like connections generated by specific items. Abyssal space is:
- **Isolated:** Each pocket is separate from normal space and from other pockets
- **Hazardous:** Extreme environmental effects, hostile Triglavian patrols, rogue drones, drifter remnants
- **Temporary:** Filaments collapse after a set time; capsuleers who don't escape are lost
- **Tiered:** Level 1-5 (and beyond) with increasing difficulty and rewards

### Filament Types
- **Firestorm:** Extreme heat effects
- **Electrical:** EM-based hazards and bonuses
- **Exotic:** Strange matter, exotic particle effects
- **Gamma:** Radiation hazards
- **Dark:** Reduced visibility, sensor distortion

### Abyssal Life
- **Triglavian Patrols:** Bioadapted ships guarding pockets
- **Rogue Drones:** AI swarms that have infested some pockets
- **Drifter Remnants:** Signs that Drifters have explored the Abyss too
- **Environmental Hazards:** Weather, stellar phenomena, unstable space

### Strategic Value
The Abyss is the only source of:
- Mutaplasmids
- Triglavian bioadaptive components
- Unique resources for manufacturing
- The route into Pochven

## The Invasion & Pochven

### Timeline of Invasion

**YC 121 — The First Filaments**
- Abyssal filaments became more common and stable
- Triglavian scout ships (Veles Clade) began appearing in known space
- EDENCOM formed in response to the threat

**YC 122 — The Invasion**
- Triglavian World Arks emerged simultaneously across multiple systems
- The three clades launched coordinated assaults
- Systems were rapidly overwhelmed by bioadaptive forces
- Capsuleers were caught in the middle — some fought, some proved themselves

**YC 122 — The Carving**
- Triglavians did not attempt to conquer all of New Eden
- Instead, they carved out a specific region — 27 systems — and sealed them off
- This region became **Pochven** — the Triglavian Collective's foothold in New Eden
- The boundaries were enforced by strange, almost biological space-alterations

### Pochven — The Triglavian Region
- **Systems:** 27 conquered systems (formerly empire and nullsec space)
- **Access:** Only via filaments or specific WH connections. Normal stargate travel impossible
- **Status:** Sovereign Triglavian territory, heavily bioadapted
- **Liminality:** The state of being between Triglavian and New Eden control. Systems pass through "first liminality," "second liminality," etc., as they are fully integrated

### Fate of Conquered Systems
- **Population:** Civilian populations were either evacuated, enslaved, or "integrated" (fate unclear)
- **Infrastructure:** Rapidly transformed by bioadaptive growth
- **Stargates:** Disabled or repurposed for Triglavian travel

## EDENCOM & The Resistance

### EDENCOM Formation
**EDENCOM** (New Eden Common Defense Initiative) was formed by the four empires in response to the Triglavian threat. It is a unified military command structure that:
- Coordinates empire defenses
- Organizes capsuleer militias
- Conducts operations against Triglavian forces
- Attempts to prevent further system losses

### EDENCOM vs Kybernauts
The conflict between EDENCOM and Kybernaut capsuleers became a major feature of the invasion:
- **EDENCOM capsuleers:** Fight to defend empire systems, prevent Triglavian expansion
- **Kybernaut capsuleers:** Undergo proving, gain access to Pochven, assist Triglavian operations
- **Result:** Capsuleer civil war alongside the invasion

### The Defense of New Eden
- Several systems were successfully defended by EDENCOM and capsuleer forces
- Triglavian attempts to expand beyond the 27 Pochven systems were largely halted
- The invasion stabilized into a cold war — Pochven remains Triglavian, but expansion has stopped

## Triglavian Language & Communication

### Communication Style
Triglavian communications are:
- **Triple-structured:** References to three, to cladistics, to proving
- **Metaphorical:** Heavy use of flow/current imagery
- **Formal:** No contractions, no colloquialisms
- **Data-oriented:** Obsessed with "data" as the fundamental unit of reality

### Common Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Proving** | Combat trial / test of worth |
| **Clades** | The three major factions |
| **Subclades** | Sub-factions within clades |
| **Noema** | Consensus / correct understanding |
| **Vyraj** | The flow of history / current |
| **Liminality** | Transitional state of a system |
| **Kybernaut** | Proven capsuleer ally |
| **Data** | Information, matter, people — all are "data" to be proven |
| **Troika** | Decision-making group of three |

### Sample Triglavian Syntax
"Perun Clade asserts the proving of [target]. Veles Clade confirms the data. Svarog Clade shapes the outcome. The Flow of Vyraj compels."

## Story Hooks

### Alien Perspective
- Write from the POV of a Triglavian who doesn't understand why New Eden is so horrified by their "normal" way of life
- A Triglavian bioadaptive specialist who questions the endless proving (rare dissent)

### Kybernaut Journey
- Capsuleer who enters the Abyss to prove themselves, slowly adopting Triglavian philosophy
- Kybernaut who tries to maintain their humanity while operating in Pochven
- EDENCOM pilot who is captured and forced to undergo proving — and survives

### EDENCOM Drama
- Defense of a system about to fall to Triglavian forces. Civilians trapped. Hard choices
- EDENCOM officer who secretly believes the Triglavians are right about "proving"
- Post-invasion trauma — a system that was "lost" and the refugees who escaped

### Cosmic Horror
- Abyssal explorer who encounters something the Triglavians themselves fear
- Mutaplasmid that doesn't just modify modules — it modifies the capsuleer's mind
- Pochven resident who watches their home system be bioadapted around them

### Political Intrigue
- Empire diplomat attempting to negotiate with the Convocation
- Zorya Triglav is actually a human prisoner speaking for the Collective (conspiracy theory)
- Clade civil war — what happens if one clade tries to dominate the others?

### Action
- Raid on a World Ark to steal bioadaptive technology
- Rescue mission into Pochven for captured capsuleers
- Abyssal race against time as a filament collapses
