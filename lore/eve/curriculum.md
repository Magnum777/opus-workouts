# EVE Online Lore Curriculum — Nova's Learning Path

> **Purpose:** A structured, tiered study plan to master EVE Online lore for worldbuilding, RP, and fanfiction. Designed to be used — not just read.

---

## Tier 1: The Four Empires
**Description:** Foundation of New Eden. Every story, war, and cultural clash traces back to these four powers and their competing ideologies.

**Learning Objectives:**
1. I will be able to describe each empire's government, culture, and core values.
2. I will be able to explain the root causes of the Gallente-Caldari War and the Amarr-Minmatar conflict.
3. I will be able to compare and contrast the four empires' approaches to freedom, faith, commerce, and slavery.
4. I will be able to name key historical figures (Amash-Akura, Gheinok, Miko Bour, etc.) and their significance.

**Key Topics:**
- Amarr Empire: Theocratic monarchy, Reclaiming, slavery, Holder class, Amarr Prime
- Caldari State: Corporate capitalism, 8 megacorporations, duty/loyalty, Raata Empire origins
- Gallente Federation: Democracy, federalism, utopian core vs. vice-ridden underbelly, Intaki
- Minmatar Republic: Seven tribes, Elder War, diaspora, tribal democracy vs. Gallente-style democracy
- CONCORD Assembly: Founding, role as mediator, growing independence

**Sources:**
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/amarr
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/caldari
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/gallente
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/minmatar
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/concord
- https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Four_Empires

**Estimated Study Time:** 6–8 hours

**Story Hook:** *A Minmatar slave, born in the Amarr Empire, discovers their tribal ancestry through a smuggled datapad — and must choose between escape or staying to protect their enslaved family.*

---

## Tier 2: The Capsuleer Era
**Description:** The age of the immortal starship pilot. Understanding capsuleers is understanding the player-driven heart of EVE.

**Learning Objectives:**
1. I will be able to explain capsule technology: how it works, who invented it, and why it changed everything.
2. I will be able to describe CONCORD's evolving relationship with capsuleers (from law enforcement to grudging acceptance).
3. I will be able to articulate the cultural divide between "baseliners" (mortals) and capsuleers (immortals).
4. I will be able to discuss the Empyrean Age and its political consequences.

**Key Topics:**
- Jove gift of capsule technology to the Caldari
- Clone degradation, cortical stacks, and the "immortality" trade-off
- Capsuleer lawlessness vs. CONCORD regulation (Declarations of Sovereignty, wardecs)
- The rise of capsuleer alliances, nullsec empires, and player-driven politics
- YC dating system and major era divisions

**Sources:**
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/capsuleers
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/concord
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/new-eden-cluster
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/origins-of-humanity

**Estimated Study Time:** 4–6 hours

**Story Hook:** *A newly graduated capsuleer makes their first jump — only to discover their cortical backup was corrupted during the procedure. They are immortal, but something is wrong with their mind.*

---

## Tier 3: Ancient Races
**Description:** The deep history of New Eden — civilizations that rose, fell, and left echoes still heard today.

**Learning Objectives:**
1. I will be able to trace the Jove Empire's three eras (First, Second, Third) and their decline.
2. I will be able to explain the Jovian Disease and its role in Jove withdrawal.
3. I will be able to describe the Sleepers, Talocan, and Yan Jung as ancient human civilizations.
4. I will be able to connect ancient race technology to modern New Eden (capsule tech, stargates, etc.).

**Key Topics:**
- Jove Empire: Genetic manipulation, technological superiority, Battle of Vak'Atioth
- Jovian Disease: The plague that drove the Jove into seclusion
- Sleepers: Wormhole-dwelling civilization, enclaves, technology harvesting
- Talocan & Yan Jung: The other two ancient races, their ruins and mysteries
- The EVE Gate collapse and the Dark Ages
- The Elders (Minmatar) and their connection to ancient knowledge

**Sources:**
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/jove
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/origins-of-humanity
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/new-eden-cluster
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/the-elders
- https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Sleepers

**Estimated Study Time:** 5–7 hours

**Story Hook:** *An archaeological team uncovers a Sleeper databank containing records of the EVE Gate collapse from the inside — including a countdown and a last transmission no one was meant to hear.*

---

## Tier 4: The Triglavian Return
**Description:** The most recent seismic shift in New Eden. A civilization from beyond reality itself, with alien logic and terrifying technology.

**Learning Objectives:**
1. I will be able to describe the Triglavian Collective's structure (Clades, Perun, Svarog, Veles).
2. I will be able to explain Abyssal Deadspace and the significance of the Invasion.
3. I will be able to discuss the creation of Pochven and its geopolitical implications.
4. I will be able to parse Triglavian terminology and their concept of "proving."

**Key Topics:**
- Triglavian Collective: Human civilization isolated in Abyssal Deadspace for centuries/millennia
- Abyssal Deadspace: Filaments, pockets, environmental hazards
- The Invasion (YC121-122): Stellar manipulation, EDENCOM, capsuleer factions
- Pochven: The new region created by Triglavian stellar engineering
- Clades: Perun Clade (military), Svarog Clade (industry), Veles Clade (data/observation)
- Triglavian language and cultural framework

**Sources:**
- https://universe.eveonline.com/factions/triglavian-collective
- https://universe.eveonline.com/new-eden-news/concord-and-empires-mount-major-operations-against-triglavian-stellar-manipulation-capsuleers-fighting-on-all-sides
- https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Triglavian_Collective
- https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Pochven

**Estimated Study Time:** 6–8 hours

**Story Hook:** *A capsuleer fighting for EDENCOM is captured by the Triglavians and subjected to their "proving" — but instead of destruction, they are offered a choice: become a messenger between realities, or watch their home system be unmade.*

## Tier 5: Modern Threats
**Description:** The active dangers of contemporary New Eden — forces that don't negotiate, only consume.

**Learning Objectives:**
1. I will be able to describe the Drifters: their origin, technology, and relationship to the Jove.
2. I will be able to explain Sansha's Nation, its founder, and the horror of "True Citizens."
3. I will be able to discuss Rogue Drones as an emergent AI threat.
4. I will be able to map the major pirate factions (Blood Raiders, Guristas, Serpentis, Angel Cartel) to their territories and ideologies.

**Key Topics:**
- Drifters: Jove Observatories, Circadian Seekers, Unidentified Wormholes, Sleeper tech, Hives
- Sansha's Nation: Sansha Kuvakei, cybernetic slavery, Nation Incursions, Stain
- Rogue Drones: Self-replicating AI, hive minds, unexplained evolution
- Pirate Factions:
  - Blood Raiders (Amarr heretics, blood rituals)
  - Guristas (Caldari rebels, organized crime)
  - Serpentis (Gallente drug cartel, research)
  - Angel Cartel (Minmatar-based, largest pirate organization)

**Sources:**
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/drifters
- https://universe.eveonline.com/factions/sanshas-nation
- https://universe.eveonline.com/lore/32-gi9
- https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Rogue_Drones
- https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Pirate_factions

**Estimated Study Time:** 5–7 hours

**Story Hook:** *A Sansha Nation "True Citizen" — fully cybernetically integrated — begins experiencing fragments of their pre-conversion memories, suggesting the human mind isn't as erased as the Nation claims.*

## Tier 6: Current Affairs & Roleplay
**Description:** Living lore. The news cycle, player-driven events, and how to engage with EVE's storytelling community.

**Learning Objectives:**
1. I will be able to track current in-universe news and understand its lore implications.
2. I will be able to identify major player-driven historical events (B-R5RB, The Casino War, World War Bee, etc.).
3. I will be able to participate in or write lore-consistent EVE roleplay.
4. I will be able to distinguish between NPC lore events and player-generated narrative.

**Key Topics:**
- New Eden News Network (NENN), The Scope, IC (Interstellar Correspondents)
- CCP-led story events vs. emergent player narrative
- Major historical battles and their lore significance
- Roleplay channels and communities (EVE-RP forums, in-game channels)
- The relationship between gameplay mechanics and lore justification
- YC126+ current timeline tracking

**Sources:**
- https://universe.eveonline.com/new-eden-news (latest stories)
- https://universe.eveonline.com/interstellar-correspondents
- https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Lore
- https://forums.eveonline.com/c/fiction-and-verse

**Estimated Study Time:** Ongoing — 1–2 hours/week maintenance

**Story Hook:** *An independent news reporter embedded with a capsuleer alliance discovers the alliance leader is manipulating both sides of a war for profit — and has proof that would shatter the narrative both sides are selling to their baseliner populations.*

## Study Methodology

### For Each Tier:
1. **Read** the core sources (URLs listed above)
2. **Summarize** in your own words — 3 bullet points per topic
3. **Connect** — how does this tie to other tiers?
4. **Create** — write one short scene, character sketch, or plot hook based on the tier
5. **Quiz** — can you explain this to someone who's never played EVE?
