# EVE Lore Glossary

## Politics & Factions

**Amarr Empire** (ah-MAR)
The oldest and largest of the core empires, a rigid theocratic imperial state built on slavery and religious doctrine. Ruled by the Amarr Emperor and the Five Heirs. Known for its massive cathedral ships and laser weaponry.

**Caldari State** (kal-DAR-ee)
A hyper-capitalist corporate state where megacorporations hold more power than the government itself. Militaristic, efficient, and technologically advanced. Home of missile and shield technology.

**Gallente Federation** (gal-LENT)
A democratic federation built on ideals of personal freedom, cultural diversity, and free expression. The most populous empire. Known for drone and hybrid weapon technology.

**Minmatar Republic** (MIN-mah-tar)
A tribal confederation founded by freed slaves who rebelled against the Amarr. Scrappy, independent, and shaped by trauma. Known for projectile weapons and speed.

**Jove Empire** (JOE-v)
An ancient, highly advanced civilization of genetically modified humans who have largely withdrawn from galactic affairs. Their technology far surpasses the core empires. Mysterious and inscrutable.

**CONCORD** (CON-cord)
The Consolidated Cooperation and Relations Command Assembly—an independent organization founded to mediate between empires, now a powerful policing and regulatory force with its own military.

**DED**
The Directive Enforcement Department, CONCORD's police force and military arm. Maintains the strongest fleet in New Eden. Includes the elite SARO special forces unit.

**Sisters of EVE (SoE)**
A humanitarian organization providing aid across the cluster. Also operates advanced research programs and maintains the Thera colony. Pioneered alpha clone technology.

**Ammatar Mandate**
A semi-autonomous region within the Amarr Empire populated by Minmatar who collaborated with the Amarr during the Rebellion. Ruled by the Ammatar Consulate.

**Thukker Tribe**
A nomadic Minmatar tribe that travels in massive caravans through deep space. Independent and self-sufficient, they maintain close ties to the Minmatar Elders.

**Khanid Kingdom**
A breakaway state from the Amarr Empire founded by Khanid the Killer after he refused to commit ritual suicide upon failing to claim the throne. Now an uneasy ally of the Empire.

**Syndicate**
A region of lawless space controlled by the Intaki Syndicate, a criminal organization that operates as a pseudo-government. Known for its "anything goes" markets.

**Deathless Circle**
A criminal organization of unclear origin, active in intercepting advanced technology. In turmoil during the YC127 Drifter Crisis over Warden access.

**Angel Cartel**
One of the most powerful pirate factions, operating from the Curse region. Known for advanced ship design and criminal enterprise.

**Guristas**
A Caldari-origin pirate faction founded by the infamous Rabbit and Fatal. Controls the Venal region and operates like a organized crime syndicate.

**Blood Raider Covenant**
A splinter Amarr sect practicing blood rituals and human sacrifice. Feared for their fanaticism and advanced energy vampire technology.

**Sansha's Nation**
A rogue state founded by Sansha Kuvakei, creating "True Slaves" via cybernetic brainwashing. Wages incursions into empire space to abduct citizens.

## Technology

**Capsule / Pod**
A hydrostatic, sealed armored shell filled with breathable liquid suspension. The pilot is wired directly into their ship's systems, experiencing the vessel as an extension of their body. The capsule's nanotoxin system enables safe consciousness transfer at death.

**Clone**
A genetically identical body kept in stasis, prepared to receive a transneural burn scan. The cornerstone of capsuleer immortality.

**Transneural Burn Scanner**
A device that takes a complete snapshot of a pilot's mind-brain state and transmits it to a clone at the moment of death. Must fire within a nanosecond window. Destroys the original brain in the process.

**Jump Clone**
A secondary clone body installed at a remote station, allowing a capsuleer to transfer consciousness between bodies without dying.

**Alpha Clone**
A simpler, limited form of cloning developed by the Sisters of EVE, allowing broader access to capsule technology without full immortality or advanced skill training.

**FTL Communications**
Faster-than-light communication networks routed through the stargate infrastructure, enabling real-time data transmission across the cluster.

**Stargate**
Ancient acceleration gates that create stable wormholes between star systems, forming the backbone of interstellar travel. The original EVE Gate was a massive stargate that collapsed millennia ago.

**Warp Drive**
A ship propulsion system that creates a localized warp bubble, allowing faster-than-light travel within a solar system.

**Warp Tunnel**
The visual and physical phenomenon experienced when a ship enters warp—often described as disorienting and psychologically taxing.

**Implants**
Neural and physical enhancements installed in capsuleers to improve interface capability, skill acquisition, or raw abilities. Some are consumer-grade; military-grade implants are highly restricted.

**Blueprints**
Specialized code used by manufacturing facilities to program robotic assembly of ships, modules, and ammunition from raw minerals.

**Entosis Link**
A device using recovered Sleeper/Drifter technology to interface with and capture sovereignty structures. Deployed during the sov warfare rework.

**Antikythera Elements**
Rare materials recovered from Drifter and Sleeper technology, used in the production of advanced devices including Entosis Links.

**Stellar Transmuter**
An ancient technology capable of manipulating stars on a fundamental level. Believed responsible for the Seyllin Disaster and Caroline's Star.

## Space & Cosmology

**New Eden**
The name for the star cluster inhabited by humanity. Sometimes used broadly for all explored space, sometimes specifically for the region around the former EVE Gate.

**Known Space (K-Space)**
The regions of New Eden connected by the stargate network and mapped by CONCORD: Highsec, Lowsec, and Nullsec.

**Highsec**
High-security space (security rating 0.5–1.0) where CONCORD enforces the law. Aggression against non-criminals triggers an inevitable military response.

**Lowsec**
Low-security space (security rating 0.1–0.4) where CONCORD does not intervene but faction navies and gate guns maintain limited order. A lawless frontier.

**Nullsec**
Null-security space (security rating 0.0) where no empire or CONCORD law applies. The domain of capsuleer alliances, pirates, and independent organizations.

**W-Space / Anoikis** (an-OY-kis)
Wormhole space: a separate, unmapped cluster of star systems accessible only through unstable wormholes. Home to the Sleeper civilization and Drifter strongholds.

**Wormhole**
A naturally occurring (or technologically stabilized) tunnel through spacetime connecting two distant points. Wormholes in New Eden are unstable and collapse after a limited time or mass transit.

**Pochven** (POCH-ven)
The Triglavian-controlled region created during the Triglavian Invasion. Systems that were pulled from known space into the "Domain of Bujan." Extremely dangerous and isolated.

**EVE Gate**
The massive, collapsed stargate in the New Eden system through which humanity's ancestors arrived from the Milky Way. Now a dangerous, unstable stellar anomaly surrounded by debris.

**The Cluster**
Informal term for all of known space and connected wormhole regions viewed as a single political and economic unit.

**Constellation**
A grouping of star systems within a region, typically 10–15 systems bound by shared stellar geography.

**Region**
A major territorial division of known space, containing multiple constellations and dozens to hundreds of star systems.

**System**
A single star and its orbiting bodies. The basic unit of territorial control in New Eden.

## Military & Warfare

**Sovereignty (Sov)**
Territorial ownership of a nullsec star system, claimed through structures, military presence, and administrative control. The basis of nullsec empire-building.

**Doomsday Weapon**
A superweapon deployed by titans and certain Drifter vessels, capable of destroying entire fleets in a single activation. The Drifter variant fires when their overshield is breached.

**Overshield**
An additional shield layer used by Drifter vessels. When depleted, it breaks target locks and triggers the doomsday response.

**EWAR**
Electronic Warfare: the use of jamming, dampening, disruption, and neutralization to degrade enemy sensor and weapons capability.

**Capacitor Warfare**
Tactics focused on draining an enemy ship's capacitor (power system) to disable their modules and defenses.

**Suicide Ganking**
The practice of attacking profitable targets in highsec knowing CONCORD will destroy the attacker. The goal is to destroy the target and loot the wreck before or during the CONCORD response.

**Faction Warfare**
Formalized proxy warfare between the empires, conducted by capsuleer militias under the CONCORD Emergency Militia War Powers Act. Fought in designated warzone systems.

**Titan**
The largest class of starship, capable of doomsday weapons, fleet-wide jump bridges, and massive logistical support. So expensive and powerful that losing one is a strategic event.

**Supercarrier / Hel**
Massive carrier vessels capable of deploying entire squadrons of fighter drones. Mobile fleet bases and projection platforms.

**Capital Ship**
A broad class of oversized vessels (dreadnoughts, carriers, force auxiliaries, supercarriers, titans) too large to use stargates and requiring cynosural fields to jump.

**Cynosural Field**
A beacon that allows capital ships to perform a jump drive teleport to a specific location. The foundation of capital warfare logistics.

**Smartbomb**
An area-of-effect weapon that damages everything in proximity, including the firing ship if not shielded. Used for clearing drone swarms or suicide tactics.

**Podding**
The act of destroying a capsuleer's escape pod after their ship is destroyed. Kills the pilot and forces a clone jump—considered the ultimate humiliation in combat.

## Triglavian-Specific

**Triglavian Collective** (trig-LAY-vee-an)
An ancient civilization from the Abyssal Deadspace regions, recently emerged into known space. Operates through a tripartite social structure and biological enhancement.

**Clade**
A sub-division of the Triglavian Collective, organized by function and specialization. Major clades include Svarog, Veles, and Perun.

**Domain of Bujan**
The Triglavian term for their home region and philosophy—the space they claim and seek to expand into.

**Flow of Vyraj**
The Triglavian concept of proper evolutionary and martial progression. To "flow" is to advance through proven fitness.

**Proving**
A Triglavian ritual of combat-based validation. Those who survive are "glorified" and their fitness is accepted.

**Narodnya**
The Triglavian term for their people or subjects—those who exist within the Collective's structure.

**Koschoi**
A Triglavian caste or multitude—often translated as leaders or commanders within their tripartite structure.

**Navka**
A Triglavian caste or multitude—often translated as the masses or the body of the Collective.

**Extirpation**
The Triglavian term for total elimination of an enemy. Applied to the Drifters ("Ancient Enemy Azdaja") and any they deem unfit.

**Pravda**
Truth or rightness in the Triglavian worldview. Fitness and correctness proven through conflict.

**Abyssal Deadspace**
Unstable, pocket dimensions accessible through filaments. The home of the Triglavian Collective and the source of their mutaplasmid technology.

**Mutaplasmid**
Triglavian bio-nanotechnology capable of mutating and enhancing ship modules, often unpredictably.

**Pochven Filaments**
Technology allowing travel into and out of Pochven. Highly valuable and dangerous to use.

## Economics & Society

**ISK**
Interstellar Kredits—the universal currency of New Eden, managed by the Secure Commerce Commission (SCC).

**SCC**
The Secure Commerce Commission, a CONCORD division overseeing interstellar trade, markets, and currency exchange. Ensures (theoretically) fair and neutral commerce.

**Tech I / Tech II / Tech III**
Technology tiers: Tech I is standard; Tech II is advanced, specialized gear requiring rare materials and reverse engineering; Tech III is the most advanced, modular technology derived from ancient (often Sleeper/Triglavian) sources.

**Boosters**
Narcotic drugs consumed for recreational or combat purposes. Neural boosters can temporarily enhance a capsuleer's cognitive and combat capabilities. Illegal in many jurisdictions.

**Planetary Interaction (PI)**
The practice of extracting and processing resources from planets, managed remotely by capsuleers. A major industry in the cluster.

**Freighter**
Massive cargo vessels used for bulk transport of goods between trade hubs. Slow, defenseless, and lucrative targets.

**Trade Hub**
A major market center—typically a specific station in a specific system (e.g., Jita 4-4 in the Caldari State). The economic heart of the cluster.

**Corporation / Corp**
The basic organizational unit of capsuleer society. Corps join alliances. The equivalent of companies, guilds, or military units.

**Alliance**
A coalition of corporations operating under a shared banner. The largest alliances control vast swathes of nullsec.

**Mercenary**
A capsuleer who sells military services for ISK. Mercenary coalitions are major power brokers in lowsec and nullsec conflicts.

**Bounty**
A price placed on a capsuleer's head by CONCORD, an empire, or an individual. Bounty hunters track and destroy targets for profit.
