# Ancient & Lost Civilizations of New Eden

> *"Imagine if the bars to your prison were all you had ever known. Then one day, someone appears and unlocks the door. If they have the power to do this, then are they really the liberator? You never remembered who it was that closed you in."*
> — Ior Labron, Jove philosopher

---

## 1. The Jove

The most technologically advanced civilization in New Eden's history—and one of the only ancient races still (theoretically) extant. Their history stretches back further than any other, across **three empires** and millennia of genetic experimentation.

### The Three Jove Empires

**First Empire** (~10,000–12,000 years before CONCORD, lasted ~8,000 years)
- Rose after the early Jove enclaves resolved their internal wars.
- A golden age of cohesion, scientific achievement, and peace.
- The various transhumanist enclaves gradually converged into a unified people.

**Second Empire** (fell during the "Shrouded Days")
- Collapsed into a dark age. The exact causes are unclear—internal conflict, the early forms of the Jovian Disease, or both.
- Led to the Jove's exodus from their original home in the **Curse** region.

**Third Empire** (current, though now silent)
- Established in the far northern regions of New Eden.
- Far reduced from past glories. The Jovian Disease and subsequent withdrawal have left them a shadow of what they once were.

### The Jovian Disease

> **Confirmed canon.** The defining tragedy of the Jove.

A genetic disorder caused by millennia of extreme bioengineering. It is **non-communicable** but afflicts every Jove eventually. Two forms exist:

1. **Fetal form**: One in ten Jove fetuses die suddenly in artificial wombs. Unpredictable and unpreventable.
2. **Adult form**: Begins with apathy, loss of concentration, depression. Progresses to severe dementia, psychopathy, and finally the victim simply loses the will to live—ceasing to eat or drink until death.

Onset is variable: some Jove live centuries before symptoms appear; others decline within decades. There is **no known cure**. The Jove have tried everything—including splicing genes from other New Eden races—but nothing works. Some conspiracy theories suggest the Jove introduced capsule technology to harvest genetic data for disease research.

### Capsule Technology: The Jove's Gift

> **Confirmed canon.** ~120 years ago, the Jovian Directorate gifted the blueprints for the hydrostatic capsule to the **Ishukone Corporation**, a Caldari megacorp.

This single act reshaped New Eden. The capsule—when combined with cloning and neural scanning—granted near-immortality and thought-controlled starship command to a select few. The Age of the Capsuleer dawned in YC105 when the transneural burn scanner interface achieved 99.7% success in consciousness transfer.

**Why did the Jove give it away?** Theories abound:
- A genuine gesture to advance humanity (naive).
- A covert research program to find a cure for the Jovian Disease by studying capsuleer neural patterns.
- A calculated move to destabilize the empires by creating an ungovernable elite.

The Jove never explained their motives.

### The Battle of Vak'Atioth

> **Confirmed canon.** 23216 AD.

The Amarr Empire, starved for new slaves after centuries without major conquests, decided the Jove were weak and ripe for Reclaiming. They assembled 200 ships and invaded the system of **Vak'Atioth** (now Atioth), broadcasting imperial propaganda for weeks beforehand.

A Jove named **Grious** had obtained the Amarr battle plans from a sympathetic Amarrian admiral, **Faus Akredon**.

The battle lasted six hours. The Jove deployed frigate wings to pin the Amarrian battleships, then brought in a **Jovian Mothership**—one of three ancient vessels of unimaginable size, equipped with a doomsday-class weapon capable of destroying an Amarrian battleship in a single shot.

**Result**: Near-total Amarr defeat. The Jove lost roughly a third of their force; the Amarr lost the majority of theirs.

The defeat shattered Amarr morale and contributed directly to the **Minmatar Rebellion** shortly after. The Jove gained a reputation of invincibility that discouraged further attacks.

### The Jove Withdrawal

> **Confirmed canon.** In recent years, the Jove have effectively vanished from interstellar affairs.

- Their delegation to CONCORD stopped appearing.
- Their section of the CONCORD Assembly station sits vacant.
- Long-range sensors still detect activity in Jove space, and something still defends their borders.

Some say the Jovian Disease finally finished them. Others whisper that the **Drifters** are what remains of the Jove—heavily cybernetically augmented, no longer recognizable as the same people.

### Jove Factions (Canon & Speculation)

The task mentions "Elders, Directorate, Stasis People." Here's what the lore actually supports:

- **The Directorate**: Confirmed. The governing body of the Jove Empire. It was the Directorate that gifted capsule technology to the Caldari.
- **Modifiers**: Confirmed. A Jove subculture of individuals who constantly tinker with their own genetic code. They are more susceptible to the Jovian Disease.
- **Stasis People / Cryostasis Factions**: Partially confirmed. The Jove made extensive use of cryostasis throughout their history—both during migration and as a way to avoid societal collapse. Early enclaves combined cryostasis with virtual reality. Some Jove may still exist in suspended animation.
- **"Elders"**: Largely speculative. May refer to ancient Jove from the First Empire period, or be conflated with the **Enheduanni** (see below).

## 2. The Sleepers

> *"Imagine if the bars to your prison were all you had ever known..."*

### Anoikis & W-Space

**Confirmed canon.** The Sleepers were an ancient civilization that inhabited **Anoikis**—the wormhole-connected systems now known as W-Space. They existed thousands of years before the current empires and were vastly more advanced.

Their remaining installations are found throughout W-Space, guarded by **Sleeper Drones**—autonomous combat vessels with sophisticated AI that assesses threat levels and switches targets dynamically. These drones are not the Sleepers themselves; they are automated defenses left behind.

### Sleeper Technology & Society

From archaeological evidence:
- Masters of **virtual reality** and **neural interfacing**
- Advanced **cryotechnology**
- Built massive **Enclaves**—ring-like structures of dome habitats connected by conduits, defended by dagger-like spires
- Their architecture suggests a society that lived much of its existence in simulated environments

### The Drifter Connection

> **Confirmed canon.** The Drifters emerged from Sleeper ruins in Anoikis around YC116–117.

The Drifters appear to be the inheritors of Sleeper technology—possibly their descendants, successors, or something else entirely. They are heavily cybernetically augmented and bear a disturbing resemblance to what Jove might look like after extensive modification. They use Sleeper technology (Sleeper Thermoelectric Converters, Sleeper Multiplex Forwarders) to stabilize wormholes.

**Key question**: Are the Drifters evolved Sleepers? Evolved Jove? Something else? CCP has deliberately left this ambiguous.

### What Happened to the Sleepers?

**No confirmed answer.** The prevailing theories:
1. **Internal collapse**: Their reliance on virtual reality led to a societal atrophy—bodies died while minds remained trapped in simulation.
2. **Conflict with the Talocan**: Archaeological evidence suggests Talocan and Sleeper ruins overlap in W-Space. Biological warfare has been theorized.
3. **Ascension/transcendence**: They uploaded their consciousness and no longer need physical form.
4. **The Drifters ARE the Sleepers**: Whatever transformed them changed them beyond recognition.

### Wormhole Mechanics

Wormholes to Anoikis first appeared in known space after the **Seyllin Disaster** (YC111). Before that, Sleepers were known only from archaeological sites in the Ani Constellation (Minmatar space). The Seyllin event—some kind of stellar catastrophe involving the planet Seyllin I—ripped open spacetime and created persistent connections to W-Space.

Whether wormholes are naturally occurring or artificially created remains debated. Some theories suggest they are being intentionally generated by unseen forces.

## 3. The Talocan

### What We Know (Sparse)

**Confirmed:**
- They existed contemporaneously with the early post-EVE Gate era.
- They were a **migrant culture**, using advanced spatial manipulation and hyperspace mathematics to travel between systems.
- They inhabited W-Space before the Sleepers arrived.
- Their most famous relics are the **Black Monoliths**—mysterious structures found at Talocan sites in both known space and wormhole space. Their purpose is unknown.
- They were discovered relatively late by New Eden standards (the "Devil's Dig Site" in Caldari space revealed their existence).

### What We Guess

- **Dyson spheres?** No direct evidence, but their mastery of spatial manipulation and the Black Monoliths' unknown function lead to speculation about megastructures.
- **Master terraformers?** Unconfirmed. Their technology seems focused on space-time manipulation rather than planetary engineering.
- **Conflict with Sleepers?** Archaeological evidence suggests both civilizations occupied W-Space. Both eventually vanished. Biological warfare has been theorized as the cause.
- **Connection to wormholes?** Their hyperspace mathematics likely gave them access to wormhole systems long before anyone else.

## 4. The Yan Jung

### Barely There, But Present

> **Confirmed canon.** The Yan Jung immigrated through the EVE Gate and settled primarily in the **Deltole** system, especially on Deltole V and VI (then more habitable).

### What We Know

- Masters of **gravitronic technology** and **force field theories**
- Archaeological sites in the **Algintal Constellation** (Gallente space)
- Most recovered relics are defensive: force fields, gravitational repellers—suggesting they were preparing for invasion

### The Big Speculation: Jin-Mei Descent?

> **Unconfirmed but plausible.** Linguistic similarities between Jin-Mei and Yan Jung texts have led some researchers (notably in the Gallente Federation) to theorize that the **Jin-Mei** are descendants of the Yan Jung. No archaeological ruins directly tie the Jin-Mei to Yan Jung sites, but the linguistic evidence keeps the theory alive.

### What Destroyed Them?

**Speculation:** Archaeological evidence suggests the Yan Jung were preparing for a powerful invading force. Many researchers believe the **Sleepers** destroyed the Yan Jung civilization, given the timeline and the nature of the defensive relics.

## 5. Other Precursors

### The Terrans

> **Confirmed canon (as concept).** The Terrans are humanity's ancestors from the other side of the EVE Gate. They built the gate and sent colony ships through it.

Most ancient relics that don't belong to any known race are attributed to the Terrans. The best-known Terran artifact is the **EVE Gate** itself. Other possible Terran relics include ancient structures on Caldari Prime and the Crystal Steppe of Matar.

The only relatively intact Terran technology ever recovered was the superweapon used by **Empress Jamyl I** to destroy the Elder Fleet at the Battle of Mekhios. Reverse-engineering Terran tech is a dream of every megacorporation.

### The Enheduanni

> **Almost entirely speculative.** The Enheduanni are referenced in scattered ancient texts and player theories. Some lore discussions connect them to the Jove—or suggest they predate even the Jove. They may be a fiction-within-fiction: an ancient myth even to the ancients.

They are sometimes theorized to be:
- An even older precursor race that seeded or guided the Jove
- A Jove faction that ascended beyond physical form
- A complete myth, a cautionary tale invented by early Jove philosophers

CCP has never confirmed their existence as a real civilization.

### The Takmahl

> **Confirmed canon.** Not a true "ancient race" in the same era as the others—they're actually an offshoot of the **Amarr Empire**, exiled for heresy shortly after the Amarr achieved spaceflight.

They became masters of biological, genetic, and cybernetic engineering. After centuries of thriving independently, they mysteriously vanished, leaving only empty structures. Their most famous site is **The Labyrinth** in Amarr space, now patrolled by Blood Raiders seeking heretical texts.

### The Feythabolis Civilization

> **Confirmed canon (barely).** A progressive, egalitarian civilization that arose in the remote Feythabolis region after the EVE Gate collapse. They lacked advanced technology and were easily conquered and exterminated by the **Angel Cartel**, who now control the region. Rare artifacts survive in Angel Cartel collections.

## 6. The EVE Gate

### The Collapse

> **Confirmed canon.** The EVE Gate was a naturally occurring wormhole (or artificially stabilized one—sources differ) in the **New Eden** system. It connected to humanity's origin point (Earth/Terra). Thousands of years ago, colony ships passed through it to settle the New Eden cluster.

Then, roughly **8,000 years ago** (~7987 AD), the wormhole collapsed.

The result was catastrophic:
- Colonies were severed from their supply lines and homeworld
- Some worlds had been terraformed but couldn't sustain themselves
- **Millions perished** in the ensuing chaos
- Civilization collapsed into the **Dark Ages**
- Only a handful of colonies survived in isolated pockets, eventually giving rise to the modern empires

### The Gate Today

The EVE Gate still exists as a massive structure, located three light-years from New Eden's star. It emits lethal radiation. The **Sisters of EVE** conduct research on it, believing it to be a gift from God. Some whisper that wreckage from the final days still orbits the gate, cloaked by the Jove to prevent scavenging of advanced technology.

### What "EVE" Means

**Nobody knows.** The letters "EVE" are written across the gate. Theories range from a Terran acronym to a Jove inscription to pure coincidence. The Sisters of EVE named themselves after it.

## 7. Story Hooks: Dramatic Moments in Ancient History

### The Gift (YC ~-105)

A Jovian delegation arrives unannounced at Ishukone Corporation headquarters. They bring a data package: the complete blueprints for the hydrostatic capsule. They ask for nothing in return. They explain nothing. They leave.

Ishukone's scientists spend decades trying to make it work for baseline humans. The first pilots die or are driven mad. Stories spread that the capsule is just a "liquid-filled coffin." But eventually, with cloning technology catching up, it works. And the universe changes forever.

**Why?** The Jove never said. Every theory—from altruism to covert experimentation to deliberate destabilization—is just a guess.

---

### The Door Opens (YC111, March 10th)

The **Seyllin Disaster**. A stellar catastrophe rips apart the planet **Seyllin I**, killing hundreds of thousands. But something else happens: the event tears open stable wormholes connecting known space to Anoikis. For the first time, capsuleers can enter Sleeper space.

CreoDron drones capture the first images of a Sleeper Enclave—dome cities connected by conduits, dagger-spires standing guard. The feed cuts out ten seconds later. A capsuleer named **Lianda Burreau** is extracted and sent in. The Age of W-Space begins.

Seyllin wasn't an accident. It was the first crack in a wall that had stood for millennia.

---

### The Mothership (23216 AD)

Two hundred Amarrian battleships array themselves at Vak'Atioth, confident that God wills their victory. They have never faced a Jovian Mothership.

The Jove deploy frigates first—nimble, harassing. The Amarr laugh. Then the Mothership arrives. It is larger than anything they have ever seen. Its weapon charges. One shot. An Amarrian battleship vanishes in a flash of light.

Six hours later, the Amarr fleet is broken. The Minmatar, watching from occupied worlds, realize the Amarr can be beaten. The Rebellion begins.

The Jove didn't just win a battle. They changed the course of history for every empire in New Eden.

---

### Caroline's Star (YC116)

A new stellar anomaly appears in W-Space: **Caroline's Star**, a superluminal explosion visible across the cluster. Simultaneously, the **Thera** system—a unique nexus of wormholes—is discovered. Drifter battleships begin appearing in known space.

The Sisters of EVE lose an entire wormhole colony. CONCORD issues confused statements. And capsuleers start reporting encounters with something new: the Drifters, wielding doomsday weapons, guarding Sleeper technology, staring at the empires with black, silent eyes.

Something ancient has woken up. Or something ancient has come home.
