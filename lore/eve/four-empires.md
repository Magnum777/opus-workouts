## Amarr Empire

### Overview
The largest of the empires in terms of territory controlled. The Amarr are a theocratic, authoritarian society ruled by an Emperor and a powerful religious hierarchy. Their official doctrine, the **Reclaiming**, holds that it is their divine duty to bring all of humanity under Amarrian rule and the Amarrian faith. Slavery is not merely accepted — it is central to their social and economic structure.

### History
- **Origin:** Traces back to a single planet (Amarr Prime / Athra), unified under religious rule
- **Space Age:** Rediscovered spaceflight roughly 2,000 years ago; reverse-engineered an intact stargate in their home system
- **Expansion:** Met and conquered the primitive Ealur (21432 AD), then the Ni-Kunni (~1000 years ago), then the Minmatar (22480 AD)
- **Setbacks:** Defeated by the Jove at the Battle of Vak'Atioth (23216 AD), which triggered the Minmatar Rebellion; lost control of Minmatar space
- **Modern Era:** More peaceful stance post-Vak'Atioth, though still theocratic and slave-holding

### Government
- **Emperor:** Absolute ruler, currently from the Tash-Murkon family line (as of recent lore)
- **Royal Heirs:** Six powerful families (Ardishapur, Kador, Khanid, Kor-Azor, Sarum, Tash-Murkon) who collectively form the Succession Council
- **Chamberlain:** Handles day-to-day governance and succession matters
- **Holders:** Planetary/system-level lords who own land and slaves
- **The Theology Council:** Religious authority that interprets scripture and doctrine

### Society & Culture
- **Religion:** Monotheistic faith in a single God. The Emperor is both political and spiritual leader
- **Slavery:** Institutionalized. Slaves (primarily Minmatar, Ni-Kunni, Ealur) form the labor backbone. Manumission is possible but rare
- **Caste System:** Holders > commoners > slaves. Mobility exists but is limited
- **Khanid Kingdom:** A breakaway state founded by a disgraced Royal Heir (Khanid II), still technically Amarrian but fiercely independent. Maintains slavery but allows more martial culture

### Key Figures
- **Emperor Heideran VII:** Ruled during the Minmatar Rebellion and Caldari-Gallente War era
- **Royal Heir Ardishapur:** Known for brutality; ordered the Starkmanir genocide
- **Khanid II:** Defied succession, founded the Khanid Kingdom
- **Jamyl Sarum:** Returned from death (controversial cloning), briefly Emperor, now deceased

### Story Hooks
- A Starkmanir survivor discovered alive in the Empire (drama, political upheaval)
- A Holder's child falls in love with a slave (tragedy, forbidden romance)
- The Khanid Kingdom's secret weapons program (espionage)
- Theology Council schism over interpretation of the Reclaiming (religious civil war)

## Caldari State

### Overview
A corporate meritocracy where loyalty to the State and one's megacorporation supersedes all other affiliations. The Caldari value discipline, conformity, and collective strength over individual freedom. Their culture was forged by centuries of harsh conditions on their home planet and a devastating war with the Gallente.

### History
- **Origin:** Indigenous to Caldari Prime. Multiple "Raata" cultures merged into a unified Caldari identity
- **Gallente Contact:** Discovered by Gallente in 22463 AD; contact established 22517 AD
- **Cultural Deliverance:** Gallente sent aid to "uplift" the Caldari technologically (22631 AD). The Caldari exploited this while resenting the cultural imperialism
- **War & Exodus:** Secret colonies discovered by Gallente (23155 AD) led to the Gallente-Caldari War. Caldari Prime was occupied; the Caldari evacuated and founded an independent State
- **Capsule Era:** Gained capsule technology from the Jove, developed Titans, fought to a draw at the Battle of Iyen-Oursta
- **Modern Era:** Peace treaty signed in 23248 AD. Caldari Prime retaken in YC110 by Tibus Heth's regime

### Government
- **State Executor:** Overall head of state (traditionally from a megacorp)
- **Chief Executive Panel:** Governing council representing the "Big Eight" megacorporations:
  - Kaalakiota, SuVee (Sukuuvestaa), Nugoeihuvi, Ishukone, Wiyrkomi, Hyasyoda, CBD, Lai Dai
- **Megacorp Structure:** Citizens are employees of their corporation. Housing, healthcare, education, security — all corporate-provided
- **Caldari Providence Directorate:** Temporary military government during Heth's rule (now defunct)

### Society & Culture
- **Collectivism:** The needs of the State outweigh individual desires. Dissent is suppressed
- **Corporate Loyalty:** Citizens identify first with their megacorp, second with the State
- **Martial Tradition:** Military service is prestigious. The Caldari Navy is formidable
- **Achura:** A spiritual minority who follow a contemplative path, often clashing with the materialist mainstream
- **Deteis, Civire, Achura:** The three main bloodlines, each with cultural stereotypes (Deteis = politicians/CEOs, Civire = soldiers/workers, Achura = mystics/scientists)

### Key Figures
- **Tibus Heth:** Populist leader who seized Caldari Prime in YC110. Authoritarian, nationalist. Eventually fell from power
- **Ishukone CEO:** (various over time) Often the voice of corporate moderation
- **Kaalakiota CEO:** Typically the most aggressive, expansionist corporate voice

### Story Hooks
- Megacorp black ops against a rival corporation (corporate warfare)
- Achura monk who becomes a capsuleer (spiritual journey)
- Caldari citizen who discovers their Gallente heritage (identity crisis)
- Corporate defector with stolen technology (thriller)

## Gallente Federation

### Overview
The only true democracy among the empires, the Gallente Federation is a multicultural society built on principles of personal freedom, artistic expression, and free enterprise. At its best, it is a beacon of liberty. At its worst, it is a hypocritical cultural imperialist that imposes its values by force.

### History
- **Origin:** Gallente Prime / Luminaire. Developed spaceflight independently
- **Expansion:** Discovered Caldari (22463 AD), Intaki (22794 AD), Mannar (22809 AD)
- **Uplifting:** Brought Caldari, Intaki, and Mannar into spacefaring society
- **Federation Founded:** 23121 AD, with Gallente, Caldari, Intaki, and Mannar as founding members
- **Caldari Secession:** 23155 AD — Caldari colonies discovered, Federation tried to penalize them. Terrorist attack on Nouvelle Rouvenor killed 500,000. War began.
- **Hueromont Incident:** Caldari fleet crashed a carrier into Gallente Prime, killing 2 million
- **Modern Era:** Peace with Caldari signed 23248 AD. Tibus Heth's occupation of Caldari Prime in YC110 shattered the peace briefly

### Government
- **President:** Elected head of state. Currently (as of latest lore) from the Intaki population
- **Senate:** Legislative body representing member states
- **Supreme Court:** Judicial authority
- **Federation Navy:** Military branch

### Society & Culture
- **Democracy & Freedom:** Core values. Elections, free press, civil liberties
- **Multiculturalism:** Multiple races and bloodlines coexist. The Intaki, Mannar, and Jin-Mei are significant minority populations with political representation
- **Cultural Imperialism:** Tendency to "uplift" or force other cultures to adopt Gallente values. This backfired catastrophically with the Caldari
- **Art & Hedonism:** Strong artistic tradition. The Caille theater district is legendary
- **Dark Underbelly:** Organized crime (Serpentis connection), corruption, and inequality exist beneath the democratic facade

### Key Figures
- **President Jacus Roden:** Former arms manufacturer turned politician
- **Senator:** Various figures representing Intaki independence movements
- **Gallente Navy Admirals:** Often more moderate than their Caldari counterparts

### Story Hooks
- Intaki separatist movement (political thriller)
- Gallente journalist uncovering Federation war crimes (investigative drama)
- Caille nightlife — capsuleer on leave in the capital (noir)
- Federation Navy officer who sympathizes with Caldari (divided loyalty)

## Minmatar Republic

### Overview
A confederation of seven tribes (formerly nine) that freed themselves from Amarr slavery. The Minmatar are a fiercely independent, tribal people with a strong warrior culture and a deep cultural wound from centuries of enslavement. Their government is decentralized, and tribal identity often supersedes national identity.

### History
- **Origin:** Matar (Pator system). Developed spaceflight in 21413 AD, colonized multiple systems using ancient stargates
- **Amarr Conquest:** 22480 AD — Emperor Damius III ordered the conquest of Matar. The Minmatar Empire ceased to exist
- **Seven Hundred Years of Slavery:** Hundreds of millions of Minmatar shipped across the Amarr Empire. Starkmanir tribe annihilated in a genocide ~450 years after conquest (ordered by House Ardishapur)
- **Great Rebellion:** 23216 AD — Triggered by the Amarr defeat at the Battle of Vak'Atioth. Minmatar resistance, secretly aided by the Gallente, rose up and ejected the Amarr from their space
- **Republic Founded:** Free Minmatar consolidated their hold and established the Republic
- **Nefantar Betrayal:** The Nefantar tribe collaborated with the Amarr and fled with them, forming the core of the Ammatar Mandate
- **Starkmanir Survivors:** Later discovered alive — the genocide was not complete
- **Modern Era:** Ongoing tensions with Amarr, tribal politics, Thukker Tribe semi-autonomous in Great Wildlands

### The Seven Tribes
| Tribe | Characteristics | Status |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| **Sebiestor** | Technical, engineers, craftsmen | Republic core |
| **Brutor** | Warriors, physical strength, martial pride | Republic core |
| **Vherokior** | Shamanic traditions, mystics, healers | Republic core |
| **Krusual** | Cautious, politicians, schemers | Republic core |
| **Thukker** | Nomadic, caravaners, Great Wildlands | Semi-independent |
| **Nefantar** | Collaborators, Ammatar Mandate | Returned to Republic (later) |
| **Starkmanir** | Once-largest tribe, thought extinct | Survivors found, rejoining |

### Government
- **Sanmatar:** Head of state (title means "king of all the tribes"), currently Maleatu Shakor
- **Republic Parliament:** Legislative body with tribal representation
- **Republic Security Services:** Intelligence and internal security
- **Tribal Chiefs:** Each tribe has a chief who holds significant authority

### Society & Culture
- **Tribal Identity:** More important than Republic citizenship to many
- **Slavery's Shadow:** Every Minmatar family has a connection to slavery. Trauma is collective
- **Vherokior Shamanism:** Spiritual practices coexist with modern technology
- **Thukker Caravans:** Nomadic fleets that roam the Great Wildlands, semi-independent
- **Freedom Fighters:** Republic Fleet and resistance cells still active in Ammatar space

### Key Figures
- **Maleatu Shakor:** Sanmatar, former Valklear (elite commando), symbolic leader
- **Admiral Allsvert:** Republic Fleet, descendant of famous rebellion figures
- **Nefantar Leaders:** Complex figures — traitors to some, pragmatic survivors to others

### Story Hooks
- Starkmanir survivor discovering their heritage (identity, healing)
- Nefantar family torn between Ammatar and Republic loyalties (tragedy)
- Vherokior shaman who communes with ancestors in space (spiritual sci-fi)
- Valklear raid to free slaves from an Amarr holding (action thriller)

## Inter-Empire Relations

### The Diplomatic Map
```
Amarr Empire
  ├── Hostile: Minmatar Republic (slavery, rebellion)
  ├── Cool: Gallente Federation (cultural clash, but not active war)
  ├── Neutral: Caldari State (limited cooperation)
  └── Complex: Khanid Kingdom (breakaway but allied)

Caldari State
  ├── Hostile: Gallente Federation (historic war, occupation)
  ├── Neutral: Amarr Empire
  ├── Neutral: Minmatar Republic
  └── Internal: Megacorp rivalries

Gallente Federation
  ├── Hostile: Caldari State (historic war)
  ├── Friendly: Minmatar Republic (aided rebellion, ideological allies)
  ├── Cool: Amarr Empire (criticizes slavery)
  └── Internal: Intaki separatism

Minmatar Republic
  ├── Hostile: Amarr Empire (slavery, genocide)
  ├── Friendly: Gallente Federation (liberators, allies)
  ├── Neutral: Caldari State
  └── Internal: Tribal politics, Thukker autonomy
```

### CONCORD's Role
The CONCORD Assembly serves as a neutral diplomatic forum and peacekeeping force. All four empires are signatories to the Yulai Accords. CONCORD enforces limited rules of engagement in empire space but has no authority in nullsec.

## Key Conflicts

### The Gallente-Caldari War (23155 AD — 23248 AD)
- **Cause:** Gallente discovery of secret Caldari colonies + cultural imperialism
- **Key Event:** Caldari terrorist attack on Nouvelle Rouvenor (500,000 dead)
- **Escalation:** Gallente fascist government took power, began military operations
- **Hueromont:** Caldari carrier crashed into Gallente Prime (2 million dead)
- **Technology Race:** Fighters vs drones > Capsule technology > Titans
- **Battle of Iyen-Oursta:** Second-largest battle in history at the time. Both sides exhausted
- **Outcome:** Peace treaty in 23248 AD. Caldari Prime remained Gallente-occupied until YC110

### The Minmatar Rebellion / Great Rebellion (23216 AD)
- **Cause:** Amarr defeat at Vak'Atioth created opening
- **Trigger:** Minmatar resistance signal across occupied worlds
- **Key Battles:**
  - **Battle of Pator:** Liberation of Matar. Stunning rebel victory
  - **Battle of the Golgothan Fields:** Minmatar delayed Amarr fleet in Ennur. Heavy casualties on both sides. Second-largest battle in history until Iyen-Oursta
  - **Mahti Jailbreak:** Thukker forces freed prisoners, including future Republic leaders
  - **Battle of Fort Kavad:** Final major rebel victory. Eliminated Amarr counter-offensive capability
- **Outcome:** Amarr ejected from Minmatar space. Ammatar Mandate created as buffer. Republic established

### The Amarr-Jove War (23216 AD)
- **Cause:** Amarr chose the Jove as next target for the Reclaiming
- **Battle of Vak'Atioth:** 200 Amarr warships vs Jove. Crushing Amarr defeat. First and last time a Jove mothership was seen in combat
- **Consequence:** Amarr had to sue for peace with Jove to handle the Minmatar Rebellion
- **Significance:** Proved the Amarr were not invincible. Triggered the cascade that freed the Minmatar
