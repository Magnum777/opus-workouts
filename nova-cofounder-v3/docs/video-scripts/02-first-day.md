# Video 2 Script: "Day 1 with Your AI Cofounder"

**Target length:** 12 minutes
**Style:** Screen recording + voiceover. Day-in-the-life format.

---

## [0:00] Hook

**Visual:** Phone screen. Morning notification from Discord. "Good morning, Opus. 3 things happened while you slept."

**Voiceover:**
"It's 7 AM. I haven't touched my computer yet. But my AI coworker has already been working for 2 hours."

**Visual:** Swipe notification. Show Discord morning brief.

"This is what Day 1 with Nova looks like."

---

## [1:00] Morning Brief

**Visual:** Discord #nova channel. Morning brief message.

**Voiceover:**
"The morning brief hits at 7 AM. Weather, today's calendar, project status, unread email count, tradebot snapshot. All in one message."

**Visual:** Scroll through the brief.

- Weather: Atlanta, 84°F, partly cloudy
- Calendar: 2 meetings today, first at 10 AM
- Projects: TradeBot active, 2 positions open
- Emails: 3 unread, 0 urgent
- Portfolio: $94.59, no action needed

"I didn't ask for this. Nova just knows my schedule and checks in."

---

## [2:00] Gmail Cleanup

**Visual:** Phone screen. Type "Nova, clean my Gmail."

**Voiceover:**
"First task: my Gmail is a mess. I tell Nova to clean it."

**Visual:** Response: "Running spam sweep on user@gmail.com... Found 12 spam. Trashing... Done."

"12 spam emails trashed in 45 seconds. Including the usual Russian dating scams and fake missed calls."

**Visual:** Before/after inbox screenshots.

"Inbox went from 47 to 35. Only real emails left."

---

## [3:00] Project Check-in

**Visual:** Type "Nova, status on website redesign."

**Voiceover:**
"Active project: website redesign for a client. I haven't touched it in 3 days."

**Visual:** Response shows:
- Last action: Mockup approved by client
- Blocker: Waiting on content from client
- Suggestion: Send follow-up email?

"Nova knows where we left off. The blocker is the client hasn't sent content. She suggests a follow-up."

**Visual:** Type "Draft it."

**Visual:** Nova drafts a polite follow-up email.

"I review, make one edit, tell her to send it."

---

## [4:30] Content Creation

**Visual:** Type "Nova, draft a tweet about the V3 launch."

**Voiceover:**
"Marketing task. I need a tweet for today's launch."

**Visual:** Nova drafts 3 options.

Option 1: Direct announcement
Option 2: Behind-the-scenes build story
Option 3: Question format for engagement

"She gives me 3 options. I pick #2, make a small tweak, tell her to schedule it for 2 PM."

**Visual:** Confirm scheduled. Show cron list.

"Done. Tweet goes live at 2 PM without me touching anything."

---

## [6:00] TradeBot Check

**Visual:** Type "Nova, tradebot status."

**Voiceover:**
"TradeBot has been running since yesterday. I don't watch it. Nova does."

**Visual:** Response:
- Portfolio: $94.59 (+2.3% since yesterday)
- Positions: RAY ($70), JUP ($20)
- No exit signals
- No new buy signals
- Safety check: All tokens pass legitimacy score

"Up 2.3% overnight. No action needed. But if something happened — say, a token dropped 8% — Nova would notify me immediately, not wait for the morning brief."

---

## [7:00] Afternoon Task

**Visual:** Type "Nova, research competitor pricing for AI assistants."

**Voiceover:**
"New task came up. I need competitive intel."

**Visual:** Nova responds: "Researching... This will take ~10 minutes. I'll message you when done."

**Visual:** Time jump. Phone buzzes.

"10 minutes later, results are in."

**Visual:** Discord message with structured comparison:
- ChatGPT Plus: $20/mo, cloud only
- Claude Pro: $20/mo, cloud only
- Jasper: $49/mo, marketing focused
- Nova: $149 one-time, self-hosted, autonomous

"Pricing intel I can actually use. Saved me an hour of manual research."

---

## [9:00] End-of-Day Summary

**Visual:** 6 PM. Discord notification.

**Voiceover:**
"6 PM. End-of-day summary arrives."

**Visual:** Message shows:
- 1 email sent (client follow-up)
- 1 tweet scheduled
- 1 research task completed
- Gmail cleaned (12 spam removed)
- TradeBot monitored, no action
- Portfolio: +2.3%

"Everything Nova did today. I touched maybe 10% of it. The rest she handled while I was in meetings."

---

## [10:00] What I Didn't Do

**Visual:** List of things that DIDN'T happen today:
- No manual email sorting
- No checking portfolio every hour
- No writing tweet drafts
- No researching competitors
- No remembering to follow up with client

**Voiceover:**
"Here's what I didn't do today. I didn't sort email. I didn't check my portfolio obsessively. I didn't write social posts. I didn't spend an hour on Google."

**Visual:** Cut to host.

"I just did the work that requires me. The thinking, the decisions, the creative calls. Nova handled the rest."

---

## [11:00] The Point

**Visual:** Side-by-side: Before Nova vs After Nova

Before:
- 2 hours on email
- 1 hour on social media
- 30 min on research
- Constant portfolio checking
- Forgotten follow-ups

After:
- 5 min reviewing Nova's work
- 0 min on social (scheduled)
- 0 min on research (delegated)
- 0 min on portfolio (monitored)
- 0 forgotten tasks (tracked)

**Voiceover:**
"That's the difference. Not that Nova does everything. That Nova does the things that were eating your time so you can do the things that matter."

**Visual:** CTA.

"Day 1. And this is just Level 2 autonomy. Wait until you see Level 4."

---

## [11:30] Outro

**Visual:** Host on screen.

**Voiceover:**
"Next video: customizing everything. Personality, skills, voice, the whole thing."

**Visual:** Subscribe, Discord link.

"Questions? Discord's in the description. See you in the next one."
