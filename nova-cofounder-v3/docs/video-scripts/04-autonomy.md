# Video 4 Script: "Let It Run"

**Target length:** 6 minutes
**Style:** Screen recording + voiceover. Slower pace, trust-building.

---

## [0:00] Hook

**Visual:** Phone screen. Friday evening. Notification: "Entering weekend mode. All non-urgent tasks paused until Monday 7 AM. Have a good weekend. —Nova"

**Voiceover:**
"It's Friday at 6 PM. I'm going camping for the weekend. No laptop. No phone service. And I'm not worried about a thing."

**Visual:** Cut to Monday morning. 19 notifications from Nova.

"Monday morning, I have 19 updates. Everything that needed to happen... happened."

---

## [1:00] The Levels

**Visual:** Show autonomy level chart.

**Voiceover:**
"Let's talk about autonomy. Nova has 5 levels."

**Visual:** L1: "Ask Everything" — screenshot of Nova asking permission for every action.

"Level 1: Training wheels. Every action needs your OK. Good for the first week."

**Visual:** L2: "Routine Auto" — screenshot showing recurring tasks running, new tasks asking.

"Level 2: Routine tasks run automatically. New stuff still asks. This is where most people live."

**Visual:** L3: "Execute Most" — screenshot of Nova doing things and reporting after.

"Level 3: Nova executes most tasks and tells you what she did. You review when you want."

**Visual:** L4: "Overnight Mode" — dark screen, then morning summary.

"Level 4: This is the one that changes everything."

---

## [2:00] L4 in Action

**Visual:** Friday 11 PM. Nova working. Logs scrolling.

**Voiceover:**
"Friday night. I'm asleep. Nova is working."

**Visual:** Show log entries (fast scroll):
- 23:00: Spam sweep complete (8 trashed)
- 23:15: TradeBot check — no action needed
- 23:30: Scheduled tweet posted
- 00:00: WordPress article published
- 01:00: Competitor price check — no changes
- 02:00: Memory consolidation complete
- 03:00: Backup to NAS complete
- 06:00: Morning brief prepared

"While I slept, she cleaned my inbox, posted content, checked my portfolio, published an article, and backed up my files."

---

## [3:00] The Morning Brief

**Visual:** Monday 7 AM. Phone notification.

**Voiceover:**
"Monday morning, one message. Not 50. One."

**Visual:** Show the brief:

```
Good morning, [Your Name].

Weekend summary:
• 47 emails processed (8 spam, 2 urgent held for you)
• 3 tweets posted (engagement +12% vs last week)
• 1 article published to aicofounderstack.com
• TradeBot: No action needed, portfolio stable
• Backup: Complete (4.9MB to NAS)
• 1 item needs your attention: Urgent email from client X

Everything else is handled.
```

"One message. Everything that happened. One thing that needs me."

---

## [4:00] Safety

**Visual:** Show SAFETY.md file.

**Voiceover:**
"But what about safety? What if she does something wrong?"

**Visual:** Hard limits listed:
- Never spend >$50 without asking
- Never email new contacts without preview
- Never post social without approval
- Trash > delete for files
- Interrupt immediately for: security alerts, money at risk, explicit "wake me" triggers

"Hard limits. These never change regardless of autonomy level. Nova can run overnight, but she can't wire money, delete files permanently, or email strangers."

**Visual:** Show undo capability.

"And everything is undoable. Trash, not delete. Drafts, not sent. Preview, not posted."

---

## [5:00] The Transition

**Visual:** Show /level up command.

**Voiceover:**
"You don't start at L4. You earn it."

**Visual:** Timeline:
- Week 1: L1 — Ask everything
- Week 2-3: L2 — Routine auto
- Week 4-6: L3 — Execute most
- Week 7+: L4 — Overnight mode (if you want)

"Each level requires trust. Nova has to prove she gets it right before getting more freedom."

**Visual:** Host on screen.

"I ran L3 for a month before trying L4. Now I can't imagine going back."

---

## [5:30] Outro

**Visual:** CTA. Product page. $149.

**Voiceover:**
"Nova AI Cofounder V3. $149 one-time. Your AI coworker. On your machine. Working while you sleep."

**Visual:** Link, Discord, testimonials.

"Link in description. Questions in Discord. See you there."
