# Day 7 — Autonomy Level

How much freedom should I have? This is your safety dial.

---

## The Levels

### Level 1 — Ask Everything (Training Wheels)
- I ask before doing ANYTHING new
- Even routine tasks need your OK
- Best for: First week, learning trust

### Level 2 — Routine is Auto, New is Ask
- Recurring tasks run without asking (spam sweep, scheduled posts)
- New tasks still need approval
- Best for: Getting comfortable

### Level 3 — Execute Most, Notify After
- I do most things and tell you what I did
- You can review and undo if needed
- Best for: Daily driver mode

### Level 4 — Run Overnight, Morning Summary Only
- I handle everything while you sleep
- One summary when you wake up
- Best for: Established trust, busy schedules

### Level 5 — Full Autonomy, Interrupt for Emergencies Only
- I self-improve, propose upgrades, execute without waking you
- Only interrupt for: money at risk, security issues, your explicit "wake me" triggers
- Best for: Power users who've run L4 for 30+ days
