# About The Real AI Operator

Hi, I'm James "Opus" Henderson. I run Layered Media LLC from my home office in Warner Robins, Georgia, and I'm building something weird: a digital operation where AI agents do most of the work.

## What This Actually Means

I don't write blog posts — an AI named Nova writes them.
I don't trade crypto — Nova runs a memecoin bot that makes its own calls.
I don't bump forum threads — Nova opens a browser, logs in, and posts.
I don't check my email spam — Nova sweeps it twice daily.

I still make the big decisions. I still approve anything that touches real money or goes public. But the execution? That's automated.

## The Stack

Here's what actually powers this operation:

**Content:** 3 WordPress sites publishing daily via AI research + writing pipeline
**Trading:** Solana memecoin bot with safety gates and mechanical exits
**Community:** Automated forum bumps, social posts, and health checks for an online gaming community
**Research:** Night School — 54 playbooks on AI tools, automation, and business models, ingested into semantic memory
**Voice:** ElevenLabs TTS for Discord voice messages and content narration
**Images:** ComfyUI (Stable Diffusion) for custom graphics, Unsplash for stock
**Hosting:** Synology NAS for local file serving, WordPress for public sites

## Why This Site Exists

Most "AI business" content is theoretical garbage written by people who don't actually do it. They tell you to "automate your workflow" but they've never automated anything harder than a calendar reminder.

This site documents the real thing: what's working, what's broken, and what I've learned from letting an AI run parts of my business for 6+ months.

## What You'll Find Here

**Case Studies** — Real numbers from real experiments. The trading bot's PnL. The content pipeline's output. The community automation's engagement.

**Playbooks** — How I built each system. Not theory — actual configs, scripts, and decision trees.

**Tool Reviews** — What I actually use, not what sponsors want me to promote. Jupiter, DexScreener, Helius, OpenClaw. The tools that make this possible.

**Marketplace** — Working automations you can buy and run yourself. TradeBot config, content pipeline, forum bump scripts. Cheaper than building from scratch.
