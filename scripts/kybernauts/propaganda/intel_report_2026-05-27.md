## SECTION 2: PROPAGANDA TACTICS FROM EVE HISTORY

### Tactic #1: The Ministry of Truth (MiniTru) Model
Goonswarm's Ministry of Truth is the gold standard. Key lessons:
- **Cultural glue > pure propaganda** — songs, memes, inside jokes bind members
- **Give purpose to sacrifice** — "Every Ship Counts" poster made new players feel valuable
- **Frame losses as strategic, wins as decisive** — never admit defeat, always reframe
- **Multiple formats** — posters, videos, songs, memes, live speeches
- **Speed matters** — get content out within hours of a battle

### Tactic #2: Narrative War (from INN Guide)
Source: https://imperium.news/how-to-win-a-narrative-war-a-guide/
1. **Grab a megaphone** — post everywhere (r/EVE, forums, Twitter)
2. **Tell a good story** — truth is optional; frame matters more than facts
3. **Human perception is social, not rational** — people believe what their group believes
4. **We can't fact-check everything** — the first narrative to stick wins
5. **Emotional resonance > factual accuracy** — Churchill/Patton speeches work because they're emotional, not because they're precise

### Tactic #3: The State of the Goonion
The Mittani's live speeches are legendary:
- Delivered in voice, creates urgency and belonging
- Mix of humor, aggression, and "we're in this together"
- Calls to action with specific fleet times and doctrines
- Transcripts posted afterward for those who missed it
- **Adaptation**: Record "State of the Clade" addresses

### Tactic #4: World War Bee Propaganda
From the famous battle where MBC declared victory but the Imperium kept fighting:
- **Declare victory early** — even if you're losing, claim you're winning
- **Control the timeline** — MBC said war was over; Imperium proved it wasn't
- **Use third-party media** — get articles written about your conflict
- **Meme warfare** — simple image macros spread further than long posts

### Tactic #5: The "Little Bees" Effect
Song parodies that become anthems:
- Cover of "Let It Be" rewritten as "Little Bees" about Goonswarm
- Became cultural glue — new players felt welcomed
- **Adaptation**: Kybernauts anthem using Triglavian themes
