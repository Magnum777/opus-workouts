**Q: Is my business data safe?**

A: V2 includes Prompt Guard — prompt-injection resistant, data isolation standard. V3's Airlock architecture is next-level: separate environments per project, military-grade isolation. Your data doesn't leak. Your prompts don't get trained on. Your business stays yours.

**Q: What does "voice output" actually mean?**

A: Edge-TTS gives Nova a voice. Morning briefing? "Here's what you have today." Call prep? "Here's everything about the client — read it out loud before the call." Voice isn't a gimmick — it's the difference between reading something and internalizing it.

**Q: What is "LanceDB memory"?**

A: Standard AI memory is session-based. It forgets. LanceDB is vector-based — it indexes everything you've shared, and lets you search across it. "What did we decide about the pricing page in March?" You ask. Nova finds it. Instantly.

**Q: What's Skippy's Academy?**

A: It's an onboarding system built into V2. You train Nova on your business, your voice, your preferences, your clients — once. That training persists across every session. You don't re-explain yourself every Monday morning.
