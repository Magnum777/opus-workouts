## Introduction

The old narrative was: it takes money to make money. You needed capital to hire people, buy software, and build systems.

The new narrative is: you need under $100 a month and a willingness to learn.

The AI tools available in 2026 have made it possible to run a legitimate business operation — website, email marketing, CRM, content creation, design, automation, and customer support — without a full-time team or enterprise software budget.

This guide breaks down exactly what to use, what it costs, and how it all connects.

---

## The Foundation: Website & Hosting

### Tool: WordPress.com (or Webflow with AI features)
**Cost:** $10–15/month

Your website is your digital home. WordPress.com handles hosting, security, and updates — you handle content and design. The built-in AI features help write and optimize pages without needing a developer.

If you want something more design-forward, Webflow starts at $14/month and has solid AI-assisted design tools.

**What you get:** Professional website, SSL security, reliable uptime, no technical maintenance.

---

## Email Marketing: Your Revenue Engine

### Tool: Mailchimp (Free tier) or ConvertKit ($9/month starter)
**Cost:** $0–9/month

Email is still the highest-ROI marketing channel for most businesses. The data is yours, the relationship is yours, and you control the communication.

**Mailchimp (free up to 500 contacts):** Good for basic newsletters, automations, and simple campaigns.

**ConvertKit ($9/month):** Better for creators and businesses focused on building subscriber relationships and selling digital products.

**What you get:** Automated welcome sequences, sales funnels, broadcast emails, and a owned audience you control.

---

## CRM: Managing Customers Without Spreadsheets

### Tool: HubSpot CRM (Free) or Pipedrive ($12/month)
**Cost:** $0–12/month

A CRM keeps track of every customer interaction, deal, and pipeline stage. Without one, you're managing relationships in your head — which breaks the moment you have more than 5 clients.

**HubSpot CRM (free):** Solid pipeline management, contact tracking, and basic automation. Good if you want a robust free tier.

**Pipedrive ($12/month):** More sales-focused. Better UI, stronger pipeline visualization. Worth the $12 if you're actively selling services.

**What you get:** No more lost leads, forgotten follow-ups, or "I don't remember what we agreed on" moments.

---

## Content Creation: Writing, Design, Video

### Tool Set: Claude (Free/$20 Pro) + Canva ($13/month)
**Cost:** $0–33/month

This is where AI does the heavy lifting.

**Claude (free tier):** Write all your content — blog posts, emails, social captions, newsletter drafts, pitch documents. The free tier is generous enough for most solopreneurs.

**Canva Pro ($13/month):** Replace a graphic designer for social graphics, presentations, email headers, and visual content. The AI design features built in mean you can create professional visuals in minutes without design skills.

**What you get:** A complete content engine that produces more in a month than most agencies produced a decade ago.

---

## Automation: Making Systems Run Themselves

### Tool: Make.com (formerly Integromat)
**Cost:** $9/month+ (free tier available)

This is the connective tissue of your stack. Make.com connects your tools so things happen automatically without you doing the same task twice.

**Examples of automations:**
- New form submission → Create CRM contact → Send welcome email → Add to email sequence
- New blog post → Share to Twitter, LinkedIn, and Facebook simultaneously
- New client signed → Generate invoice → Send to accounting tool → Schedule kickoff call
- Form submission → Route to right person based on answers

**What you get:** Systems that run while you sleep. The business keeps moving even when you're not working.

---

## Customer Support: Available 24/7

### Tool: Intercom (Free trial) or Zendesk ($19/month starter)
**Cost:** $0–19/month

You can't be online 24/7, but your business can. AI-powered support tools handle common questions, route urgent issues to you, and give every new lead a fast response.

**Intercom:** Modern UI, powerful AI resolver, good for businesses with higher support volume.

**Zendesk:** More enterprise-grade, stronger knowledge base features, better for businesses scaling support.

**What you get:** Instant response times, reduced churn from slow replies, and you stop losing sleep over unanswered messages.

---

## Research & Information: Staying Informed Without Reading Everything

### Tool: Perplexity AI (Free)
**Cost:** $0

Perplexity replaces hours of manual research. Ask questions, get synthesized answers with sources, and move on with confidence.

Use it for:
- Market research before launching products
- Competitive analysis
- Validating business ideas
- Due diligence on potential partners

**What you get:** Hours saved every week. Decisions made faster with better information.

---

## Scheduling: Protecting Your Time

### Tool: Calendly (Free tier) or Clockwise ($8/user/month)
**Cost:** $0–8/month

Stop the email tag about scheduling calls. Calendly or Clockwise let people book time on your calendar based on your availability — no back-and-forth required.

**What you get:** No more scheduling logistics. Booked calls without the timezone math.

## How It All Connects: The Automation Flow

The power isn't in any single tool — it's in how they work together.

**Example flow — New client from website:**

1. Prospect fills out contact form on your WordPress site
2. Make.com detects the submission and triggers the automation
3. Claude generates a personalized follow-up email based on their form answers
4. HubSpot creates a new contact and logs the lead
5. Mailchimp adds them to your newsletter sequence
6. Calendly sends a link to book a discovery call
7. Intercom sends an automated response thanking them and giving expected response times

This entire flow happens in under 2 minutes with zero manual work.

## Scaling This Stack

When you're ready to add capacity, here's the upgrade path:

**First hire (when you hit $5K/month):** A part-time VA to handle tasks AI can't — stakeholder communication, complex negotiations, strategic planning.

**Second hire (when you hit $15K/month):** A specialist (designer, developer, or marketer) for the work that needs human judgment and creativity.

The AI stack handles the foundation. You build the house.
