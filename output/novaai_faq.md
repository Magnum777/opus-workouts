## The Basics

### What is Nova AI?

Nova AI is a persistent, memory-equipped AI cofounder for indie hackers, solo founders, and small agencies. It's not a chatbot — it's a system that remembers who you are, what you're building, and what needs to get done — across every session.

Think of it as: the operational backbone you didn't know you were missing.

---

### How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?

Those tools answer questions. They forget everything when your conversation ends.

Nova remembers. Every conversation starts from a foundation — your business, your voice, your clients, your decisions. You don't repeat yourself. You don't re-explain context. You don't start from zero.

More importantly: Nova tracks your projects, not just your conversations. It's operational, not just conversational.

---

### Do I have to train it every time?

No. That's the whole point.

V1 has persistent memory — you don't re-explain yourself every session.
V2's Skippy's Academy means you train Nova on your business once, and it sticks. Future sessions pick up where you left off.
V3's autonomy mode means it takes initiative — you set direction, Nova executes.

---

### What does "voice output" actually mean?

Edge-TTS gives Nova a voice. Not a gimmick — a practical tool.

- Morning briefings: "Here's what you have today — 3 calls, 2 deadlines, 1 thing you forgot."
- Call prep: "Your client call is in 30 minutes. Here's their file, your last note, and what you discussed last time."
- Draft review: Read it out loud, catch what you'd miss reading silently.

Voice turns information into something you actually use, not just something you see.

## Technical & Security

### Is my business data safe?

V2 includes Prompt Guard — prompt-injection resistant, data isolation standard. Your inputs don't get trained on. Your business logic doesn't leak.

V3's Airlock architecture is next level: separate environments per project, military-grade isolation. Your client data, proprietary research, and competitive intelligence stay siloed. Nothing cross-contaminates.

General AI tools (free tier, mostly) train on your inputs unless you explicitly opt out and know how to find the setting. Nova doesn't train on your data. Ever.

---

### What is "LanceDB memory"?

Standard AI memory is session-based — it forgets the moment your conversation ends.

LanceDB is vector-based. It indexes everything you've shared — files, decisions, notes, conversations — and lets you search across it naturally.

"what did we decide about the pricing page in march?"

You ask. Nova finds it. Instantly.

---

### What is "Prompt Guard"?

Prompt Guard is a security layer that protects against prompt injection attacks — where malicious inputs try to manipulate your AI's behavior, extract data, or override instructions.

In V2 and V3, Prompt Guard is built in. Your Nova doesn't get tricked. Your data doesn't leak through injection vectors.

---

### What is "Airlock security"?

Airlock is V3's military-grade data isolation architecture. Each project runs in a separate environment. Nothing cross-contaminates — not data, not context, not prompts.

If you're handling sensitive client data, proprietary research, or competitive intelligence, Airlock ensures your AI doesn't accidentally share what it shouldn't.
