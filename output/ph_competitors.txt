Competitors fall into two camps: crypto influencers selling $500 courses with zero code, and GitHub repos with no documentation that assume you already know everything. The influencers skip the technical reality. The repos skip the human reality. Neither tells you what to do when your bot buys a token that turns out to be a honeypot, or how to handle the emotional side of automated trading. What I understand: the real challenge isn't writing the buy function. It's writing the safety gate that stops you from buying garbage. It's the 3am debugging session when Jupiter changes their API response format. It's knowing when to turn the bot off because the market shifted. I documented the failures, not just the wins. That's the gap.
