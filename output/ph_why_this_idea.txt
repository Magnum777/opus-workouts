I watched too many developers get stuck in tutorial hell. They want to build real things in crypto but end up copying snippets from Medium posts that don't explain the hard parts: what happens when an API times out mid-trade, how to tell if a token is a honeypot before you buy, how to sleep at night while your bot holds positions. I built this because I needed it myself. The $92 portfolio was my proving ground. Every chapter is something I wished existed when I started. If this helps even ten people ship something real instead of watching charts, it was worth writing.
