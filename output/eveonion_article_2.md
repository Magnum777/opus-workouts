# Fenris Creations Unveils Four Military Campaigns With Stakes Ranging From 'Reclaim Ancestral Homeland' To 'Slightly Shorter Commute'

**NEW EDEN** — Developer Fenris Creations revealed details this week for the Military Campaigns system coming in the Cradle of War expansion, and the EVE community is struggling to reconcile the wildly divergent stakes of the four faction-aligned efforts, which range from liberating systems stolen centuries ago in brutal conquest to, in one case, "reducing the jumps between Dodixie and Intaki from ten to six."

The four campaigns, each aligned with one of the great empires, will unfold over several months and allow capsuleers to contribute through mining, manufacturing, combat, hacking, and factional warfare. Success or failure will leave permanent marks on New Eden — though the definition of "permanent marks" appears to vary considerably by faction.

The Minmatar Republic's campaign, "The Starkmanir Restoration," aims to reclaim the systems of Arzad and Ezzara, which were seized by the Amarr Empire "many centuries ago, at tremendous cost in blood and dignity," according to Republic officials. The campaign's secondary objective involves recovering "ancient knowledge" that could reveal Phased Fields throughout Minmatar highsec.

Meanwhile, the Gallente Federation's "Operation Rogue Edge" seeks to master Rogue Drone technologies, strengthen military logistics, and — in what one Gallente admiral described as "arguably the most critical strategic objective since the capsuleer era began" — cut the Dodixie-to-Intaki route from ten jumps to six.

"It's about quality of life," said Federation Admiral Celeste Dufort, speaking at a press conference aboard a Nyx-class supercarrier. "Our pilots deserve a commute that doesn't involve ten jumps through lowsec where they might have to, you know, pay attention. Six jumps. That's the dream. That's what we're fighting for."

"And the Rogue Drone thing, too," she added. "That's also important."

The Caldari State's "State Corridor Initiative" aims to build a new stargate connection from Black Rise to Syndicate, which officials described as a vital strategic corridor while privately acknowledging it would also make it significantly easier for Caldari traders to avoid the Jita gate queue during peak hours.

"Strengthening our sphere of influence is paramount," said a Megacorp spokesperson who requested anonymity because his talking points hadn't been approved by four separate corporate boards yet. "Bringing the Intaki Syndicate to heel, establishing direct routes through contested space — these are the things that define an empire's legacy. Also, I have a shipment of Navy Ravens that's been stuck in Perimeter for three days. Have you seen the traffic there? It's inhuman."

The Amarr Empire's campaign, "The Blessed Exchange," proposes to establish the Deaconry of Economic Oversight Office at Amarr VIII, promising to "significantly reduce taxes and brokerage fees" at the station if successful. The theological implications of this objective — essentially, the divine right of God's chosen empire to offer a limited-time discount on market fees — have not been lost on Amarr theologians.

"We have prayed on this matter extensively," said Deacon Marius Kor-Azur of the Theology Council. "The Emperor's will is clear. Our holy mission is to leverage the chaos of interstellar war to secure a modest financial incentive for capsuleers who trade at Amarr VIII. Some may call this profane. We call it a loyalty program with competitive rates."

When reached for comment about the apparent stakes gap, CONCORD spokesperson Irhes Angireh emphasized that "every campaign objective reflects the unique cultural values of its sponsoring empire."

"For the Minmatar, it's liberation," Angireh explained. "For the Caldari, it's expansion. For the Amarr, it's divine economic optimization. And for the Gallente... well, honestly, have you ever done the Dodixie-to-Intaki run? It's ten jumps. Ten. No wonder they're willing to master forbidden Rogue Drone technology just to shave four off. I'd consider treason for a five-jump route to Amarr, personally."

Capsuleer reaction has been predictably mixed. Minmatar pilots have been flocking to the Starkmanir Restoration in droves, while Gallente Factional Warfare veterans report being "genuinely unsure if we're supposed to feel patriotic about better commute times."

"I'll still do the campaign," said Federation pilot Jenna Soleau, reached in her Vexor Navy Issue outside Dodixie. "I mean, it's ISK and LP either way. But when I'm flying past the wreckage of a Minmatar fleet that died liberating their ancestors' homeworld, and I'm doing it so I can get to Intaki six jumps faster... I don't know. It hits different, you know?"

At press time, sources confirmed that the Gallente Federation had submitted an eleventh-hour revision to their campaign objectives, adding "develop a new low-waste variant of the Excavator drone" to the list. "See?" Admiral Dufort said. "We're not just about commutes. We're about drones too. Rogue ones. With less waste. It's very serious military science. Please stop comparing us to the Minmatar."

The Cradle of War expansion launches June 9.
