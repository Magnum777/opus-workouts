# Launch Day Timeline — "How to Build a Solana Memecoin Trading Bot"

## Pre-Launch: 7 Days Before

### Day -7 (Create urgency)
- Post a teaser on X/Twitter: "Working on something for devs who want to automate Solana trading. Will share soon."
- No reveal of price or product name yet — just intrigue

### Day -5 (Build waitlist)
- Publish a landing page with email capture (Ghost, Carrd, or Gumroad pre-launch page)
- Post second tease: "Almost ready. Here's what the setup looks like..." — screenshot of terminal or code
- Share in crypto dev Discord servers, relevant subreddits (r/solana, r/cryptomarkets, r/SideProject)
- Collect emails — this is your launch fuel

### Day -3 (Social proof)
- Share a snippet from the guide — something concrete and useful (e.g., how the Jupiter price fetch works)
- Engage with replies to build familiarity with your voice

### Day -1 (Final push)
- Email waitlist: "Tomorrow I'm launching something. If you want early access or to be notified when it goes live — this is the moment."
- Post final countdown on X: "Launching tomorrow. ~6,000 words. Real code. No filler."

## Launch Day

### Timing
- **Post at 6:00 AM PST / 9:00 AM EST** — Product Hunt traffic peaks mid-morning EST
- This gives European makers the evening to engage and US makers the day to upvote

### Launch Day Rules
- **Respond to every comment within 1 hour** — PH algorithm rewards engagement
- **Don't beg for upvotes** — ask genuine questions, spark conversation
- **Own your price** — don't apologize for charging, it's a value deliverable
- **Stay human** — this is a maker product, not a corporate launch

## Post-Launch: 7 Days After

### Day +1
- Thank everyone who upvoted and commented personally
- Post a "what's inside" breakdown on X — a thread of 5–8 slides
- Monitor comments on PH — answer every single one

### Day +2
- If PH didn't convert well, don't panic — this is normal
- Repurpose: turn the PH description into a Dev.to article
- Post the article in relevant communities

### Day +3
- A/B test your launch post title if you have a v2 to try
- Analyze: how many emails captured? What percentage converted?

### Day +4–5
- Follow up with everyone who emailed you questions but didn't buy
- Address objections directly — if price is the issue, consider a payment plan mention

### Day +6–7
- Send a "launch wrap-up" email to the full list: what happened, what you learned, next steps
- Write a retrospective post about what went right/wrong for future launches
