# Finance Heartbeat Tasks

## Weekly Checks (rotate)
- [ ] Credit utilization across all cards (target: <10% per card, <30% aggregate)
- [ ] Annual fee anniversary dates — any coming up in next 30 days?
- [ ] Statement due dates — anything due soon?
- [ ] Welcome bonus progress — any spending requirements at risk?
- [ ] Unused credits audit — are we leaving money on the table?

## Monthly Checks
- [ ] Full fee justification report (AF paid vs. value received)
- [ ] Spending categorization from CSV exports
- [ ] New card application opportunity scan (elevated bonuses, new products)
- [ ] Credit score check (via free service, not hard pull)

## Quarterly Checks
- [ ] Full ecosystem review — is our card mix still optimal?
- [ ] Downgrade/upgrade analysis for fee-heavy cards
- [ ] Authorized user audit — who has access to what?
- [ ] Business vs. personal spending separation review

## Annual Checks
- [ ] Full card portfolio audit — keep, downgrade, or close?
- [ ] Tax prep — business expenses by card, deductions summary
- [ ] Insurance/benefit review — trip protections, purchase protections
- [ ] Retention offers — call in for offers before paying AF
