# Anti-Yagas Propaganda Campaign — Phased Escalation Plan

## Strategy Overview
Start vague and neutral, build credibility as an observer, then gradually introduce data, name targets, and escalate to direct confrontation. This avoids looking like a sudden coordinated attack and gives the community time to absorb each layer before the next drops.

## Escalation Controls

### If Yagas Ignores Us
- That's actually good — means we're not threatening enough yet, OR they don't care
- Response: Double down on data posts. Make it impossible to ignore. Reddit posts with solid data get upvoted regardless of target response.
- Eventually they'll have to respond or look like they're admitting guilt by silence

### If Yagas Responds Aggressively
- Perfect. That's engagement. Screenshot their responses. Mock calmly with more data.
- "Yagas says X. Here's what zKill says." Let the data speak.
- Never get emotional. Always return to evidence.

### If Yagas Responds Calmly/Defensively
- Harder to counter. They might co-opt the narrative.
- Response: Acknowledge their point, then add more data. "You say you're just playing the game. Fair. But here's what the game design says Pochven was supposed to be."

### If Other Corps Join In
- Excellent. Amplify them. Retweet/reply to supportive posts.
- "Kybernauts aren't the only ones noticing."

### If We Get Pushback from r/eve
- Some EVE players hate all propaganda. Expect downvotes on anything that looks like a campaign.
- Response: Lean harder into evidence. Data-driven posts perform better than overtly emotional ones.
