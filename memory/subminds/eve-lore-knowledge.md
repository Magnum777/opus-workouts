### The Jove Empire
**Researched:** 2026-06-06

**Summary:** The Jove Empire is the oldest surviving civilization in New Eden, steeped in mystery and technological superiority that far outmatches the four playable empires. The Jove's ancestors were among the last humans to pass through the EVE Gate, arriving in basic cryoships and establishing colonies in the Utopia system of the Heaven constellation. Over millennia, their various enclaves experimented heavily with genetic engineering, cybernetics, and psychological conditioning, eventually coalescing into the First Jove Empire — a remarkably stable and peaceful society that lasted roughly 8,000 years. However, their extreme genetic tampering introduced the Jovian Disease, a fatal psychological disorder that afflicts every Jove eventually. The disease manifests as a progressive loss of will to live, advancing through depression and dementia to total catatonia and death. It also kills one in ten Jove fetuses in artificial wombs. The Jove tried everything to cure it, even creating three massive Jovian Motherships to relocate their population, leaving the sick behind — but the disease followed them.

The most famous military engagement involving the Jove was the Amarr-Jove War (23216 AD). The Amarr Empire, believing the Jove weakened by their disease, broadcast their invasion plans for weeks and expected an easy conquest. Instead, at the Battle of Vak'Atioth, a single Jovian mothership annihilated the entire Amarr invasion force, demonstrating the sheer gap in technology. The Amarr planned a second, larger invasion but were forced to sue for peace when the Minmatar Rebellion erupted in their own territory. The Jove agreed to peace, and the lopsided victory became a deterrent against any future invasion.

The Jove's most consequential gift to New Eden was capsule technology, which they introduced to the Caldari State. This single innovation created the capsuleer — every player in EVE Online — and reshaped the balance of power across the cluster. In recent times, the Jove have completely withdrawn from interstellar affairs. Their Directorate vacated their CONCORD Inner Circle seat years before formally withdrawing in YC118, designating the Society of Conscious Thought as their successor state. Jovian corporations like Genolution and Impro are now staffed entirely by non-Jove employees. While rumors persist that the Jovian Disease has wiped them out entirely, long-range sensors still detect activity in Jove space. The Drifters, who emerged from the ruins of Sleeper civilization in Anoikis, bear a striking resemblance to heavily cybernetically augmented Jove — leading to speculation that they are what remains of the Jove who refused to die quietly.

**Key Figures:**
- The Jovian Directorate — ruling leadership, completely withdrawn from CONCORD
- Jovian Modifiers — Jove who constantly tinker with their own genetic code, accelerating disease onset
- Ior Labron — Jovian founder of the Society of Conscious Thought (SoCT)
- The Drifters — possibly Jove survivors transformed through extreme cybernetic augmentation

**Key Events:**
- ~20,000+ years ago — Jove ancestors arrive in New Eden after EVE Gate collapse, settle Heaven constellation
- ~12,000-10,000 years before CONCORD — Early Jove enclaves war among themselves
- ~10,000-2,000 years before CONCORD — First Jove Empire (8,000 years of peace and prosperity)
- Shrouded Days — period of extreme genetic manipulation that triggers the Jovian Disease
- 23216 AD — Amarr-Jove War: Jove mothership annihilates Amarr invasion at Vak'Atioth
- Unknown date — Jove introduce capsule technology to the Caldari
- Recent decades — Jove progressively withdraw from cluster affairs
- YC118 — Jovian Directorate formally withdraws from CONCORD Inner Circle, SoCT designated successor

**Why It Matters:** The Jove represent the ultimate precursor mystery in EVE lore — more advanced than any current civilization, ravaged by their own hubris (genetic engineering), and vanished with no clear explanation. Their legacy is everywhere: capsuleer technology, the SoCT, the CONCORD structure, and possibly the Drifters. For Kybernauts content in Pochven, the Jove are the "ghost in the machine" — their abandoned space is now Triglavian territory, their technology is the foundation of capsuleer society, and their fate serves as a cautionary tale about transhumanism. The SoCT (Jove successors) and the Triglavians (Jove successors in Pochven) now vie for the same territory and legacy.

**Satire/Perspective Angles:**
- The Jove invented capsule immortality but can't cure their own depression — "advanced civilization, still can't fix mental health"
- "We gave you clones so you'd help us find a cure. You didn't. Enjoy your eternal space war."
- Jove Disease: when you optimize yourself so hard your soul gives up

### Blood Raider Covenant
**Researched:** 2026-06-07

**Summary:** The Blood Raider Covenant is one of the most feared pirate organizations in New Eden, notorious for ritual blood harvesting and the brutal efficiency of their attacks. They are a particularly well-organized sect of the Sani Sabik ("Blood Seed"), an ancient Amarrian schismatic cult that dates back thousands of years to pre-spaceflight Amarr Prime. The Sani Sabik split from mainstream Amarrian theology over the belief that some people are born for greatness while others exist only to serve the chosen — combined with an obsessive fixation on blood as the vehicle for immortality. The mainstream Amarr faith branded them heretical and drove them out, but the cult survived in hiding for millennia, its sects scattered across the Bleak Lands and beyond, fighting amongst themselves until Omir Sarikusa unified the most powerful faction into what became the Blood Raider Covenant.

Under Sarikusa's leadership, the Covenant shifted focus from targeting children (whose blood was considered "purest") to targeting clones and pod pilots, believing the blood of a cloned body to be the most potent for their arcane rituals. This drove them into space piracy at scale — boarding passenger vessels, abducting entire crews, and processing their "donors" either aboard the captured ships or in hidden facilities. Their base of power is in the Delve region, and they launch lightning raids deep into Amarr Empire space through the Bleak Lands and Devoid regions. The Bleak Lands were their historic stronghold until YC107, when the Amarr Navy descended in force following the horrors of Mabnen and drove them out — though the region remains lawless and dangerous.

The Blood Raiders share a surprising amount of religious DNA with the Amarr faith. They view mainstream Amarr as "believers led astray" who could realize their true potential if they embraced the uncompromising, savage nature of their own scripture. The Sani Sabik revere the Red God, a deity associated with blood, life, rejuvenation, destruction, and vengeance — essentially a more honest version of the Amarr God that acknowledges the Empire's violent foundations. Their followers wear golden vials of blood around their necks, symbolizing both protection and the constant reminder that blood must be taken from others. This reinforces the core Sani Sabik value: eternal vigilance, aggression, and the active pursuit of their faith.

The Covenant has had dealings with high-ranking Amarr officials. During Dochuta Karsoth's corrupt rule as Court Chamberlain, he collaborated with the Blood Raiders on illicit projects and even fled to them when Empress Jamyl I returned. They were reportedly both terrified and enraged by Jamyl's return, seeing their plans with Karsoth collapse. More recently, the Blood Raiders featured prominently in the recurring **Crimson Harvest** event (annually, most recently October 2025), which pits the Covenant against the Order of St. Tetrimon mercenaries and offers capsuleers the chance to choose sides in the conflict.

**Key Figures:**
- **Omir Sarikusa** — CEO of the Blood Raider Covenant, legendary Sani Sabik priest, heads both the Amarr Empire's most wanted list and the DED's most wanted list
- **Dochuta Karsoth** — former Amarr Court Chamberlain who collaborated with (and later hid among) the Blood Raiders after Jamyl I returned
- **Antar** — Amarr Holder and Karsoth's agent sent as an emissary to the Blood Raiders
- The Red God — central deity of Sani Sabik faith (but this may be a supernatural entity, a concept, or literal belief)

**Key Events:**
- Millennia ago — Sani Sabik cult deemed heretical by Council of Apostles, driven into Bleak Lands
- Pre-Capsuleer era — The scattered sects war among themselves; Omir Sarikusa unifies the most powerful into the Blood Raider Covenant
- Post-capsuleer era — Sarikusa redirects targets from children to cloned pod pilots, expanding into space piracy
- YC106-107 — Clashes between Blood Raiders and Amarr Navy escalate, including the Mabnen incident
- YC107 — Amarr Navy offensive drives Blood Raiders out of the Bleak Lands
- During Jamyl I's reign — Sarikusa's sect appears politically withdrawn but increases incursions along empire borders
- 2015–2025 (recurring) — Crimson Harvest events; Blood Raiders vs. Order of St. Tetrimon
- YC127 (2025) — Major Crimson Harvest event with extended content, Biocybernetic Incident sites, and Blood Raider ship rebalancing (Cruor, Ashimmu)

**Why It Matters:** The Blood Raiders are the perfect villain for the Amarr Empire's narrative — they're the dark mirror of Amarrian faith, practicing openly what the Empire does behind closed doors. Their existence proves that the Amarr religion contains the seeds of its own monstrosity, a point the Minmatar have been making for centuries. For Kybernauts content: the Blood Raiders operate in the same moral neighborhood as Pochven raiders and the more violent Triglavian clades — the space between "faith" and "cargo cult" is narrower than most empires admit. Additionally, the Crimson Harvest event structure (choose Blood Raiders or Tetrimon) gives players a moral choice system that Kybernauts content could riff on — "which flavor of awful do you prefer?"

**Satire/Perspective Angles:**
- The Sani Sabik golden vial necklace is basically a "I survived bloodletting and all I got was this lousy vial" accessory
- Blood Raiders vs Amarr: "Your god eats people too, we're just honest about it"
- The Crimson Harvest is EVE's version of October spooky season — but instead of pumpkin spice, it's ritual exsanguination
- Omir Sarikusa has been CEO of the Blood Raider corporation since permadeath was invented — talk about tenure
- "The Amarr drove the Blood Raiders out of the Bleak Lands. They took it personally. Now they take everyone's blood personally."
- For Kybernauts: the Blood Raiders are what happens when AI-driven transhumanism takes a hard left into religious mania — contrast with Triglavian hive-mind collectivism
- The Order of Tetrimon + Blood Raiders dynamic = "we hired the marginally less evil mercenaries to fight the definitely evil cult"
