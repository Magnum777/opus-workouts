## Core NPC Factions

### The Four Empires

#### Amarr Empire
- **Philosophy:** Theocratic monarchy, divine right, slavery ("Reclaiming")
- **Key figures:** Empress Catiz I (current), Emperor Heideran VII (deceased), Jamyl Sarum (returned, controversial)
- **Key events:**
  - The Reclaiming — conquest and enslavement of Minmatar
  - Jamyl Sarum's return — supposedly dead heir returned with unknown tech
  - The Elder War — Minmatar-Thukker alliance strike at Amarr heartland
  - The coronation of Catiz I — first non-royal to claim throne via championship
- **Notable locations:** Dam-Torsad (capital), The Throne Worlds, Sarum Prime

#### Minmatar Republic
- **Philosophy:** Tribal democracy, freedom fighters, recovering from Amarr enslavement
- **Key figures:** Maleatu Shakor (current Sanmatar), Karin Midular (assassinated), The Seven Tribes' leaders
- **Key events:**
  - The Great Rebellion (23216 AD) — Minmatar overthrow Amarr occupation
  - The Elder War — coordinated strikes by Minmatar and Thukker against Amarr
  - Republic Fleet buildup under Shakor
- **Notable locations:** Pator (capital), Matar (homeworld, still occupied), The Bleak Lands

#### Caldari State
- **Philosophy:** Corporate meritocracy, efficiency, militarism, grudge against Gallente
- **Key figures:** Tibus Heth (former CEO, ousted after coup attempt), Ishukone CEO Mens Reppola
- **Key events:**
  - The Gallente-Caldari War — Caldari expulsion from Caldari Prime
  - The Heth era — rise and fall of nationalist strongman Tibus Heth
  - The Kyonoke Crisis — biological weapon outbreak in Caldari/Amarr border
  - The Triglavian Invasion — Caldari space heavily affected by Pochven
- **Notable locations:** New Caldari (capital), Caldari Prime (occupied by Gallente but Caldari cultural center)

#### Gallente Federation
- **Philosophy:** Liberal democracy, multiculturalism, individual freedom, drone technology
- **Key figures:** President Jacus Roden, former president Souro Foiritan
- **Key events:**
  - The Gallente-Caldari War — origin of the interstellar cold war
  - The Fountain War (player-driven, but rooted in Federation ideals)
  - The Triglavian Invasion — Federation space affected
- **Notable locations:** Luminaire (Caldari Prime system), Villore, Dodixie

### Pirate Factions & Other NPCs

#### Sansha's Nation
- **Philosophy:** Transhumanist hive mind, "True Creations" — cybernetic implants to merge consciousness
- **Key figure:** Sansha Kuvakei — visionary scientist turned villain
- **Key events:**
  - Nation's founding — Kuvakei expelled by empires for unethical experiments
  - Incursions — Nation forces periodically invade empire space
  - The Nation's return — Kuvakei still alive, leading incursions
- **Notable:** True Slave cybernetic implants, "True Sansha" officers

#### Serpentis / The Syndicate
- **Philosophy:** Narcotics, organized crime, gambling
- **Key figure:** Salvador Sarpati — former Gallente scientist, runs the Syndicate
- **Notable:** The Fountain region, drug production

#### Blood Raiders / Theology Council
- **Philosophy:** Amarr heretical sect, blood harvesting for immortality rituals
- **Key figure:** Omir Sarikusa — Blood Raider leader
- **Notable:** The Covenant, blood farm operations

#### Angel Cartel
- **Philosophy:** Mercenaries, smugglers, largest pirate organization
- **Notable:** Angel Cartel space, Dramiel ships

#### Guristas Pirates
- **Philosophy:** "Rabbit" and "Vexor" — two former Caldari Navy officers turned pirates
- **Notable:** Venal region, Guristas missions

#### Mordu's Legion
- **Philosophy:** Mercenary corp, freed from Caldari indenture
- **Notable:** Mordu ships, high-end mercenary contracts

### The Triglavian Collective
- **See:** `memory/subminds/kybernauts-knowledge.md` for detailed Triglavian/Pochven lore
- **Key figures:** The Convocations (Clades Perun, Svarog, Veles, Dazh, etc.)
- **Key events:** The Invasion (YC 121), formation of Pochven, capsuleer migration

## Player History & Major Events

### The Great Wars (Nullsec)

#### The Great War / The First Great War (~2007-2009)
- **Belligerents:** Band of Brothers (BoB) vs RedSwarm Federation (Goonswarm + Red Alliance + Tau Ceti)
- **Outcome:** BoB disbanded after a director-level defection (Haargoth Agamar), Goonswarm rose
- **Significance:** First truly massive nullsec conflict, established "theft and treachery" as valid tactics

#### The Second Great War / The War of Northern Aggression
- **Belligerents:** Northern Coalition (NC) vs Drone Region Forces (DRF)
- **Outcome:** NC fell, DRF controlled much of the North

#### The Fountain War (2013)
- **Belligerents:** TEST Alliance + Goonswarm (CFC) vs N3 + Pandemic Legion
- **Outcome:** CFC won, TEST lost Fountain, eventually resettled
- **Cultural impact:** Largest war at that point, ~$300K+ in real money lost

#### The Bloodbath of B-R5RB (2014)
- **Location:** B-R5RB system, Immensea
- **Belligerents:** N3/PL vs CFC/RUS
- **Casualties:** 75+ Titans (the largest ships), thousands of caps
- **Value:** Estimated $300,000-$330,000 in real money
- **Significance:** Still the single most expensive battle in gaming history until WWB

#### World War Bee / The Casino War (2016)
- **Belligerents:** The Imperium (Goonswarm + allies) vs Money Badger Coalition (everyone else)
- **Trigger:** Casino-funded war (IWantISK, EVE Casino)
- **Outcome:** Imperium lost most of their space, retreated to Delve, eventually rebuilt
- **Significance:** Fundamentally reshaped nullsec; Goonswarm's "rebuilding in Delve" became legendary

#### The Siege of 9-4 (2018)
- **Location:** 9-4RP2, Delve
- **Belligerents:** TEST + allies vs The Imperium
- **Significance:** Largest battle in EVE history by player count (6,000+ in system)

#### World War Bee 2 (2021)
- **Belligerents:** PAPI (TEST, Horde, Brave, etc.) vs The Imperium
- **Outcome:** After months of fighting, PAPI failed to fully destroy Imperium, eventually retreated
- **Significance:** Largest war in EVE history by total ships lost and duration

#### The Mittani (Alex Gianturco)
- **Role:** Leader of Goonswarm Federation, The Imperium
- **Famous for:** Metagaming, propaganda, "Realpolitik" in EVE
- **Controversies:** Several including harassment incidents, eventual ban
- **Quote:** "We are not here to play spaceships, we are here to ruin your game."

### The Meta & Culture

#### The Spy Game
- **Axiom:** In EVE, spying is not cheating — it's gameplay
- **Notable incidents:** Haargoth Agamar (BoB disbanding), The Judge (Test betrayal), countless moles
- **Tools:** Discord infiltration, API theft, social engineering

#### The Titan Bridge & Doomsday
- **Mechanics:** Jump bridges, cynosural fields, doomsday devices
- **Tactics:** B-R5RB proved Titans could be killed; "dread bomb" tactics
- **Economic impact:** Single Titan = ~$3,000 real money

#### The Tranquility Server
- **Name:** EVE's single global server
- **Significance:** Everyone plays together — no regions, no shards
- **Peak:** 65,000+ concurrent players

#### CCP Games & Player Relations
- **Scandals:** T20 (dev favoritism), Incarna/Monoclegate ($70 monocles), various balance issues
- **Community:** EVE is known for brutal, unforgiving gameplay
- **Permaband:** Permanently banned players, sometimes with flair (notably Helmar's "cone of shame")
