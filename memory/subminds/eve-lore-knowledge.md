##### Amarr Theology Council
**Researched:** 2026-06-01

**Summary:** The Theology Council is one of two Imperial Courts in the Amarr Empire, created by Emperor Heideran V during the Moral Reforms (around 21875 AD). Its public purpose was to clarify the emperor's legal powers per Scripture; its actual function was to rewrite the Scriptures to concentrate power in the throne. Today it functions as the supreme religious court of the Empire, with the sole authority to interpret and modify the Scriptures. Its jurisdiction covers any crime proscribed by Scripture — which is almost everything, from murder and heresy down to shoplifting and adultery. It also hears appeals from the Civic Court and lower Holder courts.

**Structure and Procedures:**
- **Justices:** Specially trained priests with deep Scriptural knowledge. Serious crimes are heard before multiple justices.
- **Advocates:** Laymen trained in Scriptural law who present interpretations of Scripture for prosecution and defense.
- **Inquisitors:** Court-appointed officials who question witnesses and the accused. There is no protection against self-incrimination (defendants required to testify).
- **Process:** Inquisitorial + adversarial hybrid. The court gathers all evidence, an inquisitor questions everyone, then advocates debate what Scripture says about the facts. Justices retire and deliver judgment.
- **Legislative role:** The Theology Council is the only body authorized to formally alter the Scriptures — adding new passages, removing obsolete ones, codifying imperial decrees into permanent law. This gives it de facto legislative power, though it rarely exercises it independently.

**Key Figures:** Emperor Heideran V (founder of the Council), assorted high justices (priest-scholars)

**Relationship to Other Bodies:**
- **Civic Court:** The lesser Imperial Court handling secular/minor crimes (permits, traffic, trespassing, civil disputes between commoners). Emerged from conquered Udorian courts. Jurisdiction was deliberately gutted by Heideran V to favor the Theology Council. Foreign contract disputes have recently revived its relevance.
- **Privy Council:** The executive branch of Imperial government. Includes the five Heirs (Ardishapur, Kador, Khanid, Kor-Azor, Sarum — later Tash-Murkon replacing Khanid) plus appointed officials (Court Chamberlain, Imperial Chancellor, etc.).
- **Imperial Ministries:** Civil Service (bureaucracy, permits, archiving, largest employer in the Empire), Trade Registry (financial/taxation overseer), Ministry of Assessment (resource surveying — declining), Ministry of Internal Order (intelligence/police/heresy hunting), Ministry of War.

**Why It Matters:** The Theology Council is the engine room of Amarr theocracy — the body that makes the empire distinct from the other four. Every law is rooted in Scripture, and the Council holds the keys to Scripture itself. For Pochven/Kybernauts content, this is fertile ground for satire: imagine Triglavian collective decision-making vs. Amarr courts debating whether shoplifting violates a passage written 20,000 years ago. The Inquisitor system is pure horror-comedy material.

**Satire/Perspective Angles:**
- The Civic Court vs. Theology Council jurisdictional battles = "who gets to ruin your day first" bureaucracy
- No self-incrimination protection + required testimony = Amarr justice is a kangaroo court with better robes
- The Book of Records (census data kept by Civil Service) tracking everything feeds surveillance state satire
- Contrast with Triglavian collective: "The Amarr must be punished... for having too much paperwork"
- The Ministry of Assessment declining because they ran out of systems to explore is a great "poverty of ambition" joke

##### Minmatar Seven Tribes
**Researched:** 2026-06-02

**Summary:** The Minmatar people trace their origins to seven distinct tribes that developed on the planet Matar over thousands of years. Each tribe has a unique cultural identity, shaped by their homeland, spiritual traditions, and historical experience with the Amarr Empire. Only five of seven tribes are currently part of the Minmatar Republic; the Thukker maintain their own independent nomadic nation in the Great Wildlands, and the Nefantar (known as Ammatar) were collaborators with the Amarr and form a semi-autonomous state called the Ammatar Mandate.

**The Seven Tribes:**

- **Brutor:** Martial, disciplined, strong-willed. Led by tribal leader Wkumi Pol, the Brutor hold their heritage close and advocate for transforming the Republic into a tribal-based government. They dream of reuniting all seven tribes into one nation. The First Elder Brute embodies martial strength. Brutor make excellent soldiers and fleet commanders. They were hit especially hard during Amarr enslavement due to their resistance.

- **Sebiestor:** Engineers and inventors, widely respected as the most innovative thinkers in the cluster. Patient, contemplative, graceful. Led by Karin Midular (tribal leader) and Vuld Haupt (spiritual leader). The Sebiestor dominate Republic engineering and industrial production. Their ship designs (Rifter, Tempest) emphasize speed and flexibility.

- **Krusual:** Cautious and cunning, shrouding their ways in subterfuge and secrecy. Isolationist by nature but increasingly drawn into Republic power politics. The First Elder Kruss embodies subversion and cunning. The Krusual and Nefantar have a historic enmity dating back to pre-empire internecine warfare on Matar.

- **Vherokior:** Originally nomads of Matar's vast desert regions. The least troubled tribe, content to live on the fringe. Most Vherokiors are shopkeepers or small-scale businessmen, seldom venturing into interstellar corporate leagues. The First Elder Vheroka embodies versatility and wisdom. Known for their welcoming nature and teaching traditions.

- **Starkmanir:** Thought to have been "lost" when the Amarr destroyed their home planet. Surprisingly, remnants of the tribe survived, hidden by the Nefantar within the Ammatar Mandate. The Sisters of EVE discovered them just prior to the Elder War, and the tribe is now being slowly reintegrated. The First Elder Stark embodies tall, quick, stoic traits. Their spiritual leader is Vuld Haupt (who also serves the Sebiestor).

- **Nefantar (Ammatar):** The collaborators. When the Amarr first raided Matar, Nefantar elites traded their fellow Minmatar for immunity. They adopted Amarr aristocratic hierarchy and moved their capital to Hjoramold. During Amarr rule they became known as the Ammatar. After the Elder Fleet invasion, many Nefantar returned to the Republic and reclaimed their original name.

- **Thukker:** The independent ones. Rather than joining the Republic after the rebellion, the Thukker chose to maintain their traditional nomadic lifestyle in the Great Wildlands, recognized as a sovereign state. The First Elder Thuk embodies nomadic, individualistic, self-sufficient traits. They produce high-end tech and are known for their fleet doctrines favoring speed and active shield tanking.

**The Elders:**
The First Elders — Sebiess, Nefan, Thuk, Vheroka, Stark, Brute, and Kruss — are deified figures in Minmatar tradition, believed to have possessed artifacts that granted them authority. Each Elder is considered an eternal spirit that passes to successors, making each current Elder "one and the same person" as the original. They were the pre-Amarr governing body of the Minmatar people and returned to prominence during the Elder War (YC 110), leading a large-scale invasion of Amarr space.

**Why It Matters:** The tribal structure is foundational to understanding Minmatar identity — it's not a homogenous nation but a coalition with deep internal fractures. The Nefantar/Starkmanir dynamic is particularly rich: the tribe that hid their kin for centuries while collaborating with slavers. The Thukker existence as a sovereign nation outside the Republic shows the limits of Minmatar unity.

**Satire/Perspective Angles:**
- Brutor wanting to "reunite all seven tribes" = Minmatar Reconstruction memes
- Krusual/Nefantar blood feud = perfect for "Old World grudges" jokes in Pochven context
- Starkmanir return narrative = "discovering you have cousins you never knew existed"
- Thukker as the "we're not joining your club, we're doing our own thing" tribe
- The First Elders artifacts being at the bottom of the ocean = Minmatar Atlantis jokes
- Comedy angle: "Which tribe is best?" bar argument that escalates into a fleet fight
- For Kybernauts: contrasts well with Triglavian Clades — both have ancient origin mythologies, but Elders are individual spirits while Triglavian Convocations are collective minds

##### Caldari Big Eight Megacorporations
**Researched:** 2026-06-03

**Summary:** The Caldari State is a corporate state controlled by eight megacorporations that are sovereign within their territories. The "Big Eight" own everything — from the factories where you work to the home you live in to the police that patrol your street. They sit on the Chief Executive Panel (CEP), which functions as the Caldari government, smoothing over inter-corporate conflicts and managing foreign policy. Each megacorp maintains its own security forces (private armies), which collectively match the official Caldari Navy in numbers.

**The Big Eight (by factional affiliation):**

**Patriots (most powerful bloc — Lai Dai, Wiyrkomi, Kaalakiota):**
- **Kaalakiota Corporation (KK):** The largest megacorp. Led by Haatakan Oiritsuu, who was famously ousted by Tibus Heth during the YC110 riots. KK has its fingers in everything — manufacturing, distribution, retail, research, real estate. Its roots date back to the Caldari-Gallente War when it led the opposition. Ships favor missiles and thick shields, painted deep black with blood red streaks. Security force: Home Guard. KK is SuVee's most hated rival.
- **Lai Dai Corporation:** Quality-over-quantity culture. Their products are the highest standard in the State, and their energetic research program rivals Ishukone's tech achievements. Ships favor buffer shield tanks. Security force: Lai Dai Protection Service.
- **Wiyrkomi Corporation:** One of two family-owned megacorps. Still owned by the founding Seituoda family, known for honor and trustworthiness. Their security force is called... Wiyrkomi Corporate Police (notably less dramatic branding).

**Liberals (free trade faction — Hyasyoda, Ishukone):**
- **Ishukone Corporation:** The comeback story of the cluster. Long the weakest megacorp, Ishukone got a Jovian contact monopoly — becoming *the* developers of capsule technology. Under legendary CEO Otro Gariushi, Ishukone became known for high-tech equipment, high employee living standards, and fierce opposition to Tibus Heth's Providence Directorate. Gariushi was assassinated (still suspicious), and successor Mens Reppola kept the corporation independent through Heth's attempted forced buyouts. Ships feature mottled black and brown, hybrid turrets over missiles. Security force: Ishukone Watch. Ishukone also administers the Caldari Districts on Caldari Prime after Operation Highlander broke the Siege.
- **Hyasyoda Corporation:** One of the oldest megacorps, formed shortly after first contact with the Gallente. Conservatism is their brand — "Only the paranoid survive." Their ships are practical, their contracts are safe. Security force: Corporate Police Force.

**Practicals (zero-sum, no ethics — SuVee, CBD, NOH):**
- **Sukuuvestaa Corporation (SuVee):** The second largest, most ruthless megacorp. Owns ~40% of all land in Caldari space through outright ownership and subsidiaries. Known for hostile takeovers, seizing smaller rivals, and quelling scandals through the best PR department in the cluster. Their PR is so good they make public execution look like a tax break. Security force: Peace and Order Unit. SuVee slumlords = legendary /r/EVE posting material.
- **CBD Corporation:** The trade and logistics giant — biggest exporter/importer in Caldari space. They're everywhere, with goods in constant fluctuation across the State. Their security force, Spacelane Patrol, is portrayed in Caldari entertainment as brash hotshots traveling the cluster (space police procedural holodrama energy).
- **Nugoeihuvi Corporation (NOH):** The only megacorp focused on entertainment. Think of them as the Caldari Disney — but with suspected ties to organized crime. Their entertainment deals have turned the megacorp police forces into propaganda icons, with holoreel serials making cops look like action heroes. Security force: Internal Security. NOH themselves have a curious irony: despite being the entertainment corp, Internal Security is portrayed as the least glamorous force.

**Three Power Blocs:**
The Caldera corporate landscape is split into three political factions that function as de facto political parties:
1. **Patriots** (KK + Lai Dai + Wiyrkomi) — jingoistic, Caldari-supremacist, ambivalent toward Heth (he did what they wanted, but he did it *to them*)
2. **Liberals** (Hyasyoda + Ishukone) — free trade, pro-CONCORD, critical of Heth but publicly quiet
3. **Practicals** (SuVee + CBD + NOH) — exploitative, pro-Heth (pragmatically), zero-sum world view, quietly planning for his downfall

**Why It Matters:** The Big Eight aren't just ship logos — they're the skeleton of Caldari society. Every Caldari player character is literally born into one of these corps (that's how character creation works). Understanding the Big Eight means understanding that Caldari political drama isn't about a president — it's about which CEO got the better quarterly earnings. The Heth era (YC110-YC115) was essentially a hostile takeover of the entire State, and the aftermath reshaped every corp's power balance. For Kybernauts/Pochven content, the megacorp dynamic is a great foil to the Triglavian Collective's clade-based structure — vs. corporate boardrooms and quarterly earnings calls, the Collective seems almost refreshing.

**Satire/Perspective Angles:**
- "The Big Eight" as Caldari corporate oligarchy — imagine EVE's version of Cyberpunk's Arasaka but eight of them
- NOH as "Caldari Disney" with mob ties — amusement parks owned by the same people who supply the underworld
- SuVee PR department: "We made you love the most hated company in the cluster" = late-stage capitalism memes
- Kaalakiota vs SuVee rivalry as "Coke vs Pepsi" but with navy-sized security forces committing actual war crimes
- The Corporate Police Force holoreels making cops look like handsome special ops = the most Caldari thing ever (propaganda-as-entertainment)
- Ishukone lived the startup dream: "We got a Jovian VC investment and now we're the biggest tech company"
- For Kybernauts: "The Amarr have a Theology Council; the Caldari have a quarterly earnings call. Pick your nightmare."

## Player History & Major Events

### The Great Wars (Nullsec)

#### The Great War / The First Great War (~2007-2009)
- **Belligerents:** Band of Brothers (BoB) vs RedSwarm Federation (Goonswarm + Red Alliance + Tau Ceti)
- **Outcome:** BoB disbanded after a director-level defection (Haargoth Agamar), Goonswarm rose
- **Significance:** First truly massive nullsec conflict, established "theft and treachery" as valid tactics

#### The Second Great War / The War of Northern Aggression
- **Belligerents:** Northern Coalition (NC) vs Drone Region Forces (DRF)
- **Outcome:** NC fell, DRF controlled much of the North

#### The Fountain War (2013)
- **Belligerents:** TEST Alliance + Goonswarm (CFC) vs N3 + Pandemic Legion
- **Outcome:** CFC won, TEST lost Fountain, eventually resettled
- **Cultural impact:** Largest war at that point, ~$300K+ in real money lost

#### The Bloodbath of B-R5RB (2014)
- **Location:** B-R5RB system, Immensea
- **Belligerents:** N3/PL vs CFC/RUS
- **Casualties:** 75+ Titans (the largest ships), thousands of caps
- **Value:** Estimated $300,000-$330,000 in real money
- **Significance:** Still the single most expensive battle in gaming history until WWB

#### World War Bee / The Casino War (2016)
- **Belligerents:** The Imperium (Goonswarm + allies) vs Money Badger Coalition (everyone else)
- **Trigger:** Casino-funded war (IWantISK, EVE Casino)
- **Outcome:** Imperium lost most of their space, retreated to Delve, eventually rebuilt
- **Significance:** Fundamentally reshaped nullsec; Goonswarm's "rebuilding in Delve" became legendary

#### The Siege of 9-4 (2018)
- **Location:** 9-4RP2, Delve
- **Belligerents:** TEST + allies vs The Imperium
- **Significance:** Largest battle in EVE history by player count (6,000+ in system)

#### World War Bee 2 (2021)
- **Belligerents:** PAPI (TEST, Horde, Brave, etc.) vs The Imperium
- **Outcome:** After months of fighting, PAPI failed to fully destroy Imperium, eventually retreated
- **Significance:** Largest war in EVE history by total ships lost and duration

### Gallente Senate politics
**Researched:** 2026-06-04

**Summary:** The Gallente Federation Senate is the legislative branch of the Federation government, forming one of the "Three Pillars of Power" alongside the executive Presidency and the judicial Supreme Court. Established during the Third Union reforms (after the Caldari secession in 23156 AD), the Senate is fully elected by universal suffrage every five years, with Senators representing districts rather than member nations. 

The Senate's structure reflects the Federation's hybrid democracy: roughly 300 Senators represent the Federation's 250+ districts, each constellation-level territory with its own parliament and court. Senators replaced appointed member-state representatives after the Third Union, a deliberate move to weaken national loyalties and strengthen Federal identity. Districts further had the power of "opt-out" � allowing territories to refuse certain Federal laws while still voting in Federation elections, a compromise that kept the Jin-Mei (with their controversial caste system) and other culturally distinct members from leaving.

The Sixth Union era (YC103 onward) saw Senate politics escalate into open conflict with President Souro Foiritan. Head Senator Mentas Blaque emerged as Foiritan's chief rival, with the Senate becoming the champion of megacorporate and entrenched interests. Key Senate figures included the astute Jaq-Foix Netharin and extremist Maridane Wilfort, who wielded lobbyists, moneymen, and armies of lawyers against the charismatic President. Meanwhile, the Supreme Court acted as a "beacon of common sense," intervening to preserve the Federation's constitutional principles. The Federation-wide referendum system suffered from chronic low turnout (around 10% on average), as most citizens had minimal interaction with central government on spaceside affairs.

- Souro Foiritan � President (YC103-present, Progressor-turned-independent), two-term reformer
- Mentas Blaque � Head Senator, Foiritan's chief rival
- Jaq-Foix Netharin � Senate leader, strategic operator
- Maridane Wilfort � Senate extremist, hardline pro-business
- Jacus Roden � last successful citizen-candidate to self-fund a Federal campaign

#### Society of Conscious Thought (SoCT)
**Researched:** 2026-06-05

**Summary:** The Society of Conscious Thought is a three-centuries-old organization founded by a Jovian mystic named Ior Labron — who, uniquely among the scientifically-minded Jove, pursued spiritual enlightenment with characteristic Jovian intensity. The SoCT began as a cult exploring humanity's spiritual needs (their stated goal: "discovering the meaning of life"), later expanded into Jove politics, briefly became the Jove Empire's shadow government, was banned when rival factions broke their power, then retreated into obscurity before re-emerging in their modern role.

Today, the SoCT is famous for two things: their elite schools and their bizarre ship designs. Each remote enclave — called a "kitz" — houses self-sufficient mini-societies with schools that produce an astonishing number of future corporate presidents and government ministers. Their admissions process seems completely random to outsiders: wealth and connections buy no advantage, and even talent doesn't appear to predict acceptance. Yet graduates go on to dominate the highest levels of interstellar power. The SoCT operates Gnosis-class battlecruisers (given free to all players on EVE's 10th anniversary), along with the Sunesis, Praxis, Apotheosis, Metamorphosis, and the tournament-exclusive Hydra and Tiamat — the only Jovian-designed ships ever given to non-Jove capsuleers. Their ships have no racial weapon bonuses but make up for it with extreme versatility: all weapon systems usable, equal shield and armor HP, uniform resists, and full scanning bonuses.

**The Sarum Prime Incident:** In YC118, SoCT Elder Mentor Matshi Raish was flying his Gnosis in Sarum Prime (the heart of the Amarr Empire's Sarum family holdings) when he allegedly began scanning the late Empress Jamyl I's personal Avatar-class titan. The Amarr Navy and capsuleer forces responded by destroying his vessel — a dramatic escalation that almost cost the SoCT their representative's life. Mentor Raish survived and reappeared months later at the historic CONCORD press conference where the Jovian Directorate formally withdrew from the CONCORD Inner Circle and designated the SoCT as its full successor state. The transfer elevated the SoCT from a mysterious scholastic sect to a major diplomatic power overnight.

**Key Figures:**
- Ior Labron — Jovian founder of SoCT, spiritual seeker
- Matshi Raish — SoCT Elder Mentor, representative on CONCORD Inner Circle, survived Gnosis destruction in Sarum Prime
- Various unnamed SoCT Elder Mentors running the kitz network

**Key Events:**
- ~18,900 AD (approx.) — SoCT founded by Ior Labron
- ~19,200 AD (approx.) — SoCT becomes Jove shadow government, later banned by rival factions
- Recent decades — SoCT opens schools to non-Jove students, gradual re-emergence
- YC118 (July) — Matshi Raish's Gnosis destroyed in Sarum Prime while scanning Jamyl's titan
- YC118 (March 15) — Jovian Directorate withdraws from CONCORD Inner Circle, designates SoCT as full successor state
- Present — SoCT holds a CONCORD Inner Circle seat, operates Jovian corporations, maintains educational network

**Why It Matters:** The SoCT is the only surviving bridge to the Jove Empire — the mysterious precursors who were once the most advanced civilization in New Eden. Their sudden elevation from shadows to CONCORD power brokers makes them one of the most intriguing wildcards in New Eden politics. For Kybernauts set in Pochven, the SoCT is particularly relevant: the Jove are gone, and the SoCT now speaks for what remains of their legacy. They also possess Jovian technology knowledge that predates even the Triglavian Collective's return.

**Satire/Perspective Angles:**
- The SoCT school admissions process as "The Sorting Hat but actually incomprehensible" — nobody knows why you're picked, not even the pickers
- "I went to SoCT and all I got was this Jovian ship and a corner office at a megacorp"
- The SoCT went from "shadow government banned by everyone" to "we sit on the UN Security Council" — the ultimate rebranding strategy
- Matshi Raish scanning Jamyl's titan in Sarum Prime — "I was just LOOKING at it!" as intergalactic incident starter
- The SoCT Elite Mentor retirement package: "You get a kitz in a remote system and nobody bothers you. Ever. We promise."
- For Kybernauts: the SoCT is another faction with mysterious origins and hidden agendas — perfect for "who's really pulling the strings?" content
- Contrast between SoCT's Jovian spiritual enlightenment goals and the Triglavian Collective's transhumanist unity — different paths to enlightenment, same cosmic ambition
