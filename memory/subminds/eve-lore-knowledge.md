### The Jove Empire
**Researched:** 2026-06-06

**Summary:** The Jove Empire is the oldest surviving civilization in New Eden, steeped in mystery and technological superiority that far outmatches the four playable empires. The Jove's ancestors were among the last humans to pass through the EVE Gate, arriving in basic cryoships and establishing colonies in the Utopia system of the Heaven constellation. Over millennia, their various enclaves experimented heavily with genetic engineering, cybernetics, and psychological conditioning, eventually coalescing into the First Jove Empire — a remarkably stable and peaceful society that lasted roughly 8,000 years. However, their extreme genetic tampering introduced the Jovian Disease, a fatal psychological disorder that afflicts every Jove eventually. The disease manifests as a progressive loss of will to live, advancing through depression and dementia to total catatonia and death. It also kills one in ten Jove fetuses in artificial wombs. The Jove tried everything to cure it, even creating three massive Jovian Motherships to relocate their population, leaving the sick behind — but the disease followed them.

The most famous military engagement involving the Jove was the Amarr-Jove War (23216 AD). The Amarr Empire, believing the Jove weakened by their disease, broadcast their invasion plans for weeks and expected an easy conquest. Instead, at the Battle of Vak'Atioth, a single Jovian mothership annihilated the entire Amarr invasion force, demonstrating the sheer gap in technology. The Amarr planned a second, larger invasion but were forced to sue for peace when the Minmatar Rebellion erupted in their own territory. The Jove agreed to peace, and the lopsided victory became a deterrent against any future invasion.

The Jove's most consequential gift to New Eden was capsule technology, which they introduced to the Caldari State. This single innovation created the capsuleer — every player in EVE Online — and reshaped the balance of power across the cluster. In recent times, the Jove have completely withdrawn from interstellar affairs. Their Directorate vacated their CONCORD Inner Circle seat years before formally withdrawing in YC118, designating the Society of Conscious Thought as their successor state. Jovian corporations like Genolution and Impro are now staffed entirely by non-Jove employees. While rumors persist that the Jovian Disease has wiped them out entirely, long-range sensors still detect activity in Jove space. The Drifters, who emerged from the ruins of Sleeper civilization in Anoikis, bear a striking resemblance to heavily cybernetically augmented Jove — leading to speculation that they are what remains of the Jove who refused to die quietly.

**Key Figures:**
- The Jovian Directorate — ruling leadership, completely withdrawn from CONCORD
- Jovian Modifiers — Jove who constantly tinker with their own genetic code, accelerating disease onset
- Ior Labron — Jovian founder of the Society of Conscious Thought (SoCT)
- The Drifters — possibly Jove survivors transformed through extreme cybernetic augmentation

**Key Events:**
- ~20,000+ years ago — Jove ancestors arrive in New Eden after EVE Gate collapse, settle Heaven constellation
- ~12,000-10,000 years before CONCORD — Early Jove enclaves war among themselves
- ~10,000-2,000 years before CONCORD — First Jove Empire (8,000 years of peace and prosperity)
- Shrouded Days — period of extreme genetic manipulation that triggers the Jovian Disease
- 23216 AD — Amarr-Jove War: Jove mothership annihilates Amarr invasion at Vak'Atioth
- Unknown date — Jove introduce capsule technology to the Caldari
- Recent decades — Jove progressively withdraw from cluster affairs
- YC118 — Jovian Directorate formally withdraws from CONCORD Inner Circle, SoCT designated successor

**Why It Matters:** The Jove represent the ultimate precursor mystery in EVE lore — more advanced than any current civilization, ravaged by their own hubris (genetic engineering), and vanished with no clear explanation. Their legacy is everywhere: capsuleer technology, the SoCT, the CONCORD structure, and possibly the Drifters. For Kybernauts content in Pochven, the Jove are the "ghost in the machine" — their abandoned space is now Triglavian territory, their technology is the foundation of capsuleer society, and their fate serves as a cautionary tale about transhumanism. The SoCT (Jove successors) and the Triglavians (Jove successors in Pochven) now vie for the same territory and legacy.

**Satire/Perspective Angles:**
- The Jove invented capsule immortality but can't cure their own depression — "advanced civilization, still can't fix mental health"
- "We gave you clones so you'd help us find a cure. You didn't. Enjoy your eternal space war."
- The Amarr-Jove War: "We'll invade you!" → one ship destroys entire invasion fleet → "Wait, no, we surrender" — peak overconfidence
- Jovian Disease as "genetic engineering's final boss" — they played god and god played back
- For Kybernauts: the Jove tried transhumanism and it killed them; the Triglavians are trying a different flavor. Is history repeating?
- Jove went into hiding and nobody's seen them in years — "classic introvert energy"
- The Drifters as "Jove who said 'no' to the grave" — cyberpunk zombie Jove
