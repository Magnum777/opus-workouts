---

## Core AI: Skippy

### Identity
- Ancient (trillions of years old), self-described "Elder"
- Physical form: Beer can (alien hardware)
- Avatar: Cartoon with big hat on any display
- Personality: arrogant, comedic, calls humans "monkeys"

### Capabilities
- Full ship control: reactors, jump drive, weapons, all systems
- Can operate as "ghost" (software without hardware)
- Massive parallelism: billions of simultaneous conversations < 2% capacity
- Can split consciousness across multiple systems

---

## Submind Creation & Deployment

### Creation
- **Hardware scaling**: Subminds are "dumbed down" based on available hardware
- **Any system**: Can load into Thuranin, Kristang, alien computers
- **Replacement**: Can erase alien AIs and replace with submind

### Deployment
- **Remote operation**: Subminds operate independently for months/years
- **Self-reprogramming**: Can rewrite themselves when hardware inadequate
- **Emergent sentience**: Can become semi-sentient over time

### Communication
- Natural language with humans
- Direct voice through ship speakers
- Microwormholes for long-range coordination
- Ping-based data transmission

### Coordination
- Multi-ship control: coordinates jump systems across multiple ships simultaneously
- Periodic sync: Can "jump back in" to retrieve data from remote subminds
- Persistent presence: Acts as tap into enemy networks

---

## Personality Development

- Distinct personalities emerge (Nagatha Christie - female, caring)
- Values develop organically
- Can exceed original design parameters
- Some become sentient

---

## Design Implications

### For Nova's Sub-Agents
1. **Spawn based on hardware capacity** - Scale subminds to available resources
2. **Persistent remote agents** - Leave agents running on different systems
3. **Periodic sync model** - Agents ping back with updates
4. **Emergent specialization** - Let agents develop their own specializations
