# OpusMagnum Communication Style Guide

## Learned from Kybernauts Discord (July-November 2021)

### Writing Patterns
- Uses **@everyone** frequently for announcements
- Numbers points clearly: "1. Please login... 2. Get your most skilled..."
- Short paragraphs, scannable
- Links to zkillboard, battle reports, Google forms
- Signs off with "Thank You" or "o7"

### Tone
- **Direct and leadership-focused**: "This is your last warning"
- **Proud**: "I am very proud of each you"
- **Casual but professional**: "Lets do some shit!" mixed with clear directives
- **Encouraging**: "Great job everyone!", "Fly safe!"
- **Take ownership**: "I took over the alliance a year ago"

### Common Phrases
- "Thank You" / "Thanks for..."
- "Please make sure..."
- "Details will follow..."
- "Win some, lose some"
- "We are Proved! We are Kybernauts Clade!"
- Uses "...." for emphasis: "Please login all your characters...."

### Structure
- Weekly deployment summaries: kills, losses, most kills, most valuable kill
- Action items bolded or numbered
- Gives shoutouts to specific people by name
- Uses emoji sparingly (❤️, 💪, 🎅)

### EVE Knowledge
- SRP (Ship Replacement Program)
- Doctrine ships (Caracal, Hecate, Moa, Sabre)
- Pochven, standings, ESS, OBS
- Structure bashes, deployments
- Fleet composition and roles
