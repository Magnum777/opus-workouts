# Spirit of the Law: Internal Dissolution to Hostile Entity

## The Principle

When a member corporation dissolves internally and its leadership directs pilots to join an entity Kybernauts Clade holds adversarial status with, those pilots forfeit their standing. They are not leaving under neutral terms — they are switching sides during active hostilities.

## Why It Matters

The alliance does not punish departure. Groups leave peacefully all the time. But when dissolution is used as a vehicle to move en masse into hostile space, it ceases to be a clean exit and becomes a transfer of personnel to the opposition. The spirit of the law is to recognize this distinction and act accordingly.

## What Triggers It

- Member corporation dissolves or restructures
- Leadership directs members to join a designated hostile entity
- That entity and Kybernauts Clade have active, unresolved hostilities

## The Hard Break

Blacklisting is not punishment. It is a firewall. It protects:
- The departing pilots, by removing any suspicion of espionage or intel leakage
- The alliance, by severing access and closing operational vulnerabilities
- Future relations, by creating a clean separation that time and diplomacy can eventually mend

## The Standard

No grace period. No individual exceptions. No appeals while hostilities persist. The standing policy applies the moment a pilot joins the hostile entity.
