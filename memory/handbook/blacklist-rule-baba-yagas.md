# Handbook Rule: Cross-Faction Recruitment and Blacklisting

## Scope
This rule governs alliance members who leave Kybernauts Clade to join corporations or alliances with whom we have active hostilities or unresolved territorial disputes.

## Current Designation
**Baba Yagas** — active adversarial status due to ongoing Pochven structure dispute.

## Rule
Any alliance member who leaves Kybernauts Clade to join Baba Yagas will be **immediately blacklisted** for the duration of active hostilities. This blacklist applies without exception and remains in force until the Pochven structure dispute is resolved to alliance leadership's satisfaction.

## Rationale

### 1. No Team-Switching
Moving from an allied or member corporation directly into an adversarial entity constitutes team-switching. This is prohibited regardless of the circumstances behind the original corporation's dissolution or restructuring.

### 2. Operational Security
Blacklisting creates a hard break that protects:
- **The departing members** — eliminates any suspicion of espionage, asset theft, or intel leaks
- **The alliance** — closes access vectors and preserves operational integrity
- **All parties** — prevents messy ambiguity about loyalties, standings, and fire permissions

## Procedure
When a member corporation announces dissolution or mass migration to a designated hostile entity:

1. **Immediate Effect** — The blacklist takes effect the moment a pilot joins the hostile entity. No grace period. No appeals during active hostilities.
2. **Communication** — Leadership will announce the dissolution and resulting policy in alliance channels. Members are expected to read and acknowledge these announcements.
3. **Standing Policy** — Any pilot who switches sides is treated as hostile until further notice. This is non-negotiable and applies at the individual pilot level, not just the corporate level.

## Example Scenario (Current)
Frame Perfect has dissolved. Its leadership elected to migrate all members into Baba Yagas. Per standing policy, all Frame Perfect pilots who join Baba Yagas are now blacklisted for the duration of the Pochven structure conflict between Baba Yagas and Kybernauts Clade.

## Duration
Blacklists tied to this rule remain active **only while the designated entity holds adversarial status**. Once the underlying dispute is resolved and leadership formally lifts the designation, affected pilots may petition for standings review.

## Questions
Direct questions about this rule or your personal status to alliance leadership. Do not argue the policy in public channels.
