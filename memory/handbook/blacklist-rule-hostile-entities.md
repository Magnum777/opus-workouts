# Blacklist Rule: Joining Hostile Entities

Any alliance member who joins a corporation or alliance designated as hostile by Kybernauts Clade leadership will be **immediately blacklisted**.

This blacklist remains in effect for the duration of active hostilities.

## What triggers it
- A member corporation dissolves and directs members to join a hostile entity
- Individual members leave to join an entity Kybernauts Clade holds adversarial status with
- Any active, unresolved hostilities exist between Kybernauts Clade and the target entity

**Baba Yagas** — active hostile. Pochven structure dispute.

*This rule applies whenever the pattern repeats, regardless of the entities involved.*
