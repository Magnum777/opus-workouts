# Draft 2 -- The Victory Lap

**After Seventeen Years of Pranks, Ceema Finally Drops Something Real, Community Still Recovering**

*(Satire)*

For seventeen years, the internet has been trying to figure out if Ceema is joking. Seventeen years. Seventeen years of posts on AreYouTrolling.me, seventeen years of Goonswarm memes, seventeen years of being so deeply committed to the bit that the bit became his whole personality. And now, finally, the man announces something for real, and the comments read like a group chat that forgot how to handle good news.

"In all fairness, I'm already running into people thinking this is an elaborate prank on my part," Ceema said. "Damn the years of shitposting."

Which is the most Ceema thing about the whole thing. Even when he's delivering real news, it sounds like the setup to a punchline. The man has trained an entire community to assume everything is a bit, and now he's paying the rent in exactly that currency.

But here's the part people are sleeping on: this is the best problem to have.

Think about it. You've spent nearly two decades building something that doesn't exist anywhere else. You've cultivated a voice so distinct that people can't distinguish it from performance. You've made yourself so consistent that authenticity reads as a gag. That's not a failure. That's called branding. That's called building a world where people can't tell where you end and the bit begins.

The new show hits different because Ceema's earned the benefit of the doubt without anyone realizing he had it. The same people who think it's a prank are the same people who can't look away. The same people who are waiting for the punchline are the same people who will share it with everyone they know.

Seventeen years of shitposting, and now the shitposting has built something real. That's not a trap. That's a legacy.

Go surprise them, Ceema. They won't know what hit them -- until they definitely do.
