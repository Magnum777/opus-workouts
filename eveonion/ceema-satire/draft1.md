# Local Man Finally Does Something Real, Internet Convinced It's A Bit

*(Satire)*

For seventeen years, Ceema has been the internet's most dedicated amateur. Seventeen years of memes, shitposts, and "weird EVE stuff" on his site AreYouTrolling.me. Seventeen years of being exactly as unhinged as the fictional universe he plays in. And now, finally, the man announces something legitimate, and the response from the community is, essentially: "Cool story, bro. Are you done?"

"In all fairness, I'm already running into people thinking this is an elaborate prank on my part," Ceema reportedly told a friend. "Damn the years of shitposting."

And there it is. The price of commitment. When you've spent nearly two decades being the guy who screams into the New Eden void, people tend to assume the void is screaming back at them.

The new show, whatever it actually is, joins a long and distinguished lineage of Ceema projects that walk the line between genuine content and sustained performance art. His blog, his memes, his deeply weird takes on Goonswarm drama all blur together into one coherent message: this man has never met a bit he couldn't run into the ground.

But here's the thing about running bits into the ground. Eventually you own the ground. The community built around him learned to treat every announcement as content, every reveal as another layer of the bit. And now Ceema's sitting there with a real press release, a real project, a real next chapter, being met with the same response he'd get if he announced the sky is blue.

The lesson, if there is one: if you perform authenticity for long enough, authenticity starts to look like performance. Ceema's spent seventeen years being the punchline. The punchline has finally punched out, and the crowd is still waiting for the punchline.

Good luck, Ceema. You'll have to earn this one the hard way.
