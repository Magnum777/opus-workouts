The Spool Problem

Kikimora disintegrators ramp over ~90 seconds. They start weak and get lethal. Here is the realistic spool curve for a T2-fitted Kikimora with Entropic Radiation Sink:

TimeSpool %DPS per Kiki30 Kikis Total

At 60+ seconds, 30 fully spooled Kikimoras = 12,000 DPS. That melts our entire fleet in ~25 seconds. We MUST kill enough of them in the first 20-30 seconds to survive.

Scenario C: The Neut Effect (Reality)

24 small neuts (12 Vexors × 2 each) = ~1,200 GJ/s total cap drain

One Kikimora cap usage: MWD (~40) + hardener (~10) + disintegrator (~5) = ~55 GJ/s

1,200 ÷ 55 = ~22 Kikimoras can be simultaneously capped out.

What this means in practice: In the first 5-10 seconds, our 24 neuts hit ~15-20 Kikimoras. Their MWDs die. Their hardeners cycle off. Their DPS drops because:
  • Without cap, disintegrator spool STOPS or resets
  • Without hardener, their resists drop 30-40%
  • Without MWD, they can't reposition or escape
Effective enemy DPS at t=10s is NOT 6,000. It's closer to 3,000-4,000 because 15-20 of them are capped out or partially capped.

Tactics: 18 vs 30

Pre-Engagement

  • Set drones to aggressive BEFORE landing. They auto-attack anything that shoots you.
  • Pre-load cap boosters. 400s in all ships. You're getting neuted.
  • Pre-heat prop mods. Overheat AB before you even see them.

The Landing

0-5s: Burn
  1. ALL 18 overheat prop and burn at them in a straight line. No orbiting. Straight through their formation.
  2. Moa #1 (left flank): MWD at closest Kikimora. Scram + web when in range.
  3. Moa #2 (right flank): Same. Target second closest.
  4. 12 Vexors + 4 Thoraxes: Follow Moas. Do NOT stop at 15km. Burn to scram range (8km).
5-10s: Lock and Kill
  1. Moas call primary + secondary.
  2. ALL ships: Lock primary. Scram. Web. Neuts ON. Blasters ON. Drones engage.
  3. Vexors specifically: Neut the primary AND a second nearby Kikimora.
  4. Primary dies in ~1.5 seconds.
10-20s: Switch and Survive
  1. Switch to secondary immediately. All 18 ships. Drones auto-switch if on aggressive.
  2. Neuts spread. Each Vexor should have neuts on 2 targets.
  3. If a ship drops below 30% hull, overheat hardener (right-click, Overheat).
  4. If you're the primary target and taking fire, do not panic. Keep shooting. Your death buys time for the fleet to kill 3 Kikimoras.
20s+: Extract Decision
  1. FC assesses: How many Kikimoras are left? How many of us are left?
  2. If they are breaking: Chase. Kill stragglers. Loot.
  3. If they are holding and we've lost 6+ ships: EXTRACT. Overheat AB. Burn to a bookmark/gate. Warp.
  4. Remember: At t=20s with 10+ Kikimora kills, you've already won the ISK war. Don't throw away a winning trade by staying too long.

Critical: What If Both Moas Die?

This will happen. 30 Kikimoras will focus our tackle first.
  • Backup tackle: Vexor #1 and Thorax #1 are designated secondary anchors. If both Moas die, THEY call primary.
  • Every ship has scram + web. The fleet doesn't stop if tackle dies.
  • Keep killing. Even without anchors, 16 ships with scram/web can still hold and kill.

Critical Rules for 18 vs 30

  1. Focus fire is mandatory. 18 ships shooting 1 target = dead in 1.5s. 18 ships shooting 18 targets = nobody dies. Call primary. Switch together. NO exceptions.
  2. Neuts on EVERYTHING. Don't just neut the primary. Each Vexor should neut 2 Kikimoras. Cap them out. Kill their MWD. Kill their hardener.
  3. Pre-heat before landing. Overheat AB, overheat guns, overheat hardener BEFORE you decloak/land. You have 20 seconds. Every tick matters.
  4. Don't chase deep. If they warp off, let them. Loot the 10+ wrecks. Extract with the win.
  5. Moa death = not the end. Vexor #1 and Thorax #1 are backup anchors. Every ship has scram + web.
  6. Extract at 20-30s if losing. You've already won the ISK war. Don't throw it away by staying too long.
  7. Drones on aggressive. Set before fight starts. They auto-switch when primary dies.

Summary

Why We Win

  • 8,900 DPS = Kikimora dies every 1.5 seconds
  • 24 neuts cap out 22 Kikimoras simultaneously
  • Buffer tank = neut-immune. Active tank dies.
  • Dual web = Kikimora speed drops to ~80 m/s
  • ~300M fleet vs ~2.7B enemy = 9:1 value ratio

What Can Go Wrong

  • 30 spooled Kikis at 60s = 12,000 DPS. We die.
  • Split fire kills 3 of ours every 13-14s
  • No focus fire = nobody dies, they spool, we lose
  • Both Moas die early = chaos, but survivable
  • Staying past 30s = DPS overwhelms us
The honest truth: We don't win the fight. We win the exchange. Expect to lose 6-10 cruisers. Expect to kill 12-18 Kikimoras. That's a +900M to +1.3B ISK profit every time.

If you want to "win" the fight (kill all 30, lose 0), you need 40+ ships or T2 logi. With 18 T1 cruisers, we trade. And we trade well.
