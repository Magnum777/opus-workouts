Purpose

The Skills Development Committee exists to forge Kybernauts.

Our role is not simply onboarding, ship contracts, or skill plans. Our purpose is transformation — taking capsuleers from wherever they are in their journey and helping them become pilots who understand what it means to live and thrive in Pochven.

Being a Kybernaut is not defined by the ship you fly. It is defined by how you approach New Eden: curiosity, aggression, cooperation, and the will to learn through action.

Standard
We do not build hangar queens. We build pilots who undock. Because Kybernauts are not recruited — they are made.

Guiding Principles

It's Not What You Fly

Ships are tools. Skills are tools. Doctrine is guidance. What matters is whether a pilot understands why they are flying what they are flying — and can execute under pressure.

All Members Are Equal in Growth

Skillpoints do not define value. A newbro and a veteran both deserve the tools to succeed.

No One Is Left Behind

We do not merely inject skills. We cultivate understanding — explanation, demonstration, repetition.

Activity Is Expected

This is not a passive program. Growth comes from participation. Undock. Ask. Fly. Improve.

Combat Is the Crucible

True learning happens in fleet. We teach through action: doctrine discipline, positioning, and execution.

SDC Responsibilities

1 — Corp Doctrine Fittings
  • Maintain purpose-built ship fittings for this isk generation, PVP and standings
  • Fits must align with alliance doctrines where applicable
2 — Income / PVP Doctrines (3 Total)
Develop three doctrines outside alliance doctrines.
  • Focus: ISK generation + combat capability
  • Must remain accessible to developing pilots
  • Examples: T1 cruiser logistics, T1 frigate hunting, destroyer fleets
3 — New Member Packages
Early Career and Newbros receive the same package:
  • Skill books
  • Basic fittings
  • Ship handouts
Distribution Point
Kino VII — Moon 17
The Convocation of Triglav Bioadaptation Chambers
4 — Communication Channels
  • Early Career and Newbros are initially closed off from most alliance channels
  • They are integrated into the alliance when ready
  • Controlled environment enables focused learning without distraction
5 — Activity Tracking
  • Automated processes track pilot progress through the program

Standings & Kyber Path

All new members must complete the following:
Standing Gates
  • Exit Ohnivak — transfer into KYBERNAUTS
  • Triglavian Standings — achieve 3.4 standing with Triglavian Unity
  • SDC Orientation — meet within 48 hours of arrival
  • Mentorship Pairing — bond with an experienced pilot for ongoing guidance
Operational Qualification

To be considered after submitting a Kyber application, a pilot must prove initiative and competence:

  • 3 solo kills in Pochven
  • Lead a fleet (at least one) after submitting their application
Intent
These gates ensure we build pilots who can fight, learn, and lead — not merely exist in space.

What We Provide / What We Expect

What We Provide
  • Skill plans calibrated for each development category
  • Mentorship pairings forged through experience
  • Fitting handouts optimized for success
  • Ship handouts for immediate contribution
  • Income-generating fleets designed for developing pilots
  • Protected spaces for learning and growth
What We Expect
  • Engagement. This is not a hanger-queen program.
  • Commitment to learning and self-improvement
  • Participation in SDC fleet operations
  • Pay it forward — assist those who follow behind you
Final Principle
WE ARE KYBERNAUTS.

We build pilots through transformation. We generate content through collective action. We strengthen community through shared growth. The Proving Grounds ensure every member who enters our space emerges adapted, capable, and ready.
