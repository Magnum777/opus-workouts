# 99designs Playbook

## Overview
99designs is a specialized graphic design platform famous for its contest-based model. Clients post design briefs, and multiple designers submit concepts. The client picks their favorite, and only that designer gets paid.

## Platform Model

### Contest-Based (Primary)
- Client posts a design brief (logo, website, packaging, etc.)
- Multiple designers submit entries
- Client selects winner → only winner gets prize money
- High competition: only small % consistently win

### 1-to-1 Projects (Direct Hire)
- Client hires a specific designer directly
- More predictable income for designers
- Lower fees than contests

## Fees & Revenue

### Contest Fees
- **Platform fee: 30-40%** of prize money
- Varies by designer level

### 1-to-1 Project Fees (Lower fees)
- Entry-level: 15%
- Mid-level: 10%
- Top-level: 5%

### Contest Prize Tiers
- Starter: ~$100-$300
- Standard: $300-$599
- Premium: $600+

## Pros
- High earning potential for top designers
- Portfolio building through contests
- Direct hire opportunities for established designers
- Secure payments through platform

## Cons
- High competition (many entries per contest)
- Only winner gets paid (others work for free)
- Platform takes 30-40% on contests
- Difficult for new freelancers to build predictable income

## Best For
- Graphic designers with strong portfolios
- Logo designers
- UI/UX designers
- Brand identity specialists
