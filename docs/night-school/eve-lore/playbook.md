## How to Use This Playbook

Each night, pick **one item** from the Research Queue below. Research it thoroughly, then append your findings to `memory/subminds/eve-lore-knowledge.md`.

## Research Method

1. **Search the EVE University Wiki** (wiki.eveuniversity.org)
   - Use `web_search` for the topic + "eve university wiki"
   - Extract key facts, dates, characters, locations

2. **Search the EVE Lore Portal** (lore.eveonline.com)
   - Look for official chronicles and fiction

3. **Search player history sources**
   - `web_search "EVE Online [topic] history"`
   - Check INN (news.imperium.news), Talking in Stations, r/Eve

4. **Synthesize**
   - Write a concise summary (200-500 words)
   - Include: What happened, who was involved, why it matters
   - Note any good quotes, funny moments, or satire angles

5. **Append**
   - Add to the appropriate section in `memory/subminds/eve-lore-knowledge.md`
   - Mark the topic as completed in this playbook
