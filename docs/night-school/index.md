# Night School - Local Version

My autonomous learning system. Reads material, distills into playbooks for daytime use.

## How It Works

1. **Queue material** - Add files to `docs/night-school/queue/` or tell me what to read
2. **Nightly run** - I read and distill while you sleep
3. **Daytime access** - Reference playbooks in `docs/night-school/[topic]/`

## Topics

- `ai-ml/` - AI, machine learning, agent architectures
- `business/` - Business strategy, ops, growth
- `creative/` - Creative writing, storytelling
  - `ai-creative-writing/` - AI tools for fiction & narrative (NEW)
