# AI SEO Services Playbook

- **Automation:** High - most tasks can be automated

### 1. Content SEO

- Keyword research
- Content strategy

### 2. Technical SEO
- Site audits
- Performance optimization
- Schema markup
- **Tools:** Screaming Frog, PageSpeed, GTmetrix

### 3. Local SEO
- Google Business optimization
- Local citations
- Review management
- **Tools:** BrightLocal, Whitespark

### 4. Link Building
- Outreach automation
- Guest posting
- Digital PR
- **Tools:** PitchBox, Hunter, BuzzSumo
