# Abyssal Grasp v2 - NEWBIE EDITION
## T1 Budget Cruiser Doctrine for New Kybernauts

For pilots with low skills and tight wallets. All fits use Tech 1 or meta modules. Costs under 8M ISK per ship.

---

## For the Newest Player: Vexor "Grappler (T1)"

The Vexor is the BEST ship for a new player in this doctrine.

**Why:**
- **Drones auto-attack.** You don't need to aim or track. Launch them, set to aggressive, they find targets.
- **Buffer tank needs no cap skills.** Plates and resists are passive. No Shield Booster or Armor Repairer management.
- **Neuts are fire-and-forget.** Turn them on, they drain enemy cap until target dies.
- **Forgiving.** Even if you forget to turn on your hardener, the plates give you 10k+ EHP.
- **Cheaper.** T1 guns, T1 drones, meta everything.

**Stats (with low skills ~Gallente Cruiser III, Drones III, Gunnery III):**
- **DPS:** ~280 (drones ~120 + blasters ~160)
- **Tank:** ~11k EHP armor
- **Speed:** ~380 m/s with AB
- **Neuts:** 2x Small Neut I = ~60 GJ/s drain
- **Web:** 55% speed reduction
- **Cost:** ~6.5M ISK

**What to do in fleet:**
1. Follow the Moa (anchor ship).
2. When Moa calls primary, lock that Kikimora.
3. Turn on both neuts. Turn on AB.
4. Get within 8km. Click Scram. Click Web.
5. Launch Warrior drones (click "engage target").
6. Turn on blasters. Overheat if you want (right-click, Overheat).
7. Wait for next primary. Repeat.

**Skill requirements (minimum):**
- Gallente Cruiser III (to sit in ship)
- Drones III (for 3 drones, or IV for 4)
- Light Drone Operation I
- Hybrid Weaponry III
- Medium Hybrid Turret I (for blasters)
- Energy Grid Upgrades II (for neuts)
- Hull Upgrades III (for plates)
- Mechanics III (for DC)

**Training time:** ~3-5 days from brand-new character.

## Alternative: Thorax "Fist (T1)"

For newbies who want raw gun DPS and don't mind more piloting.

**Why:**
- **Highest cruiser DPS in the game.** 5 blasters = lots of damage.
- **Simple concept:** Get close, scram, web, shoot.
- **No drones to manage.** Just guns.

**Why NOT for total newbies:**
- Blasters need tracking. You must get close and stay close (500m).
- If you orbit at 5km instead of 500m, you miss and do zero damage.
- Requires more manual piloting than Vexor.

## Support for Newbies: Celestis "Blindside (T1)"

For newbies who want to help without needing to shoot well.

**Why:**
- **EWAR is easy.** Click dampener on target. Scripts are automatic.
- **No gun skills needed.** Missiles don't miss.
- **Damps ALWAYS work.** Unlike ECM, there's no RNG. Target's lock range drops.
- **Lowest skill requirements of any cruiser.**

- **Speed:** ~350 m/s with AB

## Tackle for Newbies: Atron "Snare (T1)"

For brand-new characters who can't fly cruisers yet.

**Why:**
- **Can fly on day 1.** T1 frigate, minimal skills.
- **AB = scram-proof.** If they scram you, AB keeps running.
- **Web + scram holds targets.** Essential for the fleet.
- **Frigate sig = hard to hit.** Kikimora disintegrators struggle with 35m sig.

## Training Path for Newbies

**Week 1 (Day 1-7):**
- Gallente Frigate III → Fly Atron tackle
- Drones III → Fly Vexor with 3 drones
- Hull Upgrades III, Mechanics III → Tank modules

**Week 2 (Day 8-14):**
- Gallente Cruiser III → Fly Vexor/Thorax/Celestis
- Light Drone Operation III → Better drone DPS
- Medium Hybrid Turret III → T1 blasters on Thorax
- Energy Grid Upgrades II → Neuts on Vexor

**Week 3-4 (Day 15-28):**
- Gallente Cruiser IV → Ship bonuses improve
- Drones V → 5 drones on Vexor
- Medium Hybrid Turret V → T2 blasters
- Drone Interfacing IV → +20% drone DPS

## Key Rules for Newbies

1. **Follow the Moa.** Do not wander off alone.
2. **Scram + web FIRST, shoot SECOND.** A dead target that warped away = 0 ISK.
3. **Focus fire.** Only shoot what the FC calls. 8 ships shooting 1 target = dead in seconds.
4. **Overheat your guns.** Right-click, "Overheat" when target is webbed and close. +15% DPS for 30 seconds.
5. **If you're dying, overheat hardener.** Right-click, "Overheat" on your Multispectrum Coating. +15% resists.
6. **Don't be afraid to die.** T1 cruiser costs 6M ISK. One killmail = 20M+ ISK. You profit even if you die.
7. **Ask questions.** Kybernauts help each other. Fleet comms are for coordination, not silence.
