# The Bind v2 — Anti-Kikimora Brawl Doctrine
## Corrected for Actual Baba Yagas Composition

**Classification:** Internal Doctrine — Alpha Force
**Threat:** Baba Yagas (INIT) Kikimora Packs
**Actual Comp (Otela BR, 2026-05-27):**
- ~13 Kikimoras
- 2 Deacons (armor frig logi)
- Bifrosts (boosh support)
- Dictors (bubble traps)

**Core Principle:** Exploit Respool + Alpha Through Deacon Reps

---

## What I Got Wrong (Lessons Applied)

I assumed Scimitars. The actual BR shows:
- **Deacons**, not Scimitars — armor frig logi, much harder to ECM
- **Bifrosts** — can boosh their fleet or separate ours
- **Dictors** — bubble traps, especially dangerous on land

This changes everything. ECM is not the primary solution. Alpha through reps is.

---

## Deacon Math

- Deacon rep: ~200-250 HP/s each (armor, range ~7km)
- 2 Deacons = ~400-500 HP/s total rep on one target
- Deacon buffer: ~3-4k EHP (frigate hull)

**Key insight:** Deacons are fragile. If we kill a Deacon first, their entire rep chain collapses.

---

## The Kiki Problem (Confirmed)

From the actual BR:
- 13 Kikis = massive spool DPS if left unchecked
- Kikis have no scrams (standard Baba Yagas fit)
- Kikis rely on webs + spool to delete targets
- Every target swap = 90-second respool

## Engagement Sequence: The Bind v2

### Phase 1: The Grab (0:00-0:15)
1. **Stilettos land first** — identify dictors, call targets
2. **Main fleet lands** — SFIs immediately spread scrams across Kikis
3. **Wolves burn to Deacons** — scram + web, begin alpha
4. **Kill dictors first** (if Stilettos haven't already)

### Phase 2: The Kill (0:15-0:45)
1. **Wolves alpha Deacons** — 2 volleys, Deacons die
2. **SFIs spread fire across Kikis** — force respool
3. **FC calls primary** — all SFIs focus one Kiki
4. **With no Deacon reps, Kiki dies in 2-3 volleys**

### Phase 3: The Grind (0:45+)
1. Continue target swaps every 15-20 seconds
2. Wolves clean up any remaining support
3. Kikis die one by one
4. **If Bifrost booshes:** reform at range, re-engage

### Phase 4: The Break (If Needed)
1. If losses exceed 3 SFIs: "break"
2. **MWD away** — they have no scrams, can't hold us
3. Reform, reship, re-engage

## Key Mechanics

### Why This Works Now
| Mechanic | Before (Scimitars) | After (Deacons) |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Logi | 5-6 Scimitars (ECM target) | 2 Deacons (alpha target) |
| Logi fragility | Tanky cruisers | Paper frigates |
| Approach | Jam logi, alpha Kikis | Alpha logi, grind Kikis |
| Dictors | Not considered | Primary threat (bubbles) |

### The Deacon Problem
- 2 Deacons = 400-500 HP/s
- 8 SFIs x ~2,200 alpha = ~17,600 volley
- Deacon buffer = ~3,500 HP
- **Math: Deacon dies in 1 volley from the fleet, or 2 from Wolves alone**

### The Dictor Threat
- Dictor bubble = we're trapped
- But: **Kikis are trapped with us**
- If bubbled: fight. Our buffer > their buffer. Our alpha > their unspooled DPS.
- **Kill dictors first prevents the problem**

### The Bifrost Threat
- Bifrost boosh = separates their fleet or saves them
- Can't really counter this with our comp
- Accept it: if they boosh, we won the ISK trade for that engagement
- Reform and re-engage

## Counter-Counter

**If they bring more Deacons (3-4):**
- Bring more Wolves (4-5)
- Still alpha them — Deacons are fragile

**If they bring Scythes instead of Deacons:**
- Scythes are shield cruisers, tankier
- Switch back to ECM plan (Blackbirds)
- Adapt on the fly

**If they blob (20+ Kikis):**
- Disengage
- Reform with more ships
- This doctrine works against packs, not blobs

**If they bring their own fast tackle:**
- Stilettos kill it
- Or: ignore it, kill Deacons, then kill it
