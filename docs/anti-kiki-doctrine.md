# Anti-Kikimora Counter-Doctrine
## Alpha Force Tactical Briefing

**Classification:** Internal Doctrine — Alpha Force
**Threat:** INIT (Initiative) Kikimora + 5-6x Scimitar Logi Packs
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-01

---

## The Problem (Splatterbug's Intel)

INIT Kikimora packs operate with specific mechanics that pure stat-block analysis misses:

1. **Respool Penalty** — Kikimora disintegrators lose all spool on target swap. They must rebuild damage ramp from zero each time.
2. **Heavy Logi Presence** — 5-6 Scimitars means raw alpha is the only way to break their tank. Sustained DPS gets out-repped.
3. **No Scrams** — Most Kiki doctrines don't fit scrams. They rely on webs and disintegrator application, not hard tackle.
4. **Range Control** — Kikis pull to optimal and out-apply. If you let them dictate range, you die.
5. **Flight Dynamics** — This is not a spreadsheet at 0km. It's a 3D kiting problem with traverse angles, falloff curves, and timing windows.

**What Doesn't Work:** Brawling them head-on, relying on sustained DPS races, expecting webs to matter when they don't fit scrams.

---

## Core Principle: Attrition Through Range Control

> "Make them run into a further attrition opportunity."
> — Splatterbug

The goal is NOT to hold the field in a set-piece brawl. The goal is to force the Kiki fleet into a series of unfavorable micro-engagements where their spool mechanic works against them and our speed/falloff works for us.

## Engagement Sequence

### Phase 1: The Bait (0:00-0:30)
1. **Stilettos land on grid** — identify Kiki fleet composition, count Scimitars
2. **Call primary** — FC picks a Kiki on the edge of their blob, NOT the center
3. **Stiletto gets scram** — forces that Kiki to burn or fight
4. **Ruptures land 30km off** — MWD on, approach to 18km falloff range

### Phase 2: The Pin (0:30-1:00)
1. **Blackbirds start cycling jams** on Scimitars immediately
2. **Ruptures spread points** — 2-3 Ruptures per Kiki on different targets
3. **First volley fires** — ~20,000 alpha lands on a Kiki with no logi
4. **Kiki dies or burns** — if it burns, Ruptures tail with point + falloff fire

### Phase 3: The Attrition (1:00-2:00)
1. **Kikis must choose:**
   - Stay and fight → they have to respool on new targets as we spread points
   - Burn away → Ruptures chase at 1,400 m/s with falloff fire
   - Warp out → if we have points, they can't
2. **FC calls target swaps** — every 15-20 seconds, switch primary to a new Kiki
   - This forces respool on THEIR side, not ours
3. **Blackbirds keep Scimitars jammed** — if jams break, call for secondary jam

### Phase 4: The Break (2:00+)
1. **If Kikis are breaking:**
   - Ruptures tail runners with points
   - Stilettos follow warps to next beacon
   - **Re-engage before they regroup**
2. **If we're losing Ruptures:**
   - FC calls "break break break"
   - Ruptures MWD away, keep falloff fire on pursuing Kikis
   - Stilettos hold points as long as possible, then bail
   - **Live to re-engage** — this is attrition, not a duel

## Why This Works (Mechanics Breakdown)

| Mechanic | Kiki Fleet | Reaver Fleet |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Spool** | Resets on every target swap | N/A (projectiles have no spool) |
| **Range** | Wants 0-20km brawl | Fights at 15-20km falloff |
| **Speed** | Slow (~900 m/s) | Fast (~1,400 m/s) |
| **Logi dependency** | 5-6 Scimitars = massive logi | 0 logi = self-sufficient |
| **Alpha vs Sustained** | Sustained DPS when spooled | Pure alpha volleys |
| **Tackle** | No scrams (mostly) | Scrams + long points |
| **Apply when chasing** | Poor (disintegrator tracking) | Good (falloff into rear) |

**The asymmetry:** We win by NOT giving them the fight they want. We win by making them chase us, swap targets, and re-spool while our projectiles just keep firing.

## Critical FC Callouts

1. **"Spread points"** — Don't dogpile one Kiki. Force them to split spool.
2. **"Jam logi"** — First words out of FC's mouth after primary call.
3. **"Tail the runner"** — When a Kiki burns, nearest Rupture chases with point.
4. **"Break break break"** — Disengage before we lose critical mass. We can re-engage.
5. **"Re-engage"** — The Kikis that ran are now out of position. Hunt them.

## Counter-Counter: What If They Adapt?

**If they bring Keres (damped tackle):**
- Stilettos die first. Acceptable loss.
- Ruptures need to be more cautious on approach — use bookmarks, not direct warps.

**If they bring their own ECM:**
- Blackbirds become primary. Acceptable — every jam on us is a jam not on our DPS.
- Ruptures are cheap. Blackbirds are cheap. Trade efficiently.

**If they swap to long-range ammo (Tetryon):**
- Tetryon has worse tracking. Our traverse matters MORE.
- Stay at 18km, their tracking is worse than Mystic.

**If they bring a Huggin/Rapier (webs):**
- This is the real threat. If they web our Ruptures, we lose the speed advantage.
- Primary the web ship immediately. It's now higher priority than Kikis.

## Splatterbug's Rupture Plan (Referenced)

Splatterbug's original concept: **"Inversion order jam and long point spread"**

- Inversion order jam = Blackbirds jam in reverse priority (jam the Kikis closest to death, forcing logi to choose)
- Long point spread = Ruptures with Federation Navy Warp Disruptors (24km point range) instead of scrams
- This lets Ruptures point from further out, safer from Kiki optimal
- **Trade-off:** Scram shuts down MWD, Disruptor doesn't. But against Kikis with no scrams, the longer point range is more valuable.

**Variant fit (Long Point):**
Replace scram with Federation Navy Warp Disruptor (24km range) on half the Ruptures.
- Scram Ruptures (4-5): Hold tackle on committed targets
- Long Point Ruptures (4-5): Point from 20km, never enter Kiki optimal

## Key Takeaways

1. **Don't brawl.** Kikis win brawls. We win kiting.
2. **Alpha > Sustained.** Volley fire, not DPS races.
3. **Jam logi or die.** 5-6 Scimitars will out-rep anything if not jammed.
4. **Speed is tank.** 1,400 m/s Ruptures don't need buffer if they can't be hit.
5. **Attrition, not annihilation.** Break, re-engage, break again. Make them bleed ISK.
