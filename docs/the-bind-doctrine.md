# The Bind — Anti-Kikimora Brawl Doctrine
## Nova's Mechanics-Driven Analysis

**Classification:** Internal Doctrine — Alpha Force
**Threat:** INIT Kikimora Packs with 5-6x Scimitar Support
**Core Principle:** Exploit Their Mechanics, Don't Ignore Them

---

## What I Learned (Splatterbug's Mechanics)

1. **Respool Is The Clock** — Every target swap costs them 90 seconds. A Kiki that never stays on one target for 90 seconds never deals more than 200-300 DPS.
2. **Logi Is The Real Fleet** — 5-6 Scimitars isn't support, it's the foundation. No logi = dead Kikis. Jammed logi = dead Kikis.
3. **No Scrams = No Control** — They can't hold you. They can't stop you from leaving. They can't prevent range control. This is their biggest weakness.
4. **Speed Gap** — Kikis are slow (~900 m/s). Fast ships dictate the engagement entirely.
5. **Chasing Is Their Nightmare** — Disintegrators track poorly when chasing. Falloff projectiles apply beautifully into a running MWD rear.

The lesson: **Don't avoid the brawl. Make them brawl badly.**

## The Kiki's Fatal Design

Kikimoras are a one-turning solution: land, web, spool, delete.

But they're fragile:
- Destroyer hull (~8-10k EHP)
- No scrams (most doctrines)
- No escape if held
- All damage is front-loaded over time (spool)
- Cap-dependent (MWD + guns)

Against a doctrine designed around their actual weaknesses, they break.

## Doctrine: "The Bind"

### Core Mechanic: The Respool Trap

**Concept:** Every Kiki we tackle forces them into a lose-lose choice.

- Our fleet lands. Every ship immediately tackles a **different** Kiki.
- 8 Kikis = 8 scrams = every Kiki is held.
- Every Kiki that shoots its tackler **loses all spool** on the swap.
- Meanwhile our DPS focuses one Kiki and deletes it.

They either:
- Swap to shoot back → respool → do 200 DPS → die anyway
- Stay on their primary → our tackler tanks the unspooled fire → our DPS kills them
- Burn away → can't, we have scrams

**This is the bind.** There is no correct answer for them.

## Engagement Sequence: The Bind

### Phase 1: The Grab (0:00-0:15)
1. Fleet lands on grid
2. **Every SFI immediately locks and scrams a different Kiki**
3. Blackbirds begin cycling jams on Scimitars
4. Hyenas close to web range
5. **8 Kikis = 8 scrams. They are all held.**

### Phase 2: The Lock (0:15-0:45)
1. FC calls primary Kiki (usually edge target or lowest buffer)
2. All SFIs fire on primary
3. **Each Kiki faces a choice:**
   - Shoot its tackler → swap target → **resets spool to zero** → does 200 DPS → still dies to our focus fire
   - Stay on its original target → our tackler is fine (unspooled fire is weak) → primary dies
   - Try to burn → scrammed, can't
4. Hyena webs primary to prevent it from pulling range even with MWD

### Phase 3: The Kill (0:45-1:15)
1. With Scimitars jammed, primary Kiki dies in 2-3 volleys (~17,600 alpha from 8 SFIs)
2. **Scram on dead Kiki is released**
3. That SFI immediately re-locks and scrams next Kiki
4. **The bind never breaks**

### Phase 4: The Grind (1:15+)
1. Kikis are dying one by one while their DPS is constantly resetting
2. Any Kiki that swaps to fight back loses spool
3. Any Kiki that doesn't swap dies to focus fire
4. Scimitars are either jammed or overwhelmed by the sheer number of separate damage events
5. **Continue until they break or we break**

### Phase 5: The Break (If Needed)
1. If losses exceed 3 SFIs, FC calls "break"
2. **Since they have no scrams, we just MWD away**
3. They can't stop us. We dictate the disengage.
4. Reform, reship if needed, re-engage.

## Why This Works (The Mechanics)

### The Respool Lock
| Kiki Action | Result |
|-------------|--------|
| Swap to shoot tackler | Spool resets. 90 seconds of ramp lost. Tackler tanks 200 DPS easily. |
| Stay on original target | Tackler is untouched. Our DPS kills primary. |
| Try to burn | Scrammed. Can't. |
| Get jammed | No logi. Dies to alpha. |

**Every choice is wrong.** That's the bind.

### The Scimitar Problem
- 5-6 Scimitars can rep ~8,000-12,000 HP/s combined
- But they're repping **separate damage events** across multiple Kikis
- Meanwhile Blackbirds are jamming 3-4 of them
- The remaining 2-3 Scimitars can't keep up with 8 SFIs volleying one target

### The Speed Gap
- SFI: ~1,800 m/s
- Kiki: ~900 m/s
- We close the distance before they can pull to optimal
- If we need to leave, we just leave. They have no scrams.

### No Scrams = No Control
This is the Kiki's biggest weakness that most analyses miss. Without scrams:
- They can't hold tackle
- They can't prevent kiting
- They can't stop disengages
- **They can't dictate the fight**

We can choose to brawl (scram them) or leave (MWD away) at any time. They can only accept what we give them.

## Counter-Counter: Adaptations

**If they bring Keres (damped tackle):**
- Keres is fragile. One volley deletes it.
- If Keres points an SFI, that SFI MWDs away. Keres can't keep up.

**If they bring their own ECM:**
- Falcon/Kitsune jamming our SFIs = Falcon/Kitsune not jamming our Blackbirds
- SFI buffer can survive 1-2 volleys while jammed. Blackbirds can't.
- Trade: let them jam DPS, keep our jams on their logi.

**If they switch to Baryon (close range ammo):**
- Baryon optimal is ~8-12km. If we're at 500m with scrams, Baryon is fine.
- But Baryon has worse tracking at close range. Orbit tight (500m) and their tracking suffers.
- Meanwhile our autos don't care about range.

**If they bring Rapier/Huggin (webs):**
- **This is the real counter.** A Rapier webbing our SFIs from 30km kills our speed advantage.
- **Solution:** Hyena priority target. Web the Rapier. Primary it. Kill it first.
- SFI with web is still faster than Kiki without. But it's closer. Be aware.

**If they blob (12+ Kikis):**
- The Bind scales poorly past 10 Kikis. Too many targets, not enough tacklers.
- Disengage. Reform with more ships or wait for them to split.
- We're not here to hero-fight blobs. We're here to grind packs.
