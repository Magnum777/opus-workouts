# The Bind v3 — Anti-Kikimora Doctrine
## Corrected for Actual BR (Otela, 2026-05-27)

**Classification:** Internal Doctrine — Alpha Force
**Threat:** Baba Yagas Kikimora Packs
**Actual BR Result:** They lost 1 Deacon (18M). We lost 3 Dictors + 1 T3C + pods (808M).
**Lesson:** What we fielded was wrong.

---

## What Actually Happened (BR Analysis)

### Their Comp (36 involved, 1 loss):
- **~13 Kikimoras** — all survived
- **2 Deacons** — 1 died, 1 survived
- **Dictors** — bubbled, held us
- **Bifrosts** — boosh support

### Our Comp (6 involved, 8 losses):
- **3 Interdictors** — died immediately (primaried, no buffer)
- **1 Strategic Cruiser** — died (primaried, expensive, slow)
- **4 Capsules** — pods

### Why We Lost:
1. **Dictors are paper** — 2 volleys from a Kiki and they pop
2. **No actual DPS** — T3C isn't DPS, it's a utility brick
3. **No buffer** — our ships had no tank
4. **They held the field** — bubbles trapped us, Kikis spooled freely

---

## The Real Problem

The BR shows our approach was fundamentally wrong. We brought:
- **Dictors** (thinking we'd bubble them) → they primaried and killed them first
- **T3C** (thinking links/command) → big shiny target, died to spool

They brought:
- **13 Kikis + 2 Deacons** — actual DPS + actual reps
- **Dictors** — to hold us in place
- **Bifrosts** — to separate or save

We brought utility. They brought DPS. **DPS won.**

---

## Doctrine v3: "The Bind"

### Core Principle: Match Their DPS, Exploit Their Fragility

Kikis are fragile destroyers. Deacons are fragile frigates. 
We need ships that:
1. **Can tank** an unspooled Kiki
2. **Can alpha** a Deacon in 1-2 volleys
3. **Can spread tackle** across multiple Kikis
4. **Can survive** long enough for our alpha to work

## Engagement Sequence: The Bind

### Phase 1: The Alpha (0:00-0:10)
1. **Jaguars burn to Deacons immediately**
2. **Stilettos lock dictors**
3. **SFIs land, spread scrams across Kikis**
4. Jaguars alpha Deacons — they die in 1 volley

### Phase 2: The Lock (0:10-0:30)
1. **Deacons are dead**
2. SFIs have scrams on multiple Kikis
3. FC calls primary Kiki
4. All SFIs fire on primary
5. No Deacon reps = Kiki dies in 2-3 volleys

### Phase 3: The Grind (0:30-1:30)
1. Continue target swaps
2. Every Kiki swap = respool for them
3. Our autos keep firing (no spool)
4. Stilettos clean up dictors

### Phase 4: The Break (If Needed)
1. If we're losing SFIs: FC calls "break"
2. SFIs MWD away — Kikis have no scrams, can't hold us
3. Reform, re-engage with surviving ships

- 2 Deacons = 400-500 HP/s rep
- But: Deacon buffer = ~3,500 EHP
- 3 Jaguars x ~1,200 alpha = ~3,600 volley
- **Deacon dies before reps matter**

This is the key. Don't try to out-DPS the reps. Kill the repper.

- Dictors will bubble. Accept it.
- Our SFIs have 22-25k EHP. We can tank in the bubble.
- Kill dictors with Stilettos while SFIs fight Kikis.
- Once dictors are dead, we can MWD out if needed.
