Proving Grounds

Skills Development Committee Formation Document

Purpose

The Skills Development Committee exists to forge Kybernauts.

Our role is not simply to onboard pilots, distribute ships, or assign skill plans. Our purpose is transformation. We take capsuleers from wherever they are in their journey and help them become pilots who understand what it means to live and thrive in Pochven.

Being a Kybernaut is not defined by the ship you fly. It is defined by how you approach New Eden: curiosity, aggression, cooperation, and the will to learn through action.

The Proving Grounds program is the structure through which this transformation happens. Every pilot who enters our space gains the knowledge, confidence, and capability to participate in our fleets, contribute to our community, and carve their own story in Pochven. We do not build hangar queens.
We build pilots who undock. Whether someone arrives as a brand new capsuleer or a seasoned veteran discovering Pochven for the first time, the goal remains the same: to help them become Kybernauts. Pilots who understand the battlefield, support their fleetmates, and grow stronger through shared experience.

The SDC provides the tools, structure, and culture that make this possible. Through guidance, doctrine, and active fleets, we cultivate pilots who can adapt, earn, fight, and ultimately help build the next generation that follows behind them.

Because Kybernauts are not recruited.
They are made.

Guiding Principles

All Members Are Equal in Growth Whether in their first ship or commanding capitals, every pilot deserves the tools to succeed.
No One Is Left Behind Newbros and early-career capsuleers require hands-on mentorship. We do not merely inject skills; we cultivate understanding.
Activity Is Expected We reward those who engage. Passivity has no place in our growth.
Combat Is the Crucible True learning happens in fleet. We provide doctrines that build combat readiness.

SDC Responsibilities

1. Corp Doctrine Fittings

  • Maintain purpose-built ship fittings for isk generation, PVP and standings
  • Fits must align with alliance doctrines where applicable

2. Income/PVP Doctrines (3 Total)

  • Develop three doctrines OUTSIDE of alliance doctrines
  • Focus: ISK generation + combat capability
  • Must remain accessible to developing pilots
  • Examples: T1 cruiser Logistics, T1 frigate hunting, destroyer fleets

3. New Member Packages

Both Early Career and Newbro members receive the same package:

  • Skill books
  • Basic fittings
  • Ship handouts

Distribution Point: Kino VII - Moon 17 - The Convocation of Triglav Bioadaptation Chambers

4. Communication Channels

  • Early career and newbros are initially closed off from most alliance channels
  • They will be integrated into the alliance when they are ready
  • This controlled environment allows focused learning without distraction

5. Activity Tracking

  • Automated processes track pilot progress through the program

Standings Path

All new members must complete the following:

  1. Exit Ohnivak — Process to transfer from Ohnivak corp into KYBERNAUTS
  2. Triglavian Standings — achieve 3.4 standing with Triglavian Unity
  3. SDC Orientation — Meet with committee within first 48 hours of arrival
  4. Mentorship Pairing — Bond with experienced pilot for ongoing guidance

Operational Qualification

To be considered after submitting a Kyber application, a pilot must prove initiative and competence:

  • 3 solo kills in Pochven
  • Lead a fleet (at least one) after submitting their application

These requirements ensure we build pilots who can fight, learn, and lead — not merely exist in space.
