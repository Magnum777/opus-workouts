# Raven [RRR] — Alpha Clone 1M SP Training Plan
## Minimum Viable → Optimal Progression

**Fit Role:** Remote Rep Raven — Fleet logistics with torpedo DPS backup
**Ship:** Raven (Caldari Battleship)
**SP Budget:** 1,000,000 free SP (on top of ~400k starter skills)

---

## The Problem

This fit demands a lot:
- T2 shield tank (Shield Upgrades IV)
- T2 damage mods (Weapon Upgrades IV, Hull Upgrades IV)
- T2 missile support (Missile Bombardment IV, Missile Projection IV)
- T2 rigs (Shield Rigging IV)
- Battleship skill chain (Frigate → Destroyer → Cruiser → Battleship)
- Fitting skills (Power Grid V, CPU Management V)
- Drones V (for 5x Hammerheads)

**Reality:** 1M SP won't max everything. Prioritize what matters most.

**Inject in this order:**

1. **Caldari Battleship III** + all prereqs (Frigate/Destroyer/Cruiser III)
2. **Shield Upgrades IV** + prereqs (Shield Operation/Management III)
3. **Hull Upgrades IV** + prereqs (Mechanic III)
4. **Power Grid Management V** + CPU Management IV

## Fit Compromises for 1M SP

If you can't fit everything T2 immediately, swap these (cheapest to most expensive):

| Slot | T2 Module | Meta Alternative | Skill Saved |
|------|-----------|------------------|-------------|
| Rigs | T2 Shield Rigs | T1 Shield Rigs | ~150k SP (Shield Rigging IV) |
| Low | Guidance Enhancer II | Guidance Enhancer I / F-90 | ~100k SP (Bombardment/Projection IV) |
| Drones | 5x Hammerhead | 4x Hammerhead | ~100k SP (Drones V) |

**Recommended compromise:** Use T1 rigs and meta Guidance Enhancers. Keep T2 tank, T2 BCUs, T2 DC. Upgrade later.

## Summary

**Inject first:** Battleship chain → Tank skills → Fitting skills → Torpedoes
**Compromise on:** Rigs (T1), Guidance Enhancers (meta), Drone count (4)
**Keep T2:** Extenders, hardeners, DC, BCUs, launchers, MWD
**Train after:** Drones V, T2 rigs, T2 Guidance Enhancers, Battleship IV

With smart allocation, you'll be in the Raven and effective within hours. Fully T2 takes a few weeks of natural training after the injection.
