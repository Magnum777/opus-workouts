## Why Kybernauts?

### Who We Are

Kybernauts Clade is a Pro Triglavian alliance whose rich history stems from before the creation of Pochven to today. We're one of the biggest surviving non-bloc affiliated active alliances in Pochven.

We home out of **Kino VII - Moon 17 - The Convocation of Triglav Bioadaptation Chambers** in Perun Krai.

- **No Capitals** — Pure subcap warfare
- **No Cynos** — No cyno alts needed
- **Red Skybox** — Reflects the murderous rage in our hearts
- **Best PI in EVE** — 0% POCO taxes
