# PROVING GROUNDS
## Skills Development Committee Formation Document

---

**PURPOSE**

The Skills Development Committee exists to forge Kybernauts.

Our role is not simply onboarding, ship contracts, or skill plans. Our purpose is transformation. We take capsuleers from wherever they are in their journey and help them become pilots who understand what it means to live and thrive in Pochven.

Being a Kybernaut is not defined by the ship you fly. It is defined by how you approach New Eden: curiosity, aggression, cooperation, and the will to learn through action.

> We do not build hangar queens.
> We build pilots who undock.

Because Kybernauts are not recruited.
They are made.

---

**GUIDING PRINCIPLES**

**It's Not What You Fly** — Ships are tools. Skills are tools. Doctrine is guidance. What matters is whether a pilot understands why they are flying what they are flying — and can execute under pressure.

**All Members Are Equal in Growth** — Skillpoints do not define value. A newbro and a veteran both deserve the tools to succeed.

**No One Is Left Behind** — We do not merely inject skills. We cultivate understanding — explanation, demonstration, repetition.

**Activity Is Expected** — This is not a passive program. Growth comes from participation. Undock. Ask. Fly. Improve.

**Combat Is the Crucible** — True learning happens in fleet. We teach through action: doctrine discipline, positioning, and execution.

**SDC RESPONSIBILITIES**

**1. Corp Doctrine Fittings**
- Maintain purpose-built ship fittings for isk generation, PVP and standings
- Fits must align with alliance doctrines where applicable

**2. Income / PVP Doctrines (3 Total)**
- Develop three doctrines outside alliance doctrines
- Focus: ISK generation + combat capability
- Must remain accessible to developing pilots
- Examples: T1 cruiser logistics, T1 frigate hunting, destroyer fleets

**3. New Member Packages**
- Early Career and Newbros receive the same package: skill books, basic fittings, ship handouts
- Distribution Point: Kino VII — Moon 17 — The Convocation of Triglav Bioadaptation Chambers

**4. Communication Channels**
- Early Career and Newbros are initially closed off from most alliance channels
- They are integrated into the alliance when ready
- Controlled environment enables focused learning without distraction

**5. Activity Tracking**
- Automated processes track pilot progress through the program

---

**STANDINGS & KYBER PATH**

All new members must complete the following:

1. **Exit Ohnivak** — Transfer into KYBERNAUTS
2. **Triglavian Standings** — Achieve 3.4 standing with Triglavian Unity
3. **SDC Orientation** — Meet within 48 hours of arrival
4. **Mentorship Pairing** — Bond with experienced pilot for guidance

**Operational Qualification**

To be considered after submitting a Kyber application, a pilot must prove initiative and competence:

- **3 solo kills** in Pochven
- **Lead a fleet** (at least one) after submitting their application

These gates ensure we build pilots who can fight, learn, and lead — not merely exist in space.
