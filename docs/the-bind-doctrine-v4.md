## Engagement: The Two-Step

### Step 1: Alpha Deacons (0:00-0:15)
1. Fleet lands
2. **All SFIs lock Deacons**
3. **4 SFIs scram Deacons** (2 per Deacon)
4. **All SFIs fire on Deacons**
5. **Blackbirds jam Kikis** (prevent them from shooting back)
6. Deacons die in 1 volley

### Step 2: Grind Kikis (0:15+)
1. Deacons are dead — no reps
2. **SFIs spread scrams across Kikis**
3. FC calls primary
4. 8 SFIs alpha one Kiki → dies in 1-2 volleys
5. Target swap → repeat
6. **Blackbirds continue jamming** — either Kikis (resets spool) or any remaining support

### Break (If Needed)
1. FC calls "break"
2. SFIs MWD away
3. Kikis have no scrams → can't hold us
4. Blackbirds warp to ping
5. Reform, re-engage

## The Deacon Kill (The Whole Fight)

This is the only thing that matters:

1. **Can we kill Deacons faster than they rep?**
   - Deacon reps: ~200-250 HP/s
   - Deacon buffer: ~3,500 EHP
   - 4 SFIs alpha: ~8,800 volley
   - **Deacon dies in 1 volley. Reps don't matter.**

2. **Can we survive while killing Deacons?**
   - 8 unspooled Kikis = ~1,600-2,000 DPS total
   - SFI buffer: 22-25k EHP
   - **Survival time: 10-15 seconds**
   - Deacons die in 5 seconds
   - **Math checks out.**

3. **Can they stop us?**
   - No scrams on Kikis → can't hold us
   - Blackbirds jamming → their DPS is erratic
   - We can always break if needed
