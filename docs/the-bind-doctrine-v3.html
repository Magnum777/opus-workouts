Doctrine v3: The Bind

Core Principle: Match Their DPS, Exploit Their Fragility

Kikis are fragile destroyers. Deacons are fragile frigates. We need ships that:

  1. Can tank an unspooled Kiki
  2. Can alpha a Deacon in 1-2 volleys
  3. Can spread tackle across multiple Kikis
  4. Can survive long enough for our alpha to work

Engagement Sequence: The Bind

Phase 1: The Alpha (0:00-0:10)
  1. Jaguars burn to Deacons immediately
  2. Stilettos lock dictors
  3. SFIs land, spread scrams across Kikis
  4. Jaguars alpha Deacons — they die in 1 volley
Phase 2: The Lock (0:10-0:30)
  1. Deacons are dead
  2. SFIs have scrams on multiple Kikis
  3. FC calls primary Kiki
  4. All SFIs fire on primary
  5. No Deacon reps = Kiki dies in 2-3 volleys
Phase 3: The Grind (0:30-1:30)
  1. Continue target swaps every 15-20 seconds
  2. Every Kiki swap = respool for them
  3. Our autos keep firing (no spool)
  4. Stilettos clean up dictors
Phase 4: The Break (If Needed)
  1. If we're losing SFIs: FC calls "break"
  2. SFIs MWD away — Kikis have no scrams, can't hold us
  3. Reform, re-engage with surviving ships

  • Kill dictors with Stilettos while SFIs fight Kikis.
