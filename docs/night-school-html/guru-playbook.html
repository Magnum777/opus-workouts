Guru Freelance Platform Playbook

Overview

Guru is a freelance talent marketplace with a focus on professional services. Known for low fees compared to competitors and strong workroom collaboration tools.

Platform Model

  • Employers post jobs for free
  • Freelancers submit quotes/proposals
  • Direct hiring or contest-style
  • Workroom for collaboration and file sharing
  • SafePay payment protection

Fees

Freelancer Fees (Service Fee)

  • Based on membership level and agreement type
  • Employer can contribute up to 5% toward job fee
  • Free tier: Limited features, higher fees
  • Paid membership: Lower fees, more features

Membership Tiers (Freelancer)

| Level | Fee | Features |

|-------|-----|----------|

| Basic | Free | Limited proposals, basic features |

| Professional | $12.95/mo | More proposals, priority support |

| Business | $24.95/mo | Enhanced features |

| Enterprise | $49.95/mo | Full features |

Employer Fees

  • 2.9% handling fee per invoice (lowest in industry)
  • Free job postings

SafePay (Escrow)

  • Funds held until work approved
  • Protects both parties
  • Automatic payments for recurring clients

Pros

  • Lowest freelancer fees in industry (can be as low as ~5%)
  • Strong workroom collaboration
  • SafePay escrow protection
  • 24/7 support
  • Employer-funded fees option

Cons

  • Less traffic than Upwork/Fiverr
  • Payment issues reported in some reviews
  • Workroom can be clunky
  • Less brand recognition

Best For

  • Developers (web, mobile, software)
  • Designers
  • Writers
  • Virtual assistants
  • Marketing professionals

Integration with Nova

  • Profile optimization guidance
  • Proposal templates
  • Rate benchmarking
  • Skill categorization
