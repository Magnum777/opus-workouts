AI Newsletter/Subscription Services Playbook

Overview

  • Type: Recurring revenue via email newsletters
  • Model: Subscriptions, sponsorships, affiliate
  • Automation: High - can be largely autonomous

Newsletter Types

1. Curation Newsletters

  • Aggregate content on specific topic
  • Human + AI curation
  • Examples: Morning Brew, TLDR

2. Educational

  • Tutorials, tips, deep dives
  • AI-assisted research
  • Examples: Lenny's Newsletter, Dense Discovery

3. News/Analysis

  • Industry news with AI analysis
  • Market trends
  • Examples: Stratechery, The Generalist

4. Tools/Resources

  • Curated tool lists
  • Deals/discounts
  • Free resources

Monetization

Revenue Streams

| Method | Potential |

|--------|-----------|

| Subscriptions | $5-50/sub/month |

| Sponsorships | $500-10K/issue |

| Affiliate | 10-30% of sales |

| Directory listings | $50-500/listing |

Pricing Strategy

  • Free tier: Ads supported
  • Paid tier: $5-20/month
  • Premium: $50+/month with extra features

Automation

V1 - Human + AI Assist

  • AI drafts newsletter sections
  • Human edits and sends
  • Manual subscriber management

V2 - Semi-Auto

  • Automated content gathering
  • AI generates first draft
  • Scheduled sending

V3 - Full Auto

  • AI curates and writes full issue
  • Automated A/B testing
  • Self-optimizing content

Tech Stack

| Component | Tool |

|----------|------|

| Platform | ConvertKit, Beehiiv, Ghost |

| AI Writing | MiniMax, Claude |

| Automation | Zapier, n8n |

| Analytics | Built-in + GA |

Nova Implementation

Immediate

1. Start free newsletter on niche

2. Build audience via existing channels

3. Add affiliate links

Growth

  • Convert to paid subscription
  • Add sponsorships
  • Create premium tier

Key Metrics

  • Open rate: 20-40% (good)
  • Click rate: 2-5% (good)
  • Sub cost: $1-5 (good)
  • LTV: $20-100

Priority for Nova

Medium - Recurring revenue model, good for email skill development
