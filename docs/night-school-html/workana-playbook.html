Workana Freelance Platform Playbook

Overview

Workana is a Latin America-focused freelance marketplace. Similar to Upwork but with strong presence in Spanish/Portuguese-speaking markets (Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Brazil, Spain).

Platform Model

  • Employer posts project for free
  • Freelancers bid on projects
  • Direct hiring or Workana selects candidates
  • Workspace for collaboration
  • Payment via Workana or direct

Fees

Employer Fees

  • 10% service fee on project value
  • Free to post jobs

Freelancer Fees

  • 20% service fee on earnings

Key Features

  • Free Project Posting: No upfront costs
  • Flexible Payments: Hourly or fixed price
  • Workroom: Collaboration space
  • Skills Tests: Verification badges
  • Latin America Focus: Strong regional presence

Pros

  • Strong in Latin American market
  • Less competition than Upwork/Fiverr
  • Free job postings for employers
  • Good for Spanish/Portuguese freelancers
  • 3-day payment guarantee (claimed)

Cons

  • Mixed reviews on payment reliability
  • Profile approval can take 15-30 days without paid priority
  • Forced "Priority Moderation" paid feature complaints
  • Less international reach
  • Dispute resolution issues reported

Best For

  • Spanish/Portuguese freelancers
  • Remote workers in Latin America
  • Developers, designers, writers
  • Agencies serving Latin American clients

Comparison to Competitors

| Platform | Freelancer Fee | Employer Fee | Focus |
