AI Content Creation Playbook

Overview

  • Type: Autonomous income stream
  • Forms: Blog posts, YouTube videos, TikTok, social media
  • Automation: High - full pipeline can be automated

Income Models

1. Blog/SEO Content

  • AI writes articles on trending topics
  • Monetize via ads (AdSense, Mediavine)
  • Affiliate links within content
  • Tools: WordPress, SurferSEO, Jasper

2. YouTube Automation

  • Faceless channels (AI voice + visuals)
  • Niches: news recaps, tutorials, compilations
  • Monetization: AdSense, sponsorships, affiliate
  • Tools: Pictory, InVideo, ElevenLabs, Capcut

3. TikTok/Shorts

  • Viral AI-generated content
  • Affiliate links in bio
  • Brand sponsorships at scale
  • Tools: Stormy AI, Argil, Capcut

4. Social Media Management

  • Manage accounts for clients
  • Schedule + generate content
  • Tools: Postiz, Buffer, Canva

Nova-Specific Strategy

V1 - Content Engine

  • Monitor trending topics via RSS/API
  • Generate outlines with AI
  • Create drafts for human review
  • Schedule via Postiz

V2 - Full Automation

  • Auto-generate and post content
  • A/B test variations
  • Optimize based on engagement

V3 - Monetization

  • Add affiliate links automatically
  • Track performance per piece
  • Scale winning content types
