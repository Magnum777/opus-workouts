## Description: <br>
Helps users create SEO-driven pages at scale using templates and data, including programmatic SEO, template pages, directory pages, location pages, comparison pages, and integration pages. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing teams, SEO practitioners, and developers use this skill to plan programmatic SEO opportunities, page templates, internal linking, indexation strategy, quality checks, and monitoring for scaled content programs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may proactively suggest programmatic SEO when discussions mention scaled keywords, locations, integrations, personas, or competitor directories. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm that programmatic SEO is appropriate for the user's goal before acting on those suggestions. <br>
Risk: Planning many template-driven pages can create thin, duplicate, or misleading content if the data source is weak or public-only. <br>
Mitigation: Require unique page value, validated search intent, data-source review, pre-launch quality checks, and post-launch monitoring before publishing at scale. <br>
Risk: The skill checks `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` when available, which may contain business context shared with the assistant. <br>
Mitigation: Review that file before use and keep only business context that is appropriate to share in the agent session. <br>
