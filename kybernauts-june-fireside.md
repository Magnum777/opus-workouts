# 🦝 Kybernauts — June Fireside Summary

---

Welcome to everyone who's joined up recently. We've been growing fast the last couple months, and that's largely thanks to our SDC team — Vol, Spider-Bug, Ben, and the rest of the crew. Onboarding's still pretty new, so if something's busted or confusing, ping them. They're iterating on it.

## Structure Campaign

Pochven's been static for years. Now, in a matter of weeks, we're tearing structures down. We're blue with 762 and Verage on this — shared goal, free the triangle. We're pro-destruction, we always have been. This is how you build the small-gang paradise CCP said they wanted.

Frame Perfect disbanded and went their own way. We're handling structure ops directly now, running our own cover tags. Plan's set, allies are coordinated, and we're doing this on our terms.

## Local Conduct

Keep it clean in local. Good fights, no shit-talking — we don't do that, we never have. This is objective-driven, not personal. We've shown that repeatedly. Act like you're representing something, because you are.

## Garrisons

We're rolling out light garrisons in Sequenta and Otonumi — that's the Perimeter border. T1 doctrines and below, ships, ammo, modules. FCs can hand them out during fleet ops, and we're getting corp/alliance projects running to keep them stocked.

Point is: no more losing a fight because someone's jumping five systems back to grab a Dictor.

## SIGs

Three things standing up:

**World Art** — reactivating. SRP-unfriendly ships, come as you are. Ask around for details, more info coming.

**Raider's** — reopening. Deployments, merc contracts, black ops and dreads outside Pochven. We'll need this running as the structure campaign winds down.

**Industry** — brand new, standing up this week. Vouch-only, sensitive access. Industry alts on the side. This doesn't replace our PvP focus, but someone's gotta fund and supply the war machine.

## Fleets & Doctrines

We're on objective: OBS, VPs, shenanigans fleets for standings. Standings matter — home systems, clones, implants. A lot of our PvP comes from PvE content, so don't ignore the pre-pings.

KP's updating doctrines. Drakes and Rage doctrine is coming back. VPs stay on Tengus. Update your fits to current Brain Nodule standards — alliance website is source of truth. Yeah, I know it's annoying. I've got 45 Drakes floating around too. But mission-ready fits matter.

## Comms & Behavior

Be in comms when you're in space. Even if you're mining or running missions — it matters. We've had to kick a couple people recently over toxic behavior. Take the high road. The internet's forever, and our reputation out here is forever. We're chaotic, but we're *level-headed* chaotic.

Fly safe. Burn the structures. Don't be a shithead.
